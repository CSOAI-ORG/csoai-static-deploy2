The Scottish Land Commission (SLC) was established under the Land Reform (Scotland) Act 2016 as the statutory body for land reform. It advises Scottish Ministers on land rights and responsibilities, investigates land ownership patterns, reviews the impact of land law and policy, and promotes public awareness of land governance. Scotland has 11,000+ hectares of vacant and derelict land and one of the most concentrated patterns of land ownership in Europe. This pack provides a sovereign AI governance framework for every SLC statutory function.

📋 12 Entry Points

Statutory guidance under the Land Reform (Scotland) Act 2016 — AI governance for statement implementation monitoring, compliance assessment, and multi-stakeholder evidence synthesis across public bodies, private landowners and communities.

🚫 6 Non-Negotiable Red Lines

  • No AI-sole determination of land ownership or rights. All ownership classifications, title determinations and beneficial-ownership conclusions remain human-made — AI provides analytical support only. Land Registration etc. (Scotland) Act 2012 statutory authority retained by the Keeper of the Registers of Scotland.
  • No automated land valuation for statutory purposes. Land value assessments for taxation, compulsory purchase or development contributions are human-determined — AI models provide data inputs but never the final valuation.
  • No AI-based surveillance of individual landowners or tenants. Ownership pattern analysis is aggregated and anonymised — no individual-level tracking, no surveillance of lawful landownership activity, no profiling of tenant farmers or community groups.
  • No AI-sole compulsory purchase or land-acquisition recommendation. Compulsory purchase orders (CPOs) and land assembly decisions involve human judgment per the Land Reform (Scotland) Act 2016 and Acquisition of Land (Authorisation Procedure) (Scotland) Act 1947.
  • No AI-sole community asset transfer decisions. Community asset transfer and right-to-buy decisions under the Community Empowerment (Scotland) Act 2015 remain human-determined — AI tools assist evidence-gathering but never decide outcomes.
  • No sensitive land-tenure or ownership data leaves UK jurisdiction. All land-ownership, tenancy, and valuation data remains UK-resident — data residency enforced by sovereign-keystore geographic binding per DPA 2018 + UK GDPR.
