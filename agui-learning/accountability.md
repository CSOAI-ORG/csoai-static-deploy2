## 0. The regulatory anchor (read this first)

The ACCOUNTABILITY axis sits on three verbatim EU AI Act obligations *(verified — artificialintelligenceact.eu, machine-translation of Reg (EU) 2024/1689)*:

- **Art 12(1)** — "High-risk AI systems shall technically allow for the **automatic recording of events (logs) over the lifetime of the system**."
- **Art 14(4)(d)** — the human overseer must be able "**to decide, in any particular situation, not to use the high-risk AI system or to otherwise disregard, override or reverse the output** of the high-risk AI system."
- **Art 14(4)(e)** — "to **intervene** in the operation of the high-risk AI system or **interrupt** the system through a **'stop' button** or a similar procedure that allows the system to come to a halt in a safe state."
- **Art 19** — providers/deployers "must keep automatically generated logs … for **at least six months**, or longer if required by EU or national laws" *(verified)*.

Every adoption recommendation below is written to satisfy those four sentences, not a vendor's gloss.

## 2. What CSOAI should adopt — 5 concrete improvements for the AG UI **Accountability chat window**

*(Each maps to a §0 verbatim obligation and to a row above. All assume CSOAI's existing assets: the Ed25519-signed h3k card, the frozen 417-provision bank, the 14-axis GSPC, the regulator-ag-ui signed index.)*

1. **Override log (Art 14(4)(d)) — first-class, structured, signed.**
   Every "disregard / override / reverse" action by the human becomes a structured record — not a chat line — capturing: `decision_id`, `who`, `when`, `original output`, `action taken`, `reason`, `before/after`. Derive an **override-rate metric** (overrides per decision, per session, per hive) the way Oversight Sentinel's category monitors override events. Adopt from row 1/3: point the override at the *specific trajectory step* it reversed (Arize-style annotation on a span), not the final message.

2. **Art 14 predicates rendered literally in the window.**
   Show the five Art 14(4)(a)–(e) capabilities plus Art 12(1) auto-logging and Art 19 six-month retention as **live checkable states** beside each decision: e.g. `override available ✓` · `disregard/reverse ✓` · `stop button ✓` · `auto-logged ✓` · `retention ≥6mo ✓`. Adopt from row 8: quote the Act verbatim; make the predicate a boolean the system asserts, never free text. Adopt from row 5/6: flip each predicate green/red automatically and route a red to a named control/owner.

3. **Signed decision records (Art 12/19) with standards mapping.**
   Every decision + override mints an Ed25519-signed record using the existing h3k card format, carrying a content hash + time anchor (offline-verifiable). Adopt from row 9 (Asqav): add a **conformance-mapping field** so the signature also states *which clause* it satisfies ("EU AI Act Art 12(1) auto-logging; Art 14(4)(d) override; Art 19 ≥6-month retention") — and expose NIST AI RMF / OWASP Agentic cross-refs. Retention flag defaults to **≥6 months** and is surfaced, never hidden.

4. **Per-decision trace + one-click audit export (immutable).**
   Adopt from row 2 (Langfuse) + row 4 (Credo AI): every decision carries a **trace-ID** with a nested span tree (prompt → tool → output → override), stored append-only (SentinelDB-style Parquet/JSONL). Add a **"Export evidence bundle"** button that produces: trace + signed record + override log + provenance, so an auditor gets the whole chain, not a screenshot.

5. **Always-visible stop/override affordance + evidence-at-decision-time.**
   Adopt from row 10 (Vanta/Drata) + row 8: capture evidence **the instant the decision is made** (no retroactive reconstruction), and keep an Art 14(4)(e) **"stop / halt to safe state"** control and an Art 14(4)(d) **"override/ignore"** control permanently visible in the chat window — so human oversight is an affordance the UI guarantees, not a feature the human has to remember exists.
