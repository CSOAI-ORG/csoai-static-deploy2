🧬 Experimental Design — how the harness decides

Wilson 95% CI for the win-rate: k/n ± 1.96·√(p(1-p)/n + 1.96²/(4n²)) / (1 + 1.96²/n) — proper for small n, asymmetric around the point estimate.
McNemar exact test on discordant pairs: p = min(1, 2·Σ Binom(n=k..b+c, p=0.5)) — only counts items where one model is right and the other is wrong. Both-right and both-wrong cancel out.
Verdict logic: requires (a) n ≥ usable_n=30 and (b) McNemar p < 0.05. Otherwise UNMEASURED or NO_SIGNIFICANT_DIFFERENCE.
