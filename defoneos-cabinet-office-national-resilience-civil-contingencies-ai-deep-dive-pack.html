12 Entry Points

Resilience

1. COBR & Crisis Management

Cabinet Office Briefing Room (COBR) coordination, national crisis response, cross-government resilience. Civil Contingencies Secretariat, National Security Council integration, and emergency coordination centre operations across all UK regions.

Planning

2. National Risk Assessment

National Risk Register (NRR) maintenance, risk assessment methodology, community risk register alignment. Pandemic preparedness, flooding, cyber attack, CBRN, and cascading failure scenario modelling with annual refresh cycle.

Strategy

3. National Security Strategy

National Security Strategy refresh, Integrated Review, strategic threat assessment. NSS Council, threat prioritisation, and resource allocation across the 5 national security objectives.

Coordination

4. Civil Contingencies Act

CCA 2004 Category 1 & 2 responder coordination, Local Resilience Forums (LRFs), emergency planning duty. 43 LRFs across England, multi-agency coordination, and Gold/Silver/Bronze command structure.

Cyber

5. National Cyber Security

NCSC operational management, Active Cyber Defence, Cyber Essentials scheme. National Cyber Strategy, critical national infrastructure (CNI) protection, and cyber incident response coordination.

Government

6. Government Digital Service

GOV.UK platform, GOV.UK Pay/Notify/SignIn, digital service standard. Government as a Platform, open standards, and cross-department digital transformation.

Data

7. National Data Strategy

National Data Strategy implementation, data.gov.uk, Government Statistical Service. Data quality frameworks, data sharing agreements, and analytical capability building across departments.

Integrity

8. Propriety & Ethics

Ministerial code enforcement, special advisers code, business appointment rules. Propriety and Ethics team, conflict of interest management, and government transparency.

Devolution

9. Devolution & Intergovernmental Relations

Intergovernmental Relations Review, Joint Ministerial Committee, interministerial group coordination. Sewel Convention monitoring, legislative consent motion tracking, and devolution settlement management.

Honours

10. Honours & Public Appointments

Honours nomination processing, committee assessment, investiture coordination. Public appointments process, Commissioner for Public Appointments oversight, and vetting for honours.

Civil Service

11. Civil Service Reform

Civil Service Reform Plan, capability building, talent management. Fast Stream recruitment, SCS performance management, and cross-government people strategy.

Accountability

12. Parliamentary Accountability

Cabinet Office Select Committee, PACAC oversight, parliamentary questions. Ministerial accountability, government transparency, and FOI coordination across departments.

8 Transformation Priorities

Priority 1

COBR Digital Crisis Room

Crisis coordination latency 6h→30min, cross-government situational awareness +80%. AI-assisted COBR briefings, real-time data fusion from departmental feeds, and automated situation report generation. Target: national crisis response activation within 15 minutes.

Priority 2

National Risk AI Engine

Risk register refresh cycle 12mo→continuous, emerging threat detection +70%. AI-powered horizon scanning, cross-sector risk correlation, and cascading failure modelling. Target: predictive risk intelligence 6 months ahead of traditional assessment.

Priority 3

Civil Contingencies Command

Multi-agency coordination lag 48h→4h, LRF readiness scoring +60%. Automated CCA duty compliance checking, resource allocation optimisation, and exercise scenario generation. Target: all 43 LRFs with real-time readiness dashboards.

Priority 4

National Cyber Defence Fusion

Cyber incident response time 24h→2h, threat intelligence sharing +90%. AI-driven NCSC threat correlation, automated vulnerability scanning for CNI, and Active Cyber Defence scaling. Target: UK cyber defence posture ranked #1 globally.

Priority 5

GOV.UK Intelligence Platform

Service discovery latency 15min→30sec, citizen satisfaction +40%. AI-powered GOV.UK search, personalised service routing, and proactive entitlement notification. Target: every citizen receives all benefits/services they're entitled to automatically.

Priority 6

National Data Fabric

Cross-department data sharing lag 6mo→1 week, analytical capability +75%. Sovereign data mesh, government data catalogue, and federated analytics platform. Target: any government analyst can access any non-classified dataset within 24 hours.

Priority 7

Devolution Compliance Monitor

Sewel Convention violation detection real-time, legislative consent tracking +95%. AI monitoring of devolved legislature Bills, intergovernmental agreement tracking, and constitutional tension early warning. Target: zero unintended legislative consent breaches.

Priority 8

Civil Service Capability AI

Skills gap identification 6mo→real-time, talent deployment optimisation +50%. AI-assisted workforce planning, competency assessment, and cross-department talent mobility matching. Target: right person in right role within 2 weeks of vacancy.

⛔ 6 Sovereign Red Lines

  • 🚫 No COBR crisis response activated without Cabinet Secretary or designated minister authorisation — automated escalation alerts only.
  • 🚫 No National Risk Register intelligence shared with non-government entities without NSC approval and security classification review.
  • 🚫 No civil contingencies data processed beyond the UK without Five Eyes intelligence-sharing agreement and Data Protection Impact Assessment.
  • 🚫 No automated CCA enforcement action against Category 1 responders without Local Resilience Forum human coordinator sign-off.
  • 🚫 No national cyber defence action (takedown, attribution, offensive response) initiated without NCSC Director and Home Secretary approval.
  • 🚫 No devolution-sensitive information cross-shared between devolved administrations without existing intergovernmental agreement framework.
