The Scottish Fiscal Commission (SFC) is Scotland's independent economic and fiscal forecasting institution — established under the Scottish Fiscal Commission Act 2016 — responsible for producing independent, impartial forecasts of Scottish tax revenues, social security expenditure, and Scotland's GDP. The SFC scrutinises Scottish Government forecasts for the Scottish Budget and reports directly to the Scottish Parliament. This pack provides a sovereign AI governance framework for every SFC forecasting domain.

📋 12 Entry Points

TAX

Tax Revenue Forecasting (Income Tax, LBTT, Landfill)

Independent five-year forecasts for devolved Scottish taxes — Income Tax (over £15B annually), Land and Buildings Transaction Tax, and Scottish Landfill Tax — AI governance for revenue-model validation and scenario analysis.

SOCIAL

Social Security Expenditure Forecasting

Forecasts for devolved Scottish social security — Adult Disability Payment, Scottish Child Payment, Carer's Allowance Supplement — AI governance for caseload-modelling, expenditure projection, and policy-costing analysis under Social Security (Scotland) Act 2018.

GDP

GDP & Macroeconomic Forecasting

Scotland's GDP forecasting — output, productivity, employment, earnings — AI governance for macroeconomic-model validation, exogenous-shock simulation, and OBR-UK comparator analysis under the Fiscal Framework.

SUSTAIN

Fiscal Sustainability & Long-term Projections

Long-term fiscal sustainability — demographic ageing, health spending, climate transition costs — AI governance for 50-year projection modelling, scenario-sensitivity analysis, and intergenerational-fairness assessment.

BUDGET

Scottish Budget Scrutiny & Forecast Evaluation

Statutory scrutiny of Scottish Government economic and fiscal forecasts accompanying the Scottish Budget — AI governance for forecast-bias detection, assumption-review analytics, and reconciliation with outturn data.

MINISTERIAL

Ministerial Forecast Review & Challenge

Independent review of Scottish Ministers' fiscal forecasts — AI governance for forecast-assumption documentation, policy-costing methodology audit, and transparency-report generation.

PUBLIC

Public Finance Reporting & Transparency

Biannual Forecast Evaluation Reports and annual Fiscal Sustainability Reports — AI governance for report-data compilation, publication-quality-output assembly, and open-data release under the Code of Practice for Statistics.

DATA

Data Governance & Model Input Management

Management of HMRC taxpayer data, DWP benefit data, and Scottish Government administrative data — AI governance for data-access controls, provenance tracking, and input-data quality verification under DPA 2018.

PARLIAMENT

Scottish Parliament Accountability & Reporting

Direct reporting to the Scottish Parliament — Finance and Public Administration Committee evidence, parliamentary questions, and statutory annual reporting — AI governance for parliamentary-brief assembly and publication-workflow assurance.

MODEL

Model Transparency & Methodology Publication

Publication of SFC forecasting models, assumptions, and methodology — open-science approach — AI governance for model-documentation generation, sensitivity-analysis disclosure, and reproducibility-assurance workflows.

INTERNATIONAL

International Fiscal-Institution Comparators

OBR (UK), CBO (US), CPB (Netherlands), PBO (Canada) — international best-practice benchmarking — AI governance for comparative-methodology analysis, peer-review coordination, and international-standard alignment under OECD Principles for Independent Fiscal Institutions.

RESEARCH

Research, Methodology & Continuous Improvement

Fiscal-forecasting research programme — model development, forecast-error analysis, and methodological innovation — AI governance for research-pipeline management, peer-review tracking, and forecast-accuracy continuous improvement.

🚫 6 Non-Negotiable Red Lines

  • No AI-sole fiscal forecast. All published forecasts, projections, and evaluations are human-determined by SFC Commissioners and analysts. AI assists model specification and data analysis — it never independently publishes a forecast. Scottish Fiscal Commission Act 2016 independence guarantee.
  • No politicisation of independent forecasts. AI governance tools serve SFC's statutory duty of independence — no AI optimisation for politically-favourable forecast outcomes, no forecast-suppression, no selective release of favourable scenarios.
  • No AI manipulation of input data. All HMRC taxpayer microdata, DWP benefit records, and Scottish Government administrative data are used as-provided — AI tools may not impute, transform, or exclude data without documented human methodological approval.
  • No HMRC taxpayer-level data leaves UK jurisdiction. All taxpayer microdata, social security personal data, and benefit records remain UK-resident. DPA 2018 + UK GDPR + Commissioners for Revenue and Customs Act 2005 s.18 confidentiality enforcement.
  • No AI model opacity. Every AI-assisted forecast must disclose the model specification, assumptions, sensitivity analysis, and uncertainty intervals. SFC statutory transparency duty — no black-box forecasting.
  • No AI-automated parliamentary submission without Commissioner sign-off. All reports to the Scottish Parliament — Forecast Evaluation Reports, Fiscal Sustainability Reports, and committee evidence — are approved and signed by the Commissioners before publication.
