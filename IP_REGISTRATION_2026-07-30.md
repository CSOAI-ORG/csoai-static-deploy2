## Why this file exists

This is the **technical registration ledger** — every CSOAI IP artefact is
listed, dated, witnessed (signed by SIGIL Ed25519), and assigned to its
originator. It is NOT a substitute for government IP registration (patent
/ trademark / copyright). It IS the evidence pack to file alongside any
future government registration, and the audit trail that establishes prior
art and ownership if registration is challenged.

**Use cases:**
- Pre-filing disclosure (US 1-year grace period requires "first public
  disclosure" before patent filing for US only — non-US = file FIRST).

- Investor due diligence ("show me your IP register").
- Litigation discovery (chain-of-custody for each IP artefact).
- Provisional patent filing (US only, $320 small-entity fee).
