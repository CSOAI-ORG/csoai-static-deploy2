# DORA Compliance in 2026: Digital Operational Resilience for AI-Powered Financial Services

**Meta Description:** The EU Digital Operational Resilience Act (DORA) takes full effect in 2026. Financial services firms must now prove resilience testing, incident reporting, and third-party ICT risk management — including AI model infrastructure. Here's how MCP attestation fits.

## Key Points
- DORA requires financial entities to maintain digital operational resilience across ICT systems — including AI model serving infrastructure (MCP meshes, model registries, inference pipelines).
- Key mandates: ICT risk management (Art.5-16), incident reporting (Art.17-23), digital operational resilience testing (Art.24-27), and third-party risk management for ICT providers (Art.28-44).
- AI systems that process, route, or serve critical financial functions must be covered by resilience testing and incident detection — MCP servers are in scope.
- The 2026 compliance deadline is fixed — firms not ready face supervisory penalties and mandatory remediation plans under competent authority oversight.
- Automated attestation of MCP infrastructure provides the evidence trail DORA requires for ICT risk management and incident reporting.
