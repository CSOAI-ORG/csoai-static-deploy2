# The CARE Membrane: CQC-Compliant AI Governance for Healthcare Providers

**Meta Description:** The CARE (Clinical AI Risk & Evidence) Membrane framework helps healthcare providers meet CQC Well-Led requirements for AI governance. How to build a compliance layer that satisfies regulators while keeping clinical AI innovation safe and fast.

## Key Points
- The CQC's 2026 inspection framework includes AI governance as a specific line of inquiry under Well-Led — providers must demonstrate board-level visibility into AI system safety, transparency, and risk management.
- The CARE Membrane is a compliance architecture that sits between clinical AI models (diagnostics, triage, scheduling, documentation) and the MCP infrastructure serving them — capturing every model invocation for audit.
- Core components: automated attestation per MCP call, clinical safety classification (DCB 0129/0160), board-ready dashboards, and regulator-friendly evidence exports.
- NHS Foundation Trusts and independent healthcare providers both benefit — the Membrane works across HSCN, NHS Mail, and cloud environments.
- Real-world deployment in radiology AI, discharge summarisation, and surgical planning pathways shows 80% reduction in manual compliance overhead.
