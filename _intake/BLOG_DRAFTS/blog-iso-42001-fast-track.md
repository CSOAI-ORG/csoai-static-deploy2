# ISO 42001 Fast-Track: How to Build an AI Management System in 90 Days

**Meta Description:** ISO/IEC 42001:2023 provides the first certifiable AI management system standard. Here's how to fast-track your implementation in 90 days — from scope definition and gap analysis to internal audit and certification — using automated MCP attestation as the evidence backbone.

## Key Points
- ISO/IEC 42001:2023 is the world's first AI management system (AIMS) standard — certifiable, compatible with ISO 27001/27701, and designed to cover the full AI lifecycle from design to deployment and monitoring.
- A 90-day fast-track is realistic for organisations with mature information security (ISO 27001) and existing MCP infrastructure — the AIMS builds on existing management systems.
- Six key phases: Days 1-15 (scope & gap analysis), Days 16-35 (AIMS documentation & AI risk assessment), Days 36-55 (controls implementation & MCP attestation integration), Days 56-70 (internal audit & corrective actions), Days 71-80 (management review & certification body engagement), Days 81-90 (Stage 1 & Stage 2 audit).
- Automated MCP attestation serves as the evidence backbone for AIMS operational controls — Annex A controls around transparency, fairness, and human oversight are satisfied automatically per model invocation.
- ISO 42001 certification is increasingly a differentiator for regulated industries (healthcare, fintech, gaming) and a prerequisite for certain public sector AI procurement frameworks.
