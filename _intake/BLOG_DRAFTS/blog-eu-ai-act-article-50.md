# EU AI Act Article 50: The Transparency Obligation Your AI Governance Strategy Can't Ignore

**Meta Description:** EU AI Act Article 50 imposes mandatory transparency obligations on organisations deploying AI systems that interact with natural persons. Learn what it means for your MCP estate and how automated attestation ensures compliance by Q3 2026.

## Key Points
- Article 50 requires organisations to disclose when AI systems interact with natural persons, including chatbots, decision-support tools, and personalised content engines — with specific carveouts for clinical override and law enforcement.
- The obligation covers transparency of AI interaction (Art.50(1)), emotion-recognition/biometric categorisation disclosure (Art.50(2)), and deep-generation labelling (Art.50(3-4)).
- Enforcement begins Q3 2026 for high-risk systems, but early adopters gain significant advantage in regulatory readiness and customer trust.
