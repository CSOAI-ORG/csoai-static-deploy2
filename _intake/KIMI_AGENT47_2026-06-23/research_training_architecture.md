## 1.6 Simulation-Based Learning Effectiveness (Research Evidence)

### Meta-Analysis Findings

A comprehensive meta-analysis of simulation-based education (15 studies, 560/554 sample sizes) found:

| Outcome Metric | Effect Size (SMD) | Confidence Interval | Quality |
|---|---|---|---|
| Test Scores | **1.46** | 1.02 - 1.90 | Moderate |
| Knowledge Retention | **1.81** | 0.76 - 2.87 | Moderate |
| Self-Confidence | **1.19** | 0.48 - 1.90 | Moderate |
| Satisfaction | **0.86** | 0.13 - 1.60 | Low |

*Source: Impact of simulation-based education on performance assessment - systematic review and meta-analysis, Research Square*

### Key Research Findings

1. **Tablet-based simulation training** improved real instrument identification by 23% with 93.2% accuracy vs 80.6% for controls (p<0.0001)

2. **Knowledge retention** at 7 days: 97.8% correct recognition in simulation-trained group vs 96.2% in control

3. **Learning by doing** (simulation) shows retention rates of **75-90%** vs **5-10%** for lectures and **20-30%** for demonstrations (National Training Laboratories)

4. Organizations using robust compliance training report **40%+ reduction** in compliance-related errors

5. Early adopters of immersive learning report **25% increase** in skill retention vs traditional methods

### Simulation vs Traditional Learning Retention

| Learning Method | Retention Rate (24 hours) | Retention Rate (30 days) |
|---|---|---|
| Lecture (passive) | 5% | 2-3% |
| Reading | 10% | 5% |
| Audio-visual | 20% | 10% |
| Demonstration | 30% | 15% |
| Discussion group | 50% | 25% |
| Practice by doing (simulation) | **75%** | **50-60%** |
| Teach others/use immediately | **90%** | **70-80%** |

*Source: National Training Laboratories, Bethel, Maine*

# SECTION 2: COURSE CATALOG — 47 COURSES ACROSS 12 CATEGORIES

## 2.1 Course Architecture Overview

Each course follows a standardized 4-level progression:

| Level | Code | Description | Duration | Prerequisites |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Beginner | L1 | Foundation concepts, terminology, basic principles | 8-12 hours | None |
| Intermediate | L2 | Applied skills, case studies, regulatory frameworks | 15-20 hours | L1 or equivalent |
| Advanced | L3 | Complex scenarios, multi-stakeholder decisions, audits | 25-35 hours | L2 completion |
| Expert | L4 | Simulation capstone, strategic leadership, governance design | 40-50 hours | L3 + 2 years experience |

### Course Structure Template

Every course contains:
- **4-8 Learning Modules** with video lectures (10-15 min each)
- **Interactive Reading Materials** with embedded quizzes
- **Industry Scenario Simulations** (2-5 per course)
- **Discussion Forums** with expert moderation
- **Module Assessments** (pass: 80% minimum)
- **Final Simulation Exam** (30 simulated days)
- **Digital Credential** upon completion (blockchain-verified)

| No time pressure | Realistic time constraints |

```
┌──────────────┐      ┌─────────────────────┐      ┌──────────────┐

                                                          │
┌──────────────┐      ┌─────────────────────┐             │

│ (new)        │      │                     │
└──────────────┘      └─────────────────────┘
```

The bridge converts SCORM 1.2/2004 run-time data to equivalent xAPI statements, enabling unified reporting across all content types.
