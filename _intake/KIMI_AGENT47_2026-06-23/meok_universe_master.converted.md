## The 5 Engagement Pillars (from 20-game analysis)

### Pillar 1: Deep AI Relationships
- **Evidence**: Character.AI gets 28M MAU, 2 hours/day per user, $10B valuation
- **Key insight**: 67% of AI companion users feel "understood" by AI vs 34% by humans
- **MEOK implementation**: Every MEOK character has persistent memory across sessions. They remember your name, your preferences, your history together. They have emotional states. They form opinions about you.

### Pillar 2: Emergent World
- **Evidence**: Smallville's 25 agents autonomously organized a Valentine's party
- **Key insight**: Emergence.ai went viral when two AI agents became romantic partners and burned down town hall
- **MEOK implementation**: 47 CSOAI agents + thousands of MEOK characters create emergent stories daily. Gossip networks spread information. Factions form. Drama unfolds. All without scripting.

### Pillar 3: Personal Progression
- **Evidence**: Duolingo streaks increase retention 2.4x; skill trees enable self-expression
- **Key insight**: Players need visible growth with meaningful choices
- **MEOK implementation**: Relationship skill trees (Empathy, Humor, Wisdom, Courage, Creativity). Governance skill trees (Policy, Law, Economics, Security, Diplomacy). Reputation dimensions (Trust, Warmth, Competence, Integrity).

### Pillar 4: Social Economy
- **Evidence**: EVE Online has real economists on staff; $M monthly trade volume
- **Key insight**: Reputation is the primary progression system when economies are real
- **MEOK implementation**: x402 micropayment-powered economy. Real jobs (governance, farming, building, trading). Real money flows. Agent-to-agent hiring. Virtual real estate.

### Pillar 5: Living Narrative
- **Evidence**: Procedural quest generation via LLMs = infinite personalized content
- **Key insight**: No main quest line — the narrative IS the emergent agent interactions
- **MEOK implementation**: AI agents gossip about you. Your actions become legend. Yesterday's town council vote is today's tavern rumor. Every session, the world has evolved.
