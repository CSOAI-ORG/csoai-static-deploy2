### 1.5 Cross-Border Scenarios

**Scenario 1: EU-US AI Diagnostic Tool Deployment**
- An EU-developed AI radiology tool (CE-marked under MDR) seeks FDA 510(k) clearance
- Challenge: FDA requires US-based clinical validation studies; EU clinical data may not generalize to US demographics
- Compliance: Must comply with both EU AI Act (high-risk obligations by Aug 2026) and FDA PCCP guidance
- Solution: Conduct bridging studies; implement FDA-compliant PCCP for algorithm updates; ensure GDPR/HIPAA dual compliance

**Scenario 2: China-EU AI Drug Discovery Platform**
- Chinese AI pharma company uses patient data from EU hospitals to train drug discovery models
- Challenge: GDPR Art. 9 (special category data) + PIPL cross-border transfer restrictions; algorithm transparency requirements differ
- Compliance: Must execute Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) + Transfer Impact Assessment (TIA); obtain explicit consent
- Solution: Federated learning approach; EU data stays in EU; only model parameters transferred; DPIA conducted

**Scenario 3: India-UK Telemedicine AI Platform**
- Indian AI telemedicine provider offers remote diagnosis to UK patients
- Challenge: UK MHRA medical device registration required; Indian telemedicine guidelines differ from UK standards
- Compliance: Must register as SaMD with MHRA; comply with UK GDPR; professional liability under UK law
- Solution: Establish UK entity as legal manufacturer; comply with MHRA QMS requirements; implement UK-specific clinical validation

### 2.5 Cross-Border Scenarios

**Scenario 1: EU-to-US Surgical Robot Market Entry**
- European surgical robot manufacturer seeks US market access
- Challenge: FDA requires US clinical trials even with CE marking; 510(k) pathway requires predicate device identification
- Compliance: FDA QSR compliance; US-based quality system; clinical trials conducted under US IDE regulations
- Solution: Parallel FDA pre-submission meetings; leverage existing CE clinical data as supplementary evidence

**Scenario 2: China-to-EU Wearable Medical Device**
- Chinese manufacturer of AI-enabled CGM (continuous glucose monitor) seeks EU market
- Challenge: EU MDR requires Notified Body involvement for Class IIa+; clinical evidence from Chinese population may not be accepted
- Compliance: CE marking under MDR; PMS plan; PMCF (Post-Market Clinical Follow-up) required
- Solution: Conduct European clinical investigation; appoint EU Authorized Representative; establish MDR-compliant QMS

**Scenario 3: US-to-Japan AI-Enabled Diagnostic Device**
- US company with FDA-cleared AI diagnostic seeks PMDA approval in Japan
- Challenge: PMDA may require Japan-specific clinical validation; language requirements for labeling
- Compliance: Foreign Manufacturer Registration; PMDA QMS Ordinance compliance; Japanese labeling
- Solution: Utilize FDA clinical data where applicable; conduct bridging study for Japanese population; appoint Marketing Authorization Holder (MAH)

### 3.5 Cross-Border Scenarios

**Scenario 1: US-EU CRISPR Gene Therapy Clinical Trial**
- US biotech company conducts CRISPR trial for sickle cell disease in US and EU simultaneously
- Challenge: FDA IND vs. EMA CTAs have different requirements; CMC data presentation differs
- Compliance: US: IND + IRB; EU: CTA + ethics committee in each member state; MAA divergent requirements
- Solution: Utilize ICH CTD format; conduct parallel regulatory submissions; establish EU QPPV

**Scenario 2: UK-China Genomic Data Sharing for Research**
- UK genomic medicine company partners with Chinese hospital to analyze patient genomes
- Challenge: China Human Genetic Resources Regulation restricts export; UK GDPR applies to data processing
- Compliance: Chinese HGR approval required; UK GDPR Art. 9 special category data; explicit consent required
- Solution: Joint analysis in China with UK oversight; federated learning; only aggregate results shared; HGR application filed

**Scenario 3: Singapore-Japan Gene Therapy Manufacturing**
- Singapore-based gene therapy company manufactures product for Japanese patients
- Challenge: PMDA requires Japan-specific GMP inspection; viral vector manufacturing standards differ
- Compliance: Singapore HSA + Japan PMDA dual regulatory; foreign manufacturer registration with PMDA
- Solution: Pre-submission meeting with PMDA; Japan GMP audit conducted; appoint Japanese MAH
