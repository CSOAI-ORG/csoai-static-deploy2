### 1.5 Cross-Border Compliance Scenarios

**Scenario 1: 5G Network Equipment Export (US-China-EU Triangle)**
- US entity wants to sell 5G equipment to EU operator
- Must comply with US Export Administration Regulations (EAR), EU Cybersecurity Certification Scheme (5G), and potential country-of-origin restrictions
- CHIPS Act restricts Chinese fab production for advanced chips
- EU demands security-by-design certification
- Timeline: 12-18 months for full compliance

**Scenario 2: Multi-National IoT Deployment Using 5G SA Network Sliccing**
- Manufacturing company deploys 5G-connected factories in Germany, Singapore, and Brazil
- Must navigate: EU EECC (Germany), IMDA licensing (Singapore), ANATEL certification (Brazil)
- Each jurisdiction has different spectrum bands, security requirements, and data localization rules
- EU Data Act requires IoT data portability across all three locations

**Scenario 3: Satellite-Terrestrial 5G Integration (NTN)**
- Satellite operator provides 5G NTN coverage across Middle East (UAE, Saudi Arabia) and Europe
- Must comply with: UAE TDRA satellite regulations, Saudi CST framework, EU EECC roaming rules
- Cross-border spectrum coordination for LEO satellite constellations
- Data sovereignty issues when traffic routes through satellite ground stations in different jurisdictions

### 2.5 Cross-Border Compliance Scenarios

**Scenario 1: Smart Home Device Manufacturer (China to EU + US)**
- Chinese manufacturer exports smart home devices to EU and US
- Must comply with: EU Cyber Resilience Act (mandatory), EU Data Act (data portability), UK PSTI Act, US FCC certification, US Cyber Trust Mark
- China PIPL restrictions on data transfer outside China
- Device firmware must support different regional requirements

**Scenario 2: Industrial IoT Platform Across 3 Continents**
- German company operates IIoT platform monitoring factories in Germany, India, and Mexico
- EU Data Act requires data sharing with third-party maintenance providers
- India DPDP Act restricts cross-border transfers to certain countries
- Mexico data protection law (LFPDPPP) requires data subject consent
- Challenge: Synchronizing compliance across three data protection regimes

**Scenario 3: Connected Vehicle (Automotive IoT)**
- Car manufacturer collects data from vehicles across EU, China, and UAE
- EU: GDPR + Data Act + type approval requirements
- China: PIPL + DSL, CAC security assessment for >1M users
- UAE: Federal PDPL (full compliance by Jan 2027)
- Data categories vary: personal data, vehicle diagnostics, location, biometric
