- Why: Violates expectations, brain can't scroll past

- Example: "NVIDIA doesn't want solo developers to know about DGX Spark..."
- Why: Creates curiosity gap, promises exclusive info

**3. The Warning/Urgency Hook** (24% of viral videos)
- Formula: "Stop [common action] - [AI development] changes everything"
- Example: "Stop learning to code the old way. GPT-5 drops in August and..."
- Why: FOMO + fear trigger, immediate attention

**4. The Specificity Hook** (20% of viral videos)
- Formula: "[Hyper-specific detail] - here's what happened"
- Example: "I built 47 AI agents in a 1998 caravan with $847 in the bank"
- Why: Hyper-specificity creates instant credibility

**5. The Timeframe Tension Hook** (18% of viral videos)
- Formula: "[Short timeframe] ago I had [small thing]. Today: [big result]"
- Example: "90 days ago I had 0 AI agents. Today I have 47 and they run my business"
- Why: Dopamine hit + hope + "if he can, I can"

**6. The Contradiction Hook** (15% of viral videos)
- Formula: "[Statement A] but [opposite Statement B]"
- Example: "I'm building the most advanced AI system... from a caravan in the woods"
- Why: Unresolved tension forces viewer to watch

**7. The POV: Advice in Disguise Hook** (14% of viral videos)
- Formula: "POV: You [discovered/solved] [relatable problem]"
- Example: "POV: You figured out how to run 47 AI agents without a data center"
- Why: Defenses down, relatability high

**8. The Social Proof Hook** (12% of viral videos)
- Formula: "[Impressive number] people are [action] with [AI thing]"
- Example: "50,000 developers already downloaded my AI agent framework"
- Why: Bandwagon effect, fear of missing out

**9. The Counterintuitive Truth Hook** (10% of viral videos)
- Formula: "Everyone says [common belief] about AI, but the opposite is true"
- Example: "Everyone says you need a team to build AI. I did it alone from a van."
- Why: Challenges assumptions, creates intellectual engagement

**10. The Trend-Jacking Hook** (8% of viral videos)
