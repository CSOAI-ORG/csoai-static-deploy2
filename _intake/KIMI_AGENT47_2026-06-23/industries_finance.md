## 1.5 Cross-Border Scenarios

### Scenario 1: EU-to-US AI Credit Scoring
A German bank using AI to score a US loan applicant must comply with both EU AI Act "high-risk" requirements and US FCRA/ECOA obligations. Conflict: EU requires extensive documentation while US prohibits discriminatory outcomes. Resolution: Dual-compliant model with explainable AI architecture and regular bias audits.

### Scenario 2: Cross-Border AI Fraud Detection (Singapore-EU)
A Singapore-based fintech with EU customers must ensure AI fraud detection complies with both MAS FEAT Principles and GDPR automated decision-making rules. Cross-border data flows require adequacy mechanisms.

### Scenario 3: AI Robo-Advisor Serving Multiple Jurisdictions
A US robo-advisor expanding to UK, Australia, and Japan must register under SEC IA Act, FCA rules, ASIC AFSL requirements, and JFSA investment advisory registration, with different suitability standards in each jurisdiction.
