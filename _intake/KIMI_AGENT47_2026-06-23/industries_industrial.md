## 1.5 Three Cross-Border Scenarios

### Scenario 1: German Automaker Adopting AI in Chinese Smart Factory
- **Context**: A German OEM (Volkswagen/BMW) deploys AI-powered digital twins and predictive maintenance in its Chinese joint-venture manufacturing plant.
- **Cross-Border Issues**: EU AI Act extraterritoriality applies if system output affects EU market; China's algorithm registration requirements for AI systems; data localization requirements in China; need for dual compliance certification.
- **Key Challenge**: Reconciling EU GDPR/data governance requirements with China's data security laws (CSL/DSL/PIPL).

### Scenario 2: Japanese Industrial Robot Manufacturer Selling to EU
- **Context**: Fanuc or Yaskawa deploying AI-enabled collaborative robots in European automotive plants.
- **Cross-Border Issues**: Must comply with EU AI Act (high-risk category for industrial robotics); CE marking required; conformity assessment; Japan's METI voluntary guidelines vs. EU mandatory requirements.
- **Key Challenge**: Meeting EU AI Act's data governance, transparency, and human oversight requirements while maintaining Japan's innovation-focused approach.

### Scenario 3: US Cloud AI Provider Serving Manufacturing Clients in South Korea
- **Context**: AWS/Azure/Google Cloud providing AI manufacturing analytics platforms to Korean conglomerates (Samsung, Hyundai).
- **Cross-Border Issues**: South Korea's AI Basic Act applies to any AI system affecting Korean users (extraterritorial); data sovereignty considerations; US Cloud Act vs. Korean data protection; KRW 30M maximum penalty.
- **Key Challenge**: Building compliant data infrastructure that satisfies both US and Korean regulatory requirements.
