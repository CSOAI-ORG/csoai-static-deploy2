## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The AI governance and compliance market has exploded from a niche category to a **$560M+ annual funding market** in AI safety alone (2025-2026). We identified **75+ active startups**, **20 major funding rounds** in AI safety (July 2025-June 2026), and a rapidly consolidating competitive landscape. The market is characterized by:

- **Platform Consolidation**: Top 10 deals capture 91.81% of disclosed funding [^1881^]
- **Agentic AI Pivot**: 2026's defining trend is AI agent governance, not just model evaluation
- **Regulatory Tailwinds**: EU AI Act (August 2026 deadline), NIST AI RMF, DORA driving demand
- **Open Source Ecosystem**: 874+ GitHub repositories tagged "responsible-ai" [^1900^]
- **Geographic Concentration**: North America captures 87.80% of AI safety funding [^1881^]

**Critical Insight**: The market is splitting into two camps -- (1) observability/evaluation platforms (Arize, Braintrust, LMArena) and (2) governance/control platforms (Credo AI, Singulr, Trustible, Modulos). CSOAI's sovereign AI governance vision competes most directly with Category 2.

### 2. Competition is Intense but Fragmented
No single company has won. The market has:
- **Observability leaders** (Arize, Braintrust) -- infrastructure play

- **Multi-jurisdiction**: Enzai tracks global regulations, but most are EU or US focused.
- **Open source core**: Giskard and VerifyWise are open, but lack enterprise polish.
