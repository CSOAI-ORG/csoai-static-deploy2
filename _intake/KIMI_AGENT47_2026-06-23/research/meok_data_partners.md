## 1. Media Measurement Panels

### 1.1 Nielsen Computer and Mobile Panel

**What they do**: Nielsen is the world's leading provider of media and marketing information, operating for over 90 years. The Computer & Mobile Panel studies how consumers use the internet by monitoring websites visited, apps used, and device usage patterns. [^569^]

**What they pay**:
- Up to **$60/year in reward points** per registered mobile device, redeemable via PayPal or gift cards (Amazon, Walmart, etc.) [^569^]
- Computer panel members enter monthly sweepstakes for $500 prizes [^563^]
- Points-based system: ~40 points/week per connected device; bonuses of 100-200 points for joining, birthdays, long-term enrollment [^564^]
- One device earns ~2,500 points/year = ~$115 PayPal or $125 gift cards [^564^]

**Data collected**: Websites visited, apps used, content viewed/listened to, devices used. Does NOT collect content of visited websites, user IDs, passwords, or private information. [^569^]

**Data format**: Proprietary Nielsen app/software installed on devices; anonymous usage data transmitted securely.

**Privacy requirements**: Anonymous data collection; opt-in panel participation; privacy certifications; GDPR compliant.

**Partnership process**: Apply at computermobilepanel.nielsen.com; register devices; install app; passive data collection begins.

**Relevance to MEOK**: Nielsen could be a model for how MEOK structures its own data-sharing rewards program. They pay $60/year for device usage data — gameplay data could command a premium.

### 1.2 MobileXpression (by Comscore)

**What they do**: MobileXpression is a market research community operated by Comscore, Inc. (Nasdaq: SCOR), a publicly traded audience measurement company. It tracks mobile web browsing and app usage via VPN technology for market research purposes. [^566^]

**What they pay**:
- **$5 Amazon gift card (US)** after 1 week of active membership [^578^]
- **£20 Amazon voucher (UK)** after 2 weeks [^578^]
- **~2 credits/week** for active members; 10 credits = $5 gift card; 15 credits = $10 gift card [^566^]
- Periodic sweepstakes entries ($100,000 grand prize) [^578^]
- Additional credits for surveys ($0.50-$5 per survey) [^566^]

**Data collected**: Mobile web browsing activity, usage and titles of downloaded apps and files, camera/video streaming usage, web pages viewed, links accessed, usage times for texting, calls, and web browsing. Uses VPN to monitor traffic. [^578^]

**Data format**: VPN-based mobile traffic monitoring; encrypted and securely sent to MobileXpression servers (< 100KB app, 128KB data storage before encryption).

**Privacy requirements**: App requires enabling VPN; collects mobile browsing data; users must be 18+ (or 13+ with parental consent).

**Partnership process**: Download app on iOS/Android; enable VPN; keep app running passively.

**Relevance to MEOK**: Comscore is a major media measurement company actively buying mobile behavioral data. They have multi-year data partnerships with HyphaMetrics for person-level audience measurement across gaming and digital content. [^519^]

### 2.2 What Data Brokers Pay For

**Data categories in demand**:
- Demographics (age, gender, income, education, household composition)
- Purchase behavior (online + in-store transactions)
- Browsing behavior (websites visited, time spent, content consumed)
- App usage (which apps, frequency, duration)
- Location data (where people go, dwell time, travel patterns)
- Interest segments (hobbies, preferences, lifestyle categories)
- Health indicators (OTC purchases, health-related subscriptions, search queries)

**Pricing models**:
- Per-record pricing: $0.01-$1.00 per consumer record depending on richness
- Subscription: Monthly/annual access to datasets
- Licensing: Per-use or unlimited-use licenses
- Revenue share: Percentage of media spend or downstream revenue

**How to sell to them**: Data brokers typically purchase data through:
1. Direct data supply partnerships (long-term contracts)
2. Data marketplaces (LiveRamp, Datarade, etc.)
3. Custom one-off dataset purchases

**Relevance to MEOK**: Data brokers are interested in behavioral data of all types. Gameplay data (session length, in-game purchases, player preferences, engagement patterns) could be packaged as "digital lifestyle" or "entertainment behavior" segments. LiveRamp specifically has a Data Marketplace where gaming behavioral segments could be listed. [^715^]

## 12. Ethical Data Monetization Framework

### 12.1 Core Principles

Based on industry best practices, MEOK should follow these principles: [^706^] [^707^] [^708^]

1. **Prioritize anonymization**: All PII must be removed or replaced with synthetic alternatives
2. **Obtain explicit consent**: Users must clearly understand what data is collected and how it's monetized
3. **Transparency**: Clear communication about data practices, partners, and revenue sharing
4. **Equitable use**: Ensure data doesn't enable discrimination or harmful targeting
5. **Regulatory compliance**: GDPR, CCPA, and emerging data protection laws
6. **Data minimization**: Collect only what's necessary
7. **User control**: Allow users to opt out, access, and delete their data

### 12.2 Consent Model Options

| Model | Description | Example |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| **Opt-in rewards** | Users actively choose to share data for rewards | Nielsen Computer & Mobile Panel ($60/year) |
| **Micro-rewards** | Small payments per data contribution | Google Opinion Rewards ($0.10-$2/survey) |
| **Revenue share** | Users receive % of data monetization | Brave Browser (BAT tokens) |
| **Service trade** | Data in exchange for free service | MobileXpression ($5 gift card/week) |
| **Premium tier** | Pay for privacy OR share data for free | Freemium model |

### 12.3 Regulatory Considerations

- **GDPR** (EU): Requires explicit consent, right to deletion, data portability
- **CCPA** (California): Requires opt-out rights, disclosure of data selling
- **EU AI Act**: Regulates biometric data handling
- **HIPAA**: Applies if any health-related data is collected
- **COPPA**: Applies if users under 13 are present
