## 1. Executive Summary

### Key Findings

**The core lesson from 5+ years of play-to-earn experiments is clear**: Models that pay players purely for participation collapse under inflationary pressure. Sustainable models tie earnings to (1) verifiable value creation, (2) external revenue streams, or (3) scarcity-based gameplay that makes spending more attractive than selling.

**What Failed**: Axie Infinity's scholarship model, STEPN's unsustainable token yields, early P2E games that printed tokens without sinks. 93% of Web3 games launched between 2020-2026 collapsed, with $12-15 billion written off [^666^].

**What Works**: Brave Browser's BAT (real advertiser revenue), Sweatcoin (brand subsidies + subscription), Sorare (licensed IP + fantasy sports), Mythical Games' player-owned economies (optional Web3 on mainstream games), and UBI pilots (government/philanthropic funding).

**CSOAI Applicability**: The most promising model combines data monetization (players earn for contributing data/attention) with sustainable gameplay loops. Players should earn for verifiable contributions (data, content, referrals) funded by external buyers, not inflationary token printing.

## 2. Axie Infinity

### Model: Play-to-Earn via NFT Battles

Axie Infinity emerged as the flagship play-to-earn game, built around breeding, battling, and trading NFT creatures called Axies. At its peak, it had 2.5 million monthly active users and generated over $1.3 billion in revenue in 2021 [^584^].

### How Earnings Worked

Players earned **Smooth Love Potion (SLP)** through daily battles and quests, which could be sold on exchanges. Competitive players earned **Axie Infinity Shards (AXS)** through tournaments and leaderboard rankings. The Ronin blockchain (a custom Ethereum sidechain) enabled low-cost transactions [^667^].

- **Peak earnings**: Top players earned $15-50/day during the boom
- **Scholarship system**: Asset owners ("managers") lent Axies to players ("scholars") in exchange for 30-70% of earnings
- **Entry cost**: $600-1,000 for a competitive team at peak

### What Worked

- Created an entire economy around gameplay with guilds, scholarships, and professional players
- Free starter Axies later lowered the barrier to entry [^667^]
- Community-led guilds provided structured earning opportunities
- The Ronin sidechain solved Ethereum gas fee problems

### What Collapsed

- **The Ronin Bridge Hack**: $625 million stolen in March 2022 — one of the largest crypto heists ever [^584^]
- **Death Spiral Economics**: SLP supply exploded while demand collapsed. As token prices fell, player earnings dropped, causing players to leave, further reducing demand
- **Scholar System Breakdown**: When SLP prices crashed, scholarships became unprofitable for both managers and scholars
- **NFT price collapse**: Axies that cost hundreds of dollars became nearly worthless
- **Current earnings**: Skilled players earn $2-20/day depending on rank — a fraction of peak earnings [^667^]

### Sustainability Verdict: **FAILED — Classic Ponzi Dynamics**

The model required continuous new player inflow to sustain token prices. When growth stalled, the entire economy collapsed. The game is now rebuilding as "Axie Origins" with a focus on gameplay rather than earnings.

### Scam Risk: Low (legitimate game, flawed economics)

### CSOAI Applicability: **HIGH** — The scholarship model proves that players will grind for cents/hour if the path to earnings is clear. The key lesson: earnings must be funded by external revenue (ads, data buyers, brand subsidies), not token inflation.

## 3. STEPN

### Model: Move-to-Earn via GPS-Tracked Exercise

STEPN pioneered the "move-to-earn" category, rewarding users with cryptocurrency for walking, jogging, or running while wearing NFT sneakers tracked via GPS [^582^][^586^].

### How Earnings Work

- **GST (Green Satoshi Token)**: Uncapped utility token earned through physical activity. Used for repairs, upgrades, and minting new sneakers. Initially earned at ~5 GST/day for new users, up to 150 GST/day for upgraded sneakers [^582^]
- **GMT (Green Metaverse Token)**: Governance token with fixed supply of 6 billion
- **Dual-token model**: GST for in-game utility, GMT for governance and value accrual
- **Burn mechanics**: GST is constantly burned through repairs, upgrades, and minting
- **Daily earning caps**: Limit inflation by capping daily earnings per sneaker

### What Worked

- Strong token burn mechanics that created genuine in-game demand for GST
- Fitness + financial incentive attracted millions of users
- Daily earning caps prevented runaway inflation
- The dual-token separation allowed gameplay utility to function independently of speculation

### What Failed

- **Entry barrier**: NFT sneakers cost $500-1,000+ at peak
- **GPS spoofing**: Players faked movement to earn without exercising
- **Token death spiral**: When GST price collapsed, earnings became negligible
- **Unsustainable yields**: Early adopters earned extraordinary returns that couldn't last
- **Maintenance costs**: Repair costs in GST often exceeded earnings for casual players

### Sustainability Verdict: **MARGINAL** — Survived longer than pure P2E games but earnings are now minimal

### Scam Risk: Low-Medium (legitimate app, unsustainable yields at peak)

### CSOAI Applicability: **HIGH** — The GPS verification model proves that real-world activity can be gamified and monetized. The GPS tracking + burn mechanic combination is directly applicable to a "run to earn crypto" game.

## 4. Sweatcoin

### Model: Walk and Earn via Brand-Sponsored Rewards

Sweatcoin is one of the oldest and most successful "walk and earn" apps, founded in 2014. It converts steps into a digital currency that can be redeemed for goods, services, or donated to charity [^528^].

### How Earnings Work

- **1 Sweatcoin per 1,000 steps** (capped at 5,000 steps/day for free users, higher for premium)
- **Revenue sources**: Brand partnerships (95% of users redeem brand offers), premium subscriptions, and the SWEAT alt-coin [^528^]
- **Brand marketplace**: Users redeem Sweatcoins for discounts on products, services, and experiences
- **Premium tier**: Users pay for increased earning limits and faster coin generation

### Key Statistics (2025)

- **150 million registered users** as of 2024 [^528^]
- **$10 million revenue** in 2025 [^528^]
- **22.5 million downloads** in 2025 [^528^]
- **SWEAT token market cap**: $32 million (June 2025) [^528^]
- Peak monthly sales averaged $28.6M in 2022, declining to $8.3M in 2024

### What Works

- **No cost to participate** — zero entry barrier
- **Brand subsidies fund rewards** — advertisers pay for user attention
- **Premium subscriptions** create recurring revenue
- **Health focus** attracts users regardless of earning potential
- **95:5 model**: 95% redeem small brand offers, 5% save for large purchases [^528^]

### Limitations

- **Low individual earnings**: Most rewards are discounts, not cash
- **Cash conversion limited**: Difficult to convert Sweatcoin to real money
- **Declining downloads**: Down from 2022 peak [^528^]
- **User complaints**: Many offers are just discount codes, not real earnings [^533^]

### Sustainability Verdict: **MODERATE** — Survived 10+ years because brand subsidies + subscriptions fund the model. Not a path to meaningful income, but sustainable as a retention mechanism.

### Scam Risk: **VERY LOW** — Established company, real revenue, legitimate business model

### CSOAI Applicability: **VERY HIGH** — The Sweatcoin model proves that players will engage daily for small rewards funded by brand advertisers. This is directly applicable: a game where players earn for completing fitness challenges, with brands subsidizing rewards.

## 5. Pi Network

### Model: Phone-Based "Mining" via Social Referrals

Pi Network claims to be a mobile-first blockchain where users "mine" Pi coins by tapping a button daily and referring others. Founded by Stanford PhDs in 2019, it has attracted over 60 million registered users [^526^][^527^].

### How "Earnings" Work

- Users tap a button once every 24 hours to "mine"
- Mining rate determined by: base rate + Security Circle bonus + Referral Team bonus + lockup bonus + node participation + app usage [^526^]
- **KYC mandatory** to access mined tokens
- Open Mainnet launched February 2025, but token trading remains heavily restricted
- Current price: ~$0.15 (down from ~$1 opening price) [^529^]

### Legitimacy Analysis

**Evidence FOR legitimacy**:
- Founded by verified Stanford PhDs (Dr. Nicolas Kokkalis, Dr. Chengdiao Fan) [^525^]
- Built on modified Stellar Consensus Protocol [^526^]
- Three known VCs funded: Designer Fund, 137 Ventures, Ulu Ventures [^525^]
- 125,000+ merchants reportedly accepting Pi [^526^]
- $100M venture fund launched for ecosystem development [^526^]

**Red Flags**:
- All mainnet nodes reportedly operated centrally by the core team (CNN report, Jan 2025) [^526^]
- No listings on major exchanges [^526^]
- Heavy reliance on referral system draws MLM comparisons [^526^]
- In-app ads generate revenue for founders regardless of token value [^529^]
- Token value collapsed from $1 to $0.15 [^529^]
- Vague roadmaps and missed deadlines [^530^]
- Users create no real value except viewing ads and providing data [^529^]

### Key Verdict

Pi Network "sits somewhere between ambition and execution — not an obvious scam, but not yet the revolution it claims to be" [^526^]. The project has survived 6+ years and built genuine infrastructure, but its MLM-like referral structure and centralized control raise serious concerns. Users invest time and personal data with limited returns.

### Sustainability Verdict: **UNCERTAIN** — Infrastructure exists but token has no external value. The entire model depends on ecosystem adoption that has not materialized.

### Scam Risk: **MEDIUM-HIGH** — Not a clear-cut scam, but highly centralized, limited utility, MLM-like growth pattern. Users should not expect significant earnings.

### CSOAI Applicability: **LOW** — The "tap to earn" model creates no real value. However, the massive user acquisition (60M+ users) shows the power of zero-cost onboarding with social referral mechanics.

## 6. Helium (HNT)

### Model: Proof of Coverage for IoT Network

Helium created a decentralized wireless network where individuals deploy hotspots to provide LoRaWAN coverage for IoT devices, earning HNT tokens in return [^576^][^580^].

### How Earnings Work

- **Proof of Coverage (PoC)**: Hotspots send and receive radio signals to verify coverage [^576^]
- **Three roles in PoC**: Challenger, Transmitter, Witness — each earns HNT [^583^]
- **Data transfer rewards**: Hotspots earn a percentage of Data Credit fees for relaying IoT device data [^578^]
- **Location is everything**: Two identical devices can generate completely different revenue based on placement [^576^]

### Key Economics

- **Hotspot cost**: $300-800 per device [^576^]
- **Power consumption**: Very low (<10 watts) [^576^]
- **Current earnings**: "Low single-digit euro range per month" for most miners [^576^]
- **Payback period**: "In many cases, no longer at all or only over very long periods of time" [^576^]
- **Network migrated to Solana** in April 2023, now also earns MOBILE and IOT tokens [^579^]

### What Worked

- Built the world's largest contiguous IoT wireless network [^580^]
- Genuine utility: real IoT devices use the network
- Low power consumption makes operation cheap
- Pioneer of DePIN (Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Networks)

### What Failed

- **Hotspot oversaturation**: Too many hotspots in some areas, splitting rewards
- **Declining rewards**: As more hotspots joined, individual rewards plummeted
- **Limited real data usage**: Network was built before demand materialized
- **No longer economically viable** for new entrants in most locations [^576^]
- **Complexity**: Requires technical knowledge to optimize placement

### Sustainability Verdict: **DECLINING** — The network infrastructure is real and used, but individual earnings have collapsed. Pioneer hotspots did well; new entrants cannot profit.

### Scam Risk: **LOW** — Legitimate technology with real utility. Not a scam, but economics don't work for new participants.

### CSOAI Applicability: **HIGH** — The DePIN model (earn for providing real-world infrastructure/data) is directly applicable. Players could earn for providing data, compute, or connectivity that has real value to buyers.

## 7. Brave Browser & BAT

### Model: Pay Users for Attention (Viewing Ads)

Brave blocks invasive ads by default and offers users opt-in privacy-preserving ads in exchange for 70% of ad revenue paid in Basic Attention Token (BAT) [^572^][^573^].

### How Earnings Work

- Users opt-in to view privacy-preserving ads
- **Users receive 70% of ad revenue** in BAT [^572^]
- **Brave keeps 30%**
- Ads are matched locally on-device — no personal data leaves the user's device [^573^]
- Users can use BAT to tip content creators, buy gift cards, or exchange for other currencies [^575^]
- **BAT supply**: Fixed at 1.5 billion tokens, ~1.495 billion in circulation [^587^]

### Key Statistics

- **18.3 million monthly active users** (December 2021), grown significantly since [^574^]
- **1.5 million+ verified content creators** registered [^574^]
- **8%+ average click-through rate** vs 2% industry average [^574^]
- **400+ advertisers** on the platform [^577^]
- Founded by Brendan Eich (creator of JavaScript, co-founder of Mozilla/Firefox) [^574^]
- Raised $35M in 2017 ICO + $7M+ from VCs including Peter Thiel's Founder Fund [^574^]

### What Works

- **Real revenue**: Advertisers actually pay for ad placements [^572^]
- **Privacy-preserving**: No tracking, no personal data collection [^573^]
- **Low friction**: Users do what they already do (browse) and earn passively
- **Fixed token supply**: No inflationary death spiral
- **Creator tipping**: Creates a circular economy within the browser

### Limitations

- **Low individual earnings**: Typical users earn $1-5/month in BAT
- **BAT price volatility**: Earnings fluctuate with token price
- **Limited cash-out options**: Depends on region and custodial partner availability
- **Ad volume varies**: Earnings depend on advertiser demand in user's region

### Sustainability Verdict: **STRONG** — The only model in this report backed by real advertiser revenue. Brave is a real company with real revenue, real users, and a sustainable model.

### Scam Risk: **VERY LOW** — Public company with transparent revenue, regular BAT purchases published [^572^]

### CSOAI Applicability: **VERY HIGH** — This is the gold standard. Users earn for their attention, funded by advertisers who pay real money. A gaming equivalent would have players watch/engage with sponsored content and earn a share of revenue. The 70/30 revenue split and on-device privacy matching are directly applicable.

## 8. Presearch

### Model: Earn Tokens for Searching

Presearch is a decentralized search engine that rewards users with PRE tokens for conducting searches and running nodes. It routes search queries through a network of independently operated nodes [^581^].

### How Earnings Work

- Users earn PRE tokens for performing searches
- Node operators earn for routing search traffic
- **Tens of thousands of independently operated nodes** [^581^]
- Tokens can be staked on keywords in the ad system
- ERC-20 token on Ethereum and Base blockchains [^581^]

### Key Features

- Partially open-source as of 2025 [^581^]
- Google added Presearch as a default search engine option on Android in the EU following antitrust rulings [^581^]
- Featured in PCMag's list of alternative privacy search engines [^581^]
- WeFunder campaign in 2025 to raise $1.2M for decentralized web index [^581^]

### Sustainability Verdict: **MARGINAL** — Decentralized search is a compelling concept, but competing with Google is extremely difficult. Revenue from search ads must grow significantly.

### Scam Risk: **LOW** — Legitimate project, academic reviews confirm potential, but faces scalability limitations [^581^]

### CSOAI Applicability: **MEDIUM** — The "search to earn" model shows users will change behavior for small rewards. A game-integrated search feature could earn players tokens for discovery activities.

## 9. Sorare

### Model: Fantasy Sports + Licensed NFT Player Cards

Sorare combines officially licensed sports NFTs with fantasy sports gameplay. Players collect digital trading cards of real athletes and compete in fantasy tournaments for ETH prizes [^617^][^619^].

### How Earnings Work

- Players buy NFT player cards through Primary Market Auctions (~85% of revenue) [^619^]
- Cards are used to enter fantasy competitions with ETH prize pools
- Rare cards have higher scoring multipliers
- Secondary market trading (no direct fees on P2P trades) [^619^]
- **Threshold/hot streak mechanics** reward consistent high performance [^617^]

### Key Financials

- **$680 million Series B** at $4.3B valuation (2021) [^617^]
- **Revenue**: $167M (2022) → $69M (2023) → $50M (2024) [^617^]
- **EBITDA losses**: $234M (2022) → $117M (2024) — halved [^617^]
- **Cash reserves**: $230M (2023) → $91M (2024) → $41M projected (2025) [^617^]
- **Monthly transaction peaks**: 1M+ monthly transactions in August 2025 [^617^]
- **Platform trading volume**: Estimated $500M+ annually [^619^]
- **Licensing**: 300+ clubs and major leagues (NBA, MLB, football leagues)

### What Works

- **Licensed IP creates scarcity**: Official partnerships with real sports leagues
- **Fantasy sports is a proven model**: Billions spent annually on traditional fantasy
- **Skill-based earnings**: Better fantasy managers earn more
- **NFTs have genuine utility**: Cards affect gameplay scoring
- **Revenue diversification**: Primary sales, premium features, B2B data partnerships [^619^]

### What Failed

- **Threshold payouts removed** in September 2024 due to hard costs [^617^]
- **Sales declining steeply**: From $28.6M/month average (2022) to $8.3M (2024) [^617^]
- **Burning through cash**: Reserves declining rapidly [^617^]
- **Monthly sales hit lowest since 2021** in June 2025 ($2.7M) before hot streak mechanic revival [^617^]

### Sustainability Verdict: **MARGINAL** — The fantasy sports model is sound, but the NFT market downturn has hit hard. Recent hot streak mechanics show signs of revival, but cash burn is concerning.

### Scam Risk: **LOW** — Real licensed partnerships, real gameplay, transparent (though declining) revenue.

### CSOAI Applicability: **HIGH** — The licensed IP + fantasy competition model creates genuine demand for assets. A sports prediction or fantasy game with licensed partnerships could drive sustainable earnings.

## 10. Gods Unchained

### Model: Free-to-Play Trading Card Game with NFT Ownership

Gods Unchained is a digital trading card game similar to Magic: The Gathering where cards are NFTs with real ownership. Players earn GODS tokens through gameplay [^615^][^618^].

### How Earnings Work

- **Free to play**: New players get free cards to start
- **GODS token**: Earned through ranked play, daily rewards, and tournaments
- **Sealed Mode**: Entry fee of 5 GODS (~$1.20), earn up to 47.75 GODS for 7 wins [^618^]
- **Card ownership**: Cards are NFTs tradeable on the marketplace
- **Total GODS supply**: 500 million (34% allocated to play-to-earn rewards) [^624^]
- **Daily earnings**: $1-2/day for active players at ranks 10-12 [^620^]

### What Works

- **Free entry** eliminates barrier
- **Skill-based**: Better players earn more
- **True card ownership**: Cards have resale value
- **Regular tournaments and events** create engagement
- **Mobile version** expanded reach [^615^]
- **Built on Immutable X (Layer 2)**: Zero gas fees for trading

### Limitations

- **Low earnings**: $1-2/day is typical for active players [^620^]
- **GODS token price volatility** affects earnings
- **Requires significant playtime** to reach earning ranks
- **Competition from Hearthstone/MTG Arena** (free competitors without earnings)

### Sustainability Verdict: **MODERATE** — One of the more sustainable P2E games because it's genuinely fun and free to play. Earnings are modest but the core game can survive without them.

### Scam Risk: **LOW** — Well-funded ($15M Series A), established since 2018, transparent mechanics.

### CSOAI Applicability: **HIGH** — The card game + ownership model shows that giving players tradeable assets creates engagement. Earnings tied to skill (ranked play) rather than grinding.

## 11. Splinterlands

### Model: Trading Card Game with Multiple Token Types

Splinterlands (originally Steem Monsters) is one of the oldest blockchain games, running on the Hive blockchain. Players battle with NFT cards and earn multiple token types [^616^].

### How Earnings Work

- **$10 entry fee** for Summoner's Book (required to earn) [^616^]
- **SPS (Splintershards)**: Governance token earned through battles, season rewards, and daily quests [^616^]
- **DEC (Dark Energy Crystals)**: In-game currency earned by burning cards [^616^]
- **Credits**: In-game currency for purchases [^616^]
- **Card NFTs**: Own your cards, trade on marketplace
- **Energy system**: 50 matches per energy refill (regenerates hourly) [^616^]
- **Energy limitations**: Prevents unlimited grinding

### What Works

- **Low entry fee**: $10 is accessible
- **Multiple earning streams**: Battles, quests, seasons, tournaments, guild brawls
- **Energy system**: Prevents bot farming and inflation
- **Cross-chain**: Cards bridgeable to Ethereum, BNB Chain, Wax [^616^]
- **Established community**: Operating since 2018

### Limitations

- Requires upfront $10 investment
- SPS and DEC token prices highly volatile
- Earnings depend on card collection value
- Competing in higher leagues requires expensive cards
- Complex economy with multiple tokens may confuse new players

### Sustainability Verdict: **MODERATE** — One of the longest-running P2E games. The energy limitation system is a key sustainability feature that prevents grinding inflation.

### Scam Risk: **LOW** — Established game with years of operation, transparent mechanics.

### CSOAI Applicability: **HIGH** — The energy-based earning system (limited matches per day) is directly applicable to prevent grind-to-earn death spirals.

## 12. Alien Worlds

### Model: DeFi Mining Metaverse with Planetary Governance

Alien Worlds is a free-to-play mining game on the WAX blockchain where players mine Trillium (TLM) and NFTs across seven planets. Each planet operates as a DAO [^614^][^631^].

### How Earnings Work

- **Free to play**: No entry cost, requires WAX wallet [^623^]
- **TLM (Trillium)**: Earned through mining, staking, and governance participation [^614^]
- **NFTs**: Mining tools, land, weapons, and avatars with real value [^614^]
- **Land ownership**: Buy virtual land to earn a share of mining rewards on your plot [^631^]
- **Planetary DAOs**: Stake TLM to vote on reward distribution [^614^]
- **Tool rental system**: New feature allowing passive TLM earnings [^627^]
- **150,000+ unique wallets** have interacted with the game [^632^]

### What Works

- **Free entry** removes all barriers
- **Multiple earning mechanisms**: Mining, land ownership, staking, governance
- **Cross-chain**: Runs on WAX, Ethereum, and BSC [^631^]
- **DAO governance**: Players control reward distribution
- **Tool rental**: New passive income option [^627^]

### Limitations

- **Heavy bot presence**: Free-to-play attracts bot farms
- **Complex economy**: Multiple tokens, planets, and mechanics
- **Limited gameplay depth**: Mining is mostly clicking and waiting
- **TLM token price decline** since peak

### Sustainability Verdict: **MARGINAL** — The most-played blockchain game by wallet count, but much activity may be bots. The free-to-play model attracts users but also exploits.

### Scam Risk: **LOW-MEDIUM** — Legitimate game but bot presence and limited gameplay depth are concerns.

### CSOAI Applicability: **MEDIUM** — The land ownership + mining model creates genuine scarcity. The DAO governance approach gives players control over the economy.

## 13. Thetan Arena

### Model: Free-to-Play MOBA with Optional NFT Premium Heroes

Thetan Arena is a multiplayer online battle arena (MOBA) game where players use free heroes or purchase premium NFT heroes to earn THC and THG tokens [^621^][^629^].

### How Earnings Work

- **Free Heroes**: Anyone can play without investment, earn minimal THC
- **Premium Heroes**: Purchase NFT heroes for enhanced earning potential
- **THC (Thetan Coin)**: Unlimited supply, earned through playing, battles, quests, and ranking [^621^]
- **THG (Thetan Gem)**: Governance token, limited supply of 420M, earned through staking heroes, challenges, governance [^621^]
- **Hero rental system**: Players can rent heroes to others (Axie-style scholarship) [^622^]
- **Free-to-earn path**: Free players accumulate THC slowly, then open Thetan Boxes for premium heroes [^622^]

### What Worked

- **Free entry** attracted 7 million users rapidly [^629^]
- **Optional premium**: Players can progress without spending
- **Hero rental**: Enables scholarship-style earnings
- **Low barrier vs Axie**: Unlike Axie's $500+ entry, Thetan is genuinely free to start
- **MOBA genre**: Proven popular genre (League of Legends, Dota)

### What Failed

- **THG token collapsed**: From $20 ATH to ~$6 within months of launch [^629^]
- **IDO plagued by cyber attacks** [^629^]
- **Daily on-chain users very low**: Only 4,800 daily active wallets vs 7M claimed users [^629^]
- **Most "users" were off-chain**, not actually interacting with blockchain
- **Token heavily allocated to team**: 36% to team, strategic sale, and advisors [^622^]
- **Only 19% allocated to gameplay rewards** [^622^]

### Sustainability Verdict: **MARGINAL** — The gameplay is accessible but the tokenomics heavily favor insiders. The gap between claimed users and on-chain activity is concerning.

### Scam Risk: **MEDIUM** — Legitimate game from established Vietnamese studio, but token distribution heavily favors insiders and the user count discrepancy is suspicious.

### CSOAI Applicability: **MEDIUM** — The genuinely free-to-play approach with optional premium earning is a good model. The hero rental system shows that asset lending can work in games.

## 14. UBI Pilot Programs

### 14.1 Kenya (GiveDirectly) — The Largest UBI Experiment

**The Setup**: 295 villages, 14,474 households randomly assigned to four groups: comparison (no payments), long-term UBI ($0.75/adult/day for 12 years), short-term UBI (2 years), and lump sum (~$500/adult) [^644^]. Delivered via M-PESA mobile money since 2018.

**Key Results** [^638^][^641^][^644^]:
- **All participants reported improved housing and food security**
- **6%+ increase in happiness and life satisfaction**
- **10% reduction in household stress**
- **23% increase in average consumption**
- **Lower domestic violence** in long-term UBI households
- **Better educational outcomes** for primary school children
- **Small business creation and job growth** in recipient communities
- **Lump sum and long-term UBI significantly outperformed short-term UBI** for wealth building

**Critical Insight**: "Policymakers wanting to reduce poverty by creating wealth and independence should rethink using this popular design [short-term UBI], as both a long-term UBI and a large lump sum were found to be significantly more transformative" [^638^].

### 14.2 Finland — The Developed Nation Test

**The Setup**: 2,000 unemployed individuals received EUR 560/month (~$640) for 2 years (2017-2018). Europe's first national government-backed UBI experiment [^672^][^641^].

**Key Results**:
- **Increased well-being and lower stress** compared to control group [^641^]
- **No significant increase in employment rates** overall [^641^]
- **Participants more likely to engage in freelance or entrepreneurial work** [^641^]
- **Mild positive employment effect** for families with children [^672^]
- **Improved mental health, concentration, financial security** [^672^]
- **Public support for UBI increased** as a result [^641^]
- **Freelancers, artists, and entrepreneurs** had the most positive views [^672^]

### 14.3 Stockton, California (SEED)

**The Setup**: 125 residents received $500/month for 24 months (2019-2021). Founded by then-Mayor Michael Tubbs. Private donor-funded [^707^][^716^].

**Key Results** [^712^][^716^][^719^]:
- **Full-time employment rose** among recipients (12% increase in full-time employment year 1)
- **Financial, physical, and emotional health improved**
- **Reduced income volatility**
- **No significant reduction in work incentive** — only 2% were unemployed and not seeking work [^707^]
- **Food security significantly improved**
- **Participants used money for groceries and bills** (most common expenditure) [^707^]
- **Key caveat**: Study impacted by COVID-19 pandemic; some results not statistically significant due to small sample size [^715^]

### Cross-Cutting UBI Insights

| Program | Amount | Duration | Employment Impact | Wellbeing Impact |
|---------|--------|----------|-------------------|------------------|
| Kenya (Long-term) | $0.75/day | 12 years | Positive (business creation) | Strongly positive |
| Kenya (Lump Sum) | ~$500 | One-time | Positive (investment) | Strongly positive |
| Kenya (Short-term) | $0.75/day | 2 years | Minimal | Modestly positive |
| Finland | ~$640/mo | 2 years | None overall | Positive |
| Stockton SEED | $500/mo | 2 years | Positive | Positive |

**Key Lesson for MEOK**: Short-term, conditional payments have limited impact. Long-term or lump-sum payments that give people security to take risks produce the best outcomes. In a gaming context: small daily rewards create habits; meaningful lump-sum rewards create engagement and investment.

### Sustainability Verdict: **PROVEN (as anti-poverty tool)** — UBI demonstrably improves wellbeing. Sustainability depends entirely on funding source (government budgets, philanthropy). The model itself works.

### Scam Risk: **N/A** — Not applicable; these are research programs.

### CSOAI Applicability: **VERY HIGH** — The data shows that unconditional cash improves mental health, productivity, and risk-taking. A game that pays players a "basic income" for participation (funded by data/ads) could have real positive effects on player behavior and engagement.

## 15. Time Banking Systems

### 15.1 Fureai Kippu (Japan)

Japan's pioneering time banking system for elderly care. Participants earn credits by providing care services to seniors, redeemable for their own future care or transferable to family members [^708^][^713^].

**Key Features**:
- Originated in Osaka in 1973 [^717^]
- Over 400 branches operating across Japan [^717^]
- Hybrid model combining time credits with small cash payments [^713^]
- Volunteers motivated by community service, not just credit accumulation (only 9% cited saving for own old age as main motivation) [^713^]
- Combined conventional money + time currency model is most sustainable [^713^]

### 15.2 Ithaca Hours (USA)

Local time-based currency launched in Ithaca, New York in 1991 by Paul Glover. One Ithaca Hour = $10 = one hour of work [^716^][^718^][^723^].

**Key Features**:
- At peak: **500+ businesses** and **1,000+ individuals** accepted Hours [^723^]
- **$100,000+ worth in circulation** at peak [^718^]
- Credit union accepted deposits and provided mortgage payments in Hours [^716^]
- Zero-interest loans available for local businesses [^724^]
- Operated successfully for **20+ years** [^716^]
- Eventually declined due to founder departure and shift to electronic payments [^723^]

### 15.3 TimeRepublik (Global)

Online time banking platform with 100,000+ users worldwide. Users exchange services using TimeCoins earned with 15 minutes of labor [^716^].

### Cross-Cutting Lessons

**What works in time banking**:
- Community rootedness and trust
- A full-time evangelist/networker to promote circulation [^723^]
- Hybrid models (time credits + small cash) [^713^]
- Focus on non-material motivations (community, purpose)

**What fails**:
- Dependence on a single founder/organizer
- Lack of integration with digital payment systems
- Insufficient liquidity (not enough participants offering diverse services)

### Sustainability Verdict: **MODERATE for local systems, LOW for global platforms** — Time banking works at community scale where trust exists. Global online time banks struggle because they lack the social fabric that makes time credits meaningful.

### Scam Risk: **VERY LOW**

### CSOAI Applicability: **HIGH** — The concept of earning "time credits" for in-game contributions that can be redeemed later is directly applicable. A "skill bank" where players teach/mentor others in exchange for credits creates genuine value.

## 16. Citizen Science Payment Models

### Model: Players Contribute to Real Science

Citizen science platforms enlist volunteers to contribute to scientific research. Most operate on volunteer labor, but some compensate participants.

### Key Platforms

**Zooniverse**: The largest citizen science platform. Millions of volunteers classify galaxies, transcribe historical documents, and identify wildlife. Operates on volunteer labor with no monetary compensation [^721^].

**Foldit**: A game where players fold proteins to solve scientific problems. Has led to published research and real scientific discoveries. No monetary compensation — players motivated by competition, purpose, and recognition.

**Eyewire**: Players map neurons in the brain. Volunteer-based with no direct payment.

### The Economics of Unpaid Citizen Science

Research on seven crowd science projects found:
- Total volunteer contributions valued at **$1.55 million** (evaluated at $12/hour undergraduate research assistant rate) in just the first 180 days [^721^]
- Alternative valuation (using Amazon Mechanical Turk piece rates): **$1.54 million** [^721^]
- Average project received $222,068 worth of free labor in 180 days [^721^]
- Zooniverse operating budget is funded through grants [^721^]

**Key Insight**: "Drawing on unpaid volunteers who are driven by intrinsic or social motivations may indeed enable crowd science organizers to perform research that would strain or exceed most budgets if performed on a paid basis" [^721^].

### Sustainability Verdict: **HIGH (as volunteer models)** — Citizen science proves that people will do valuable work for free if they believe in the mission. But paying participants could attract orders of magnitude more contributors.

### Scam Risk: **N/A** — These are legitimate scientific platforms.

### CSOAI Applicability: **VERY HIGH** — The most untapped opportunity. Games could pay players to label data, classify images, transcribe text, or solve puzzles that have real scientific/commercial value. The data created is worth more than the cost of paying players.

## 17. Data Unions & User Data Monetization

### Model: Collective Data Selling

Data Unions are frameworks that allow people to bundle and sell their real-time data collectively, earning revenue when buyers purchase access [^637^].

### Key Platforms

**Swash (Streamr)**: World's first Data Union. Browser extension that captures, anonymizes, and sells browsing data. Users earn tokens for their data. Available for Chrome, Firefox, Brave, and Edge [^637^][^639^].

**How it works**:
- Users install browser extension
- Choose privacy settings and data sharing preferences
- Data is streamed to a Data Union where it's bundled with others' data
- When a buyer purchases access, revenue is distributed equally to all members
- Admin (Swash) takes a small percentage [^639^]
- ~1,000 organic users in first month with zero advertising [^639^]

**Tracey (Streamr)**: App for Filipino fisherfolk that captures seafood supply chain data. Fisherfolk earn tokens for documenting catch data, which is sold to institutions for sustainability monitoring [^637^].

### The Economics

- Individual data has little value; **aggregated data has significant value** [^637^]
- Streamr's Monoplasma tool distributes payments to thousands of data providers via single smart contract [^637^]
- Users maintain control over what data they share
- Revenue depends on buyer demand for specific data types

### Sustainability Verdict: **EARLY STAGE** — The infrastructure exists but buyer demand is still developing. The concept is sound: data is valuable, and people should be paid for it.

### Scam Risk: **LOW** — Transparent systems with user control over data sharing.

### CSOAI Applicability: **VERY HIGH** — This is one of the most promising models. A game could aggregate player data (with consent), bundle it, and sell it to researchers/marketers, sharing revenue with players. The data generated by gameplay (attention patterns, preferences, decisions) has real market value.

## 18. Ocean Protocol

### Model: Decentralized Data Marketplace

Ocean Protocol provides infrastructure for secure data sharing and monetization. Users can publish datasets as "datatokens" that others purchase to access data [^635^][^640^].

### How It Works

- **Datatokens**: Each dataset gets its own ERC-20 token [^636^]
- **Compute-to-Data**: Algorithms run on data without exposing raw data — preserves privacy [^636^]
- **Automated Market Maker (Balancer)**: Provides price discovery for datasets [^640^]
- **OCEAN token**: Used for governance, staking, and marketplace fees [^636^]
- **Data NFTs**: Represent ownership of data assets [^636^]
- Anyone can create a data marketplace on the protocol [^645^]

### Key Applications

- **Healthcare**: AI training on patient data without exposing records [^636^][^643^]
- **Automotive**: Daimler used Ocean for secure data sharing across production hubs [^643^]
- **Used car marketplace**: sgCarMart integrated Ocean for verifiable vehicle history [^643^]
- **Self-driving cars**: Manufacturers share training data without losing IP [^643^]

### Economics

- Founded 2017 by Bruce Pon (BigChainDB) and Trent McConaghy (AI researcher) [^643^]
- Raised $28M+ in funding [^643^]
- ~347 million OCEAN tokens in circulation [^643^]
- Exited Artificial Superintelligence Alliance in October 2025 to focus on independent development [^636^]

### Sustainability Verdict: **MODERATE** — The technology is sound and has real enterprise applications. However, "current industry norms have hindered Ocean from realizing the full benefit of potential network effects" [^635^]. Securing sustained data supply remains a challenge.

### Scam Risk: **LOW** — Established protocol since 2017, real enterprise partnerships, open-source.

### CSOAI Applicability: **VERY HIGH** — Ocean Protocol provides the infrastructure for a game to sell player data to buyers (researchers, AI companies, marketers) while preserving privacy through compute-to-data. Players could earn a share of data sales.

## 19. Streamr

### Model: Decentralized Real-Time Data Network

Streamr is a decentralized P2P network for real-time data transport and monetization. Think "BitTorrent for real-time data streams" [^665^][^668^].

### How Earnings Work

- **Broker nodes**: Run nodes providing bandwidth and storage, earn DATA tokens [^665^]
- **DATA token**: ERC-20 on Ethereum; hard-capped at 2 billion tokens [^668^][^669^]
- **Data Marketplace**: Buy and sell real-time data streams [^668^]
- **Data Unions**: Framework for collective data selling (Swash is the first) [^637^]
- **Delegators**: DATA holders can delegate to brokers to earn yield [^668^]

### Key Statistics

- 30,000+ nodes participated in Brubeck Testnet [^668^]
- Current market cap: ~$836K, FDV: $1.32M [^669^]
- DATA price: $0.00066 (down 94.83% from ATH of $0.37) [^669^]
- 24h volume: ~$26K [^669^]

### What Works

- P2P architecture scales linearly with nodes [^665^]
- Data Unions framework enables collective bargaining for data value [^637^]
- Real enterprise use cases: IoT, DeFi, live video, AI data delivery [^676^]
- Node running is accessible to anyone

### Limitations

- **Token price collapsed**: Down 94%+ from ATH [^669^]
- **Low liquidity**: Very thin trading volume [^669^]
- **Adoption still early**: Enterprise data buyers are slow to adopt decentralized solutions

### Sustainability Verdict: **EARLY STAGE** — Infrastructure works but adoption is slow. The network needs more data buyers to create sustainable demand for DATA tokens.

### Scam Risk: **LOW** — Open-source project, transparent operations, real technology.

### CSOAI Applicability: **HIGH** — Streamr provides the data transport layer. A game could use Streamr to collect and monetize player-generated data streams in real-time.

## 20. DIMO

### Model: Earn for Car Data

DIMO is a DePIN (Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Network) platform that rewards drivers with DIMO crypto tokens for sharing verified vehicle data. Data consumers (automotive companies, insurers, mobility startups) pay to access aggregated vehicle data [^670^].

### How Earnings Work

- **Connect your car**: Via hardware device or software connection
- **Earn DIMO tokens** for sharing verified vehicle data [^670^]
- **Earning factors** [^670^]:
  - Connection streak (regular data sharing)
  - Vehicle type and age (richer data = more earnings)
  - Data quality (verified hardware data earns more than software-only)
  - Network participation (linking vehicle NFT, profile activity)
- **Token utility**: Both reward mechanism and governance token [^670^]

### Key Partnerships

- **Grupo Kaufmann** (June 2025): Latin America's largest automotive dealership network. Deploying DIMO LTE devices across 6 countries for real-time diagnostics and loyalty programs [^674^]
- 1.7 million connected cars projected to be sold in Latin America in 2024 [^674^]

### Sustainability Verdict: **PROMISING** — The DePIN model connects real-world data generation with real buyers (insurers, automakers). As the connected vehicle market grows, demand for vehicle data increases.

### Scam Risk: **LOW** — Real hardware, real partnerships, real data buyers.

### CSOAI Applicability: **HIGH** — The DePIN model (earn for providing real-world data) is directly applicable. A game could track player movement, activity, or behavior and monetize that data, sharing revenue with players.

## 21. 2025-2026 P2E Landscape: New Models

### Key Industry Trends

**93% of Web3 games launched 2020-2026 collapsed**, with $12-15 billion written off [^666^]. The industry is evolving rapidly:

### 21.1 Play-and-Own (Not Play-to-Earn)

The dominant trend is shifting from "earn" to "own" — players own assets with utility rather than grinding for inflationary tokens [^666^][^678^].

**MapleStory Universe** (Case Study):
- **3.82 million registered accounts**, 850,000 engaged wallets in first year [^666^]
- **$46M marketplace transaction volume**
- **Dual-token model**: NXPC consumed in-game exceeds NXPC issued (Q1 2026) — first time utility exceeded rewards [^666^]
- 8 million NXPC permanently burned (deflationary pressure)
- Built on MapleStory franchise equity — fun first, blockchain second

**Off The Grid** (Case Study):
- **740 million transactions, 14 million wallets** [^666^]
- Optional blockchain — players can play without crypto
- Game topped charts BEFORE token launch
- "Build a great game first. Add ownership second." [^666^]

### 21.2 Key Sustainability Principles for 2026

1. **Gameplay first, blockchain second**: No tokenomics model can sustain a game that isn't fun [^678^]
2. **Dual-token models work**: When emissions are balanced by burns and in-game utility [^666^]
3. **Retention targets**: 35-45% D1, 15-25% D7, 5-10% D30 retention required for sustainable economics [^666^]
4. **Optional blockchain**: Converting engaged players into traders without alienating mainstream gamers [^666^]
5. **Deflationary mechanics**: Crafting, upgrades, seasonal content must burn tokens at emission rate or higher [^678^]
6. **Player-generated economies**: Studios should not sell NFTs directly [^666^]

### 21.3 Infrastructure Evolution

- **Immutable and Polygon** dominate blockchain gaming infrastructure [^666^]
- **Layer 2 networks** reduce minting costs and improve scalability [^666^]
- **Mobile blockchain gaming** surging in India, Southeast Asia, Latin America [^673^]
- **Major studios entering**: Ubisoft, Square Enix, EA exploring blockchain gaming [^673^]
- **AI integration**: For economy balancing, anti-cheat, and game design [^673^]

### 21.4 Emerging Earn Models

| Model | Example | Key Innovation |
|-------|---------|---------------|
| Play-and-Own | MapleStory Universe | Own assets, not inflationary tokens |
| Optional Blockchain | Off The Grid, Mythical Games | Web2 game first, Web3 ownership optional |
| Data Monetization | DIMO, Streamr Swash | Earn for real-world data contributions |
| Attention Economy | Brave Browser | 70% ad revenue to users |
| DePIN | Helium, DIMO | Earn for providing infrastructure |
| Fantasy Sports NFT | Sorare | Licensed IP + skill-based competitions |

### CSOAI Applicability: **VERY HIGH** — The industry is converging on sustainable models. The key insight for MEOK: build a genuinely fun game first, then add optional earning through (1) data monetization revenue sharing, (2) brand-sponsored rewards, (3) player-owned asset trading.

## 23. Recommendations for MEOK

### The Optimal Model: Hybrid Revenue-Share

Based on this research, MEOK should build a **multi-revenue-player-earning model**:

```
Player Earnings Funded By:
├── Brand Advertising Revenue (40%) — Daily quests, rewarded videos
├── Data Monetization Revenue (30%) — Aggregated, anonymized gameplay data
├── Premium Subscriptions (15%) — Boosted earning, exclusive features  
├── Tournament/Competition Entry Fees (10%) — Skill-based prize pools
└── Marketplace Transaction Fees (5%) — Player-to-player asset trading
```

### Critical Success Factors

1. **Build a fun game first** — No tokenomics can save a boring game [^678^]
2. **Keep entry free** — Zero barrier to maximize adoption
3. **External revenue only** — Never fund player earnings from token inflation
4. **Burn mechanics** — Every reward token must have multiple sinks
5. **Skill-based earnings** — Better players earn more, not just grinders
6. **Daily limits** — Cap daily earnings to prevent inflation
7. **Hybrid on/off-chain** — Keep gameplay off-chain for speed; assets on-chain for ownership
8. **Transparent revenue** — Publish advertiser/data revenue like Brave does [^572^]

### What to Avoid

1. **Token printing without sinks** — Death spiral guaranteed
2. **High entry costs** — Blocks adoption, creates Ponzi dynamics
3. **Pure grinding rewards** — Attracts bots, kills gameplay
4. **MLM referral structures** — Regulatory risk, unsustainable growth
5. **Overpromising earnings** — Creates scam perception when yields drop
6. **Uncapped daily earnings** — Inflationary, exploitable by bots

### Revenue Projections (Conservative)

| Revenue Source | Assumptions | Monthly Revenue at 100K Players | Monthly Revenue at 1M Players |
|---------------|-------------|--------------------------------|------------------------------|
| Brand ads | $0.10/player/day | $300K | $3M |
| Data monetization | $0.05/player/day | $150K | $1.5M |
| Premium subs ($5/mo, 5% conversion) | 5,000 subs | $25K | $250K |
| Tournament fees | $1/entry, 10% weekly | $40K | $400K |
| Marketplace fees (2.5%) | $1M volume | $25K | $250K |
| **Total** | | **$540K** | **$5.4M** |

### Player Earning Potential

| Activity | Earnings (Conservative) | Frequency |
|----------|------------------------|-----------|
| Daily login + ad view | $0.10-0.50 | Daily |
| Brand-sponsored quest completion | $0.50-2.00 | Weekly |
| Data sharing reward | $0.05-0.20 | Daily |
| Tournament winnings | $1-100 | Weekly |
| Premium subscriber bonus | 2-5x base earnings | Daily |
| Referral bonus | $1-5 per active referral | One-time |

**Total realistic monthly earnings for active player: $5-50/month**

This is modest but sustainable. Players in developing countries (Philippines, Venezuela, Southeast Asia) have shown willingness to play for $2-20/day. A sustainable $5-50/month with genuine gameplay fun and no entry cost can attract millions of players.
