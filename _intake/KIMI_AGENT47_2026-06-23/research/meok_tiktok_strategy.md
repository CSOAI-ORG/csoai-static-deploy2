## PART 1: TIKTOK ALGORITHM DEEP DIVE (2026)

### 1.1 How the Algorithm Works in 2026

TikTok uses an **interest graph, not a social graph** - it recommends content based on what you'll enjoy, not who you follow [^230^]. This is the most democratic algorithm in social media - a zero-follower account can get 10 million views.

#### Three Ranking Signal Categories:

| Signal Category | Weight | Examples |
|----------------|--------|----------|
| **User Interactions** | ~40-50% | Watch time, completion rate, shares, saves, comments |
| **Video Information** | Medium | Captions, hashtags, sounds, effects, on-screen text |
| **User/Account Settings** | Low | Device type, language, location |

**Source:** TikTok Official Documentation, Hootsuite 2026 Algorithm Guide [^229^], PostEverywhere [^230^]

#### The Phased Distribution System (2026 Update):

1. **Phase 1 - Follower-First Testing** (NEW): Videos shown to existing followers first
2. **Phase 2 - Audience Expansion**: If it performs well, reaches progressively larger non-follower waves
3. **Phase 3 - Virality Stage**: Engagement snowballs, each wave triggers wider distribution
4. **Phase 4 - Plateau/Decline**: Distribution slows as algorithm exhausts relevant audiences

**Key Insight for Nick:** With 16K followers, Phase 1 performance is CRITICAL. Nick needs to train his audience to engage immediately to trigger Phase 2 expansion. [^230^]

### 1.2 What Changed in 2026 (Critical for Strategy)

| Change | Impact | Action Required |
|--------|--------|-----------------|
| **Completion rate bar raised to ~70%** | Videos need higher retention to go viral | Create tighter, more engaging content |
| **Follower-first testing** | Existing followers see content first | Prioritize audience engagement training |
| **Shares/saves > likes** | Deep engagement matters more than surface engagement | Create content worth sharing/saving |
| **Niche consistency matters more** | Posting across 3+ unrelated topics = 45% lower reach | Stay focused on AI/gaming/simulation niche |
| **Quality over quantity** | One engaging video/week outperforms 7 mediocre daily posts | Focus on high-production value per video |
| **AI-generated content down-ranked** | Authentic human creators prioritized | Show real gameplay, real reactions, real Nick |
| **Original audio rewarded** | TikTok rewards original sounds over borrowed | Create signature sounds/hooks |
| **TikTok as search engine** | 40% of Gen Z prefer TikTok over Google for search | Use SEO-friendly captions and keywords |

**Source:** PostEverywhere Algorithm Guide [^230^], MiraFlow [^232^], SocialRescue [^235^]

### 1.3 The Engagement Hierarchy (Most to Least Weighted)

1. **Shares** - Content valuable enough to pass along (highest signal)
2. **Video completion rate** - Compelling content throughout (~70% threshold)
3. **Comments** - Conversation-worthy content (quality > quantity)
4. **Profile visits after viewing** - Creator interest indicator
5. **Likes** - Basic positive response (declining weight in 2026)
6. **Views** - Simple exposure

**Source:** Team5pm Research [^203^], PostEverywhere [^230^]
