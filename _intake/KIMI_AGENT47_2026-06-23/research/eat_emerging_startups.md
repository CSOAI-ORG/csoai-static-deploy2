# AI Governance & Compliance Startup Landscape 2024-2026
## Comprehensive Emerging Threat Assessment for CSOAI

**Research Date**: July 2026
**Total Startups Identified**: 80+
**Funding Range**: $0 (stealth) → $132M raised
**Sources**: 25+ web searches across Crunchbase, YC, Techstars, Product Hunt, GitHub, venture publications

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The AI governance/compliance market has exploded from $308M (2025) to a projected $418M+ (2026) at 35%+ CAGR. **80+ startups** were identified across six categories. The most significant competitive threats to CSOAI come from:

1. **Well-funded governance platforms**: Credo AI ($42M), Noma Security ($132M), WitnessAI ($58M), Geordie AI ($36.5M)
2. **YC-backed compliance automation**: Confident AI, Alter, Galini, Cardamon, Ritivel, Luthor/Agency
3. **EU AI Act specialists**: Modulos (CHF 8.7M), Calvin Risk ($6M), Saidot (EUR 1.75M)
4. **Agentic AI governance**: JetStream ($34M), Lumia ($18M), Vijil ($23M), Scorecard ($4M)
