## 2. TALENT MOVES

### Noam Shazeer: Google -> OpenAI (June 18, 2026)

**The biggest AI talent move of 2026.**

- **Who:** Noam Shazeer, VP of Engineering at Google, co-lead of Gemini AI models
- **What:** Joins OpenAI as **Lead for Architecture Research**
- **Significance:** Co-authored the 2017 "Attention Is All You Need" Transformer paper - the technical foundation for every major LLM (GPT, Gemini, Claude, Grok, Llama)
- **Also co-authored:** Sparsely-Gated Mixture of Experts (2016) - architecture used in GPT-4, Gemini, Mistral
- **Also designed:** Multi-Query Attention - inference efficiency technique that made fast inference at scale economically viable
- **History:** Google paid ~$2.7 billion in 2024 to bring him back from Character.AI. He lasted less than 2 years.
- **Sam Altman's response:** "noam is one of the people I have most wanted to work with since the very beginning of openai. only took 10 years. i think it will be worth the wait!"
- **Source:** CNBC, TechTimes, Neowin (June 18, 2026)

**What it means for CSOAI:** OpenAI is now positioned to potentially leapfrog competitors in next-generation model architecture (GPT-6 family). Shazeer's expertise in sparse MoE and attention mechanisms could give OpenAI a structural advantage that lasts years. For any company building on OpenAI's stack, this is bullish. For competitors, the talent war just got more intense.

### The Real Trigger (per WIRED and Washington Post)

1. **Step 1:** SK Telecom (South Korea's largest wireless carrier, $100M Anthropic investor) identified by White House as Chinese security risk with access to Mythos 5
2. **Step 2:** Amazon researchers flagged separate Fable 5 vulnerabilities and reported them
3. **Escalation:** Intervention expanded from "revoke SK Telecom access" to "block all foreign nationals globally"

### ID Verification as Restoration Path

Anthropic updated its privacy policy (effective July 8, 2026) to collect:
- Government-issued ID documents
- Biometric data including facial geometry
- Verification results

This enables a **US-citizens-only restoration** without fully lifting the export control directive. Uses Peter Thiel-backed **Persona** for verification.

**What it means for CSOAI:** The Fable 5 crisis is the most significant AI governance event of 2026. It establishes precedents for: (1) government pre-release access to frontier models, (2) export controls on AI as dual-use technology, (3) identity verification as a condition of AI access. For enterprises dependent on Claude, multi-provider architecture is now essential. For the industry, this accelerates the shift toward sovereign/national AI strategies.
