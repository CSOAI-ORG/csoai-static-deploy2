## 1. Executive Summary

The in-game advertising market represents one of the fastest-growing segments of digital marketing, valued at **$9.4-11.8 billion in 2025** and projected to reach **$17-22 billion by 2030-2034** [^625^][^627^]. Multiple proven revenue models exist for brands to advertise inside games, ranging from immersive brand worlds on Roblox to dynamic in-game billboards, virtual concerts, product placement, rewarded video ads, and sponsored physical locations.

**Key findings for MEOK:**

| Model | Revenue Potential | Player Reception | Complexity |
|-------|------------------|------------------|------------|
| Roblox Brand Worlds | $1M-$20M+ per activation | Positive if well-designed | High |
| Fortnite Events | $10M-$50M+ revenue | Very positive | Very High |
| In-Game Billboards (Dynamic) | $4-$18 CPM | Neutral/Positive | Medium |
| Rewarded Video Ads | $10-$50 eCPM | Positive (opt-in) | Low |
| Playable Ads | $20-$100+ eCPM | Positive (interactive) | Medium |
| Branded Virtual Items | Millions in sales | Positive | Medium |
| Sponsored Locations | $0.15-$0.50/visit | Positive | Medium |
| Virtual Concerts | $20M+ per event | Very positive | Very High |
| Advergames | Varies widely | Depends on quality | High |

## 2. Roblox Brand Partnerships

### 2.1 Overview

Roblox has emerged as the premier platform for brand activations in the metaverse, with **65+ million daily active users** and a thriving virtual economy. In Q3 2023 alone, Roblox earned **$839 million in revenue** [^508^]. The platform has evolved from requiring brands to build full virtual experiences to offering a self-serve advertising platform with billboard-style ads, video ads, and contextual targeting [^647^][^650^].

### 2.2 Key Brand Activations

#### Nike / Nikeland
- **Launch:** November 2021
- **Visitors:** 7 million in first two months; **21 million total visits** to date [^597^]
- **Favorites:** ~118,000 gamers
- **Revenue Impact:** Nike's digital results (partially driven by metaverse experiences) now represent **26% of total Nike brand revenue** [^597^]
- **Approach:** Built with acquired metaverse agency RTKFT; allowed users to buy virtual goods; gaming experience that was Nike-branded

#### Gucci
- **Gucci Garden (May 2021):** Immersive multimedia experience celebrating Gucci's 100th anniversary [^705^]
  - **20 million users visited** during the two-week exhibition [^708^][^712^]
  - A virtual Gucci Dionysus handbag resold for **$5,460** -- nearly $1,000 more than the physical bag [^712^]
  - Hundreds of thousands of users acquired multiple virtual items [^708^]
- **Gucci Town (May 2022):** Persistent presence on Roblox following Gucci Garden success [^514^]

#### Vans World
- **Launch:** September 2021
- **Visitors:** **100 million visits** on Roblox as of July 2023 [^706^]
- **Engagement:** 8 million+ hours logged
- **Approach:** Skateboarding experience with avatar customization, virtual skate spots, interactive challenges
- **Collaborations:** Gucci, The North Face, Timbaland, Stranger Things
- **Key Success Factor:** Prioritized fun gamification, customization, and brand integrity [^706^]

#### Chipotle
- **Halloween 2021 Campaign:** Virtual restaurant experience with free burrito codes
  - First 30,000 people received free real-world burritos
  - Record-level app downloads and loyalty program sign-ups
- **Burrito Builder (April 2022):** 
  - **25.6 million lifetime visits**
  - Average session length: 5.82 minutes
  - Players earned "Burrito Bucks" redeemable for real-world discounts [^623^]

#### Other Notable Activations

| Brand | Activation | Year | Key Metric |
|-------|-----------|------|------------|
| Walmart | Walmartland + Universe of Play | 2022 | Two activations |
| Forever 21 | Shop City with Barbie collection | 2022 | Virtual + physical sales |
| Invisalign | Interactive dentist office in Livetopia | 2022 | Mini-games for engagement |
| Hasbro | My Little Pony - Maretime Bay | 2022 | Major IP activation |
| Tommy Hilfiger | Tommy Play persistent world | 2023 | Frequent content updates |
| PACWORLD | PacSun activation | 2022 | Mall Rats NFT series tie-in |

### 2.3 Revenue Model

- **Platform Revenue Share:** Roblox takes 30-70% of in-game item sales [^518^]
- **Forever 21 Example:** Sold millions of virtual black beanies at $0.50-$0.70 each [^518^]
- **Advertising:** Roblox's self-serve platform sells billboard and video ads through open auction; ads only served to users 13+ (57% of audience, ~39.9M DAU) [^650^]
- **Commerce Pipeline:** 84% of Gen-Z Roblox users are somewhat likely to consider buying from a brand they've seen/worn in Roblox [^508^]
- **Monthly Avatar Budget:** 52% of Gen-Z users comfortable budgeting up to **$10/month** to style their avatar [^508^]
- **Roblox Q3 2023 Revenue:** $839 million [^508^]

### 2.4 Player Reception

- **Success Factors:** Evergreen experiences with ongoing content updates (Gucci Town, Vans World, Nikeland) keep players returning [^597^]
- **Failure Pattern:** Chipotle's daily visits dropped to ~4K/day post-launch despite 25.6M lifetime visits -- brands need game mechanics and regular updates to maintain engagement [^623^]
- **Key Insight:** Experiences that prioritize social spaces, interactivity, and authentic brand expression perform best

### 2.5 Technical Implementation

- Options range from full custom experiences (high investment) to buying billboard/video ads through self-serve platform (lower barrier)
- Roblox's new contextual and age-based targeting allows genre-based ad placement (sports, action, RPG, fashion) [^650^]
- Video ads: up to 30 seconds, muted by default, can be rewarded
- Partnership with PubMatic enables programmatic video inventory purchasing [^647^]

- Integration of custom avatars, physics disruptions, alternate worlds
- In-game item shop for associated merchandise

## 4. In-Game Billboards & Dynamic Advertising

### 4.1 Overview

In-game billboard advertising spans from static product placement (embedded in game code) to dynamic ads (served programmatically and changeable in real-time). Dynamic in-game advertising held **52.8% market share** of in-game ad formats in 2025 [^645^].

### 4.2 Key Platforms

#### Anzu.io
- **Technology:** Intrinsic in-game advertising platform blending ads into game environments
- **Valuation:** $1.1 billion (as of June 2025) [^646^]
- **Annual Revenue:** $15 million (estimated, June 2025) [^646^]
- **Total Funding:** $83.5 million (Series B: $48M in June 2023) [^646^]
- **Key Metrics:**
  - Viewability rates: **98%** (vs. 78% digital display norm) [^646^]
  - Brand recall: **49%** of players show prompted brand recall [^646^]
  - IVT (fraud): 0.16% mobile, 0.47% PC (vs. 6% industry average) [^711^]
- **Partners:** WPP, NBCUniversal, PayPal Ventures, Amex Ventures [^646^]
- **Measurement:** Integrates with DoubleVerify, IAS, Nielsen [^648^]
- **Launch:** Click-enabled intrinsic in-game ad formats (February 2026) -- boosting fill rates by up to 50% [^645^]

#### Bidstack
- **Focus:** Dynamic in-game advertising in premium sports and racing simulation
- **Deep Integrations:** Codemasters racing titles, Football Manager
- **Case Study - Penguin Random House (Football Manager 2019):**
  - Won "Guerilla Marketing Campaign of the Year" at Book Marketing Society Awards
  - Targeted male demographic
  - Book became Sunday Times Bestseller [^651^]
- **Quote:** "Running a Football Manager ad is cheaper than running an in-stadium ad in the real world, but you can still get the same brand uplift" -- James Draper, Bidstack founder [^651^]

#### Other Platforms
- **AdInMo:** Programmatic and contextual brand integration
- **Frameplay:** Non-disruptive in-game advertising
- **Gadsme:** In-game advertising technology

### 4.3 Revenue Model

| Ad Format | CPM/eCPM | Best For |
|-----------|----------|----------|
| Dynamic Billboards | $4-$18 CPM | Sports, racing, urban games |
| Intrinsic Display Ads | $12-$18 CPM (premium sports) | AAA titles |
| Programmatic Intrinsic | Variable; outcome-based pricing emerging | All game types |

### 4.4 Player Reception

- **GTA / NBA 2K Context:** Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick noted NBA 2K has in-game ads because it replicates the NBA experience where "ads are everywhere in stadiums" -- but "that's not a big economic contributor" [^512^]
- **Full-Price Game Concern:** Zelnick stated in-game ads in $70-80 games "would seem unfair" [^512^]
- **EA Sports UFC 4 Backlash (2020):** Full-screen ad placements led to angry backlash; EA reconsidered [^512^]
- **Key Principle:** Ads must fit the game context -- players accept ads in sports stadiums and urban environments because they add realism

### 4.5 Technical Implementation

- **SDK Integration:** Game developers embed ad platform SDK for native ad placement
- **Dynamic Insertion:** Server-side ad serving enables real-time creative changes without game patches
- **3D Viewability Tracking:** Patented 3D ad tracking engines measure if ads are actually seen in complex environments [^648^]
- **Contextual Targeting:** Genre, gameplay style, and environment-based ad matching
- **Privacy-First:** Supports universal IDs (ID5, RampID) to counter cookie deprecation [^646^]

## 5. Product Placement in Games

### 5.1 Overview

Product placement in games mirrors film/TV techniques but adds the dimension of interactivity. The principal advantages are visibility and notoriety -- a single in-game ad may be encountered multiple times [^511^].

### 5.2 Successful Examples

#### Mercedes-Benz in Mario Kart 8
- Players can choose Mercedes-Benz SUV as racing vehicle -- product is gameplay tool [^510^]

#### Tony Hawk's Pro Skater Series
- "Every skateboard wheel, truck and deck aped a real-world brand and model" [^510^]
- Brands inseparable from gameplay

#### Pizza Hut & KFC
- Classic examples of product placement in video games [^507^]

#### Nike in Fortnite (Travis Scott Event)
- Travis Scott's avatar wore Air Jordan sneakers
- Estimated Nike brand exposure value: **$518,000** minimum [^506^]

#### FIFA / EA Sports Series
- Adidas, Panasonic billboards since 1994 FIFA International Soccer [^511^]
- Bidstack enables in-stadium native advertising [^510^]

### 5.3 Revenue Model

- Typically negotiated placement deals between brands and publishers
- Can range from $50,000 to $350,000 for a performance in a video game (artist fee range) [^506^]
- Travis Scott potentially commanded **$500,000** fee for Astronomical [^506^]
- Automotive brands often pay for vehicles to be featured with no crash damage (reputation protection) [^510^]

### 5.4 Player Reception

- Generally positive when product placement is seamless and enhances realism
- Negative when products feel forced or interrupt gameplay
- Players enjoy using real-world branded items as gameplay elements

### 5.5 Technical Implementation

- **Static:** Embedded in game code during development (1 month to years of lead time)
- **Dynamic:** Server-side ad serving enables post-launch campaign management
- **Integration Depth:** Range from background billboards to core gameplay items

## 6. Advergames

### 6.1 Overview

Advergames are custom-built video games with the primary purpose of promoting a brand or product. The gaming industry generates **$183.9 billion annually** with 3.4 billion players globally, yet in-game advertising spending remains surprisingly small (3.7% of US digital ad spend) [^571^].

### 6.2 Historical Examples

#### Classic Successes
- **Burger King's "Sneak King" / "Pocket Bike Racer":** Released on Xbox; prioritized gameplay and user experience [^571^]
- **Doritos "Crash Course":** Built from scratch; highly playable [^571^]
- **Domino's "Avoid the Noid":** One of the first brand-tie-in games [^571^]
- **7-Up "Cool Spot":** Full branded entity
- **US Army Recruitment Games:** Government use of advergaming

#### 21st Century Evolution
- **Wendy's x Fortnite:** "Super Wendy's World" -- 119% increase in brand mentions, 1.5M minutes watched on Twitch [^571^]
- **Modern approach:** Branded partnerships on Roblox, Decentraland, Fortnite [^571^]

### 6.3 Revenue Model

- Development costs vary widely; spiraling costs make it "risky business" for brands [^571^]
- ROI measured through brand awareness, social media mentions, and engagement
- US advertisers forecast to spend **$6.7 billion** on in-game ads [^571^]
- **IAB Survey:** Nearly 90% of advertisers agree gaming is a brand-safe channel [^571^]

### 6.4 Player Reception

- Successful when gameplay quality is prioritized over advertising
- Failures result from "rushed production, glitchy gameplay and blatant over-advertising" [^571^]
- Key insight: "By meeting players within an existing game space, brands move closer to answering the question: 'How do we get people to play the game we've built?'" [^571^]

### 6.5 Technical Implementation

- Options range from full custom game development (high cost, high risk) to building within existing platforms (Roblox, Fortnite Creative)
- B2B gamification trend: Smartphones make mobile branded games more accessible [^591^]

## 7. Rewarded Video Ads

### 7.1 Overview

Rewarded video ads give players in-game rewards (extra lives, coins, currency) in exchange for watching short videos. They are the **most profitable mobile game ad format**, considered the gold standard for player-friendly monetization [^520^].

### 7.2 Revenue Metrics

| Metric | Value | Source |
|--------|-------|--------|
| eCPM Range | **$10-$50** per 1,000 views | [^520^][^515^] |
| Average eCPM (US) | ~$30 | [^520^] |
| 30-Second Spot Value | $0.01-$0.05 per view | [^515^] |
| Android eCPM (US) | $10.11 | [^520^] |
| iOS eCPM (US) | $9.64 | [^520^] |

### 7.3 Revenue Scenarios

**Scenario 1: Casual Game (30K DAU)**
- 2 sessions/day, 3 banner impressions, 1 interstitial
- Banner revenue: ~$90/day
- Interstitial revenue: ~$800/day
- **Total: ~$890/day** [^520^]

**Scenario 2: RPG Game (200K DAU)**
- 2 rewarded videos/user/day at $30 eCPM
- **Total: ~$12,000/day** from rewarded video alone [^520^]

**Scenario 3: Small Game (1K-2K DAU)**
- Well-optimized: **$50-$200/day** in ad revenue [^515^]

### 7.4 Player Reception

- **Most player-friendly format** -- voluntary, opt-in nature
- Users decide to watch; can interrupt if desired (but forfeit reward)
- Best for games with virtual economies or upgrade systems [^515^]
- Improves player retention by 15%+ in certain genres [^624^]

### 7.5 Technical Implementation

- Simple SDK integration (AdMob, Unity Ads, AppLovin, ironSource)
- Timing at logical pause points (between levels, after failure)
- Reward must feel valuable to player
- Typically 15-30 second videos

## 8. Playable Ads

### 8.1 Overview

Playable ads are interactive mini-games that serve as demos of the advertised product. They deliver some of the highest conversion and payout rates in mobile advertising [^515^].

### 8.2 Revenue Metrics

| Metric | Value | Source |
|--------|-------|--------|
| eCPM Range | **$20-$100+** per 1,000 impressions | [^520^][^515^] |
| Conversion Rate Boost | **319% higher** than traditional ads | [^522^] |
| Average eCPM | $60 (market benchmark) | [^520^] |

### 8.3 Success Stories

- **Royal Match:** Simple match-3 playable ad; 12M+ iOS downloads, **$125M+ revenue** [^525^]
- **Blackout Bingo:** Fast-paced playable; 100K+ Android downloads [^525^]
- **War Machines:** "Aim & Shoot" simplified playable; 100M+ total downloads [^525^]

### 8.4 Player Reception

- High engagement due to interactive nature
- Players get "demo" experience before downloading
- Best suited for apps with active, curious players
- Must showcase unique gameplay in first 10 seconds [^522^]

### 8.5 Technical Implementation

- No-code solutions available for quick creation
- Typically start with short video, followed by interactive game
- Key elements: engaging visuals, simple mechanics, clear CTA
- Can be built with playable ad makers (Playable Factory, etc.)

## 10. Branded Virtual Items

### 10.1 Overview

Branded virtual items (skins, clothing, accessories, emotes) represent one of the highest-ROI in-game advertising models. Fortnite's skin-based economy and Roblox's Avatar Marketplace have proven that players will pay real money for branded digital goods.

### 10.2 Key Data Points

- **Roblox Avatar Marketplace (2021):** 5.8 billion virtual items acquired (free and paid) [^712^]
- **Fortnite Item Shop:** Skins sold for 800, 1,200, 1,500, or 2,000 V-Bucks [^524^]
- **Forever 21:** Sold millions of virtual black beanies on Roblox at $0.50-$0.70 each [^518^]
- **Gucci Dionysus Bag:** Resold for $5,460 on Roblox (vs. ~$4,500 physical) [^712^]
- **Morgan Stanley Prediction:** Digital fashion could increase industry sales by **$50 billion by 2030** [^710^]
- **25%** of all in-experience dollars on Roblox spent on items [^518^]

### 10.3 Revenue Model

- Direct purchase using in-game currency (V-Bucks, Robux, etc.)
- Limited-time drops create scarcity and urgency
- Cross-promotion with real-world product launches
- Revenue split: Platform takes 30-70% (Roblox); 100% to creators through end of 2026 (Fortnite) [^518^]

### 10.4 Player Reception

- Very positive when items enhance self-expression
- Players actively seek out branded items for avatar customization
- 84% of Gen-Z Roblox users likely to consider buying from brands they've "worn" in-game [^508^]
- Limited editions can command premium prices (up to $10,000 for community-created "Limiteds") [^508^]

### 10.5 Technical Implementation

- 3D asset creation matching platform's avatar system
- Integration with platform's item shop/economy
- Support for wearables, emotes, gear, accessories
- Optional: tie to real-world physical item (twinning) [^508^]

## 11. Sponsored Locations in Games

### 11.1 Overview

Sponsored locations turn real-world business locations into in-game destinations that drive physical foot traffic. Pokemon GO pioneered this model, which has since been replicated by other location-based games.

### 11.2 Pokemon GO Model

#### Revenue Structure
- **Cost Per Visit (CPV):** Partners spend less than **$0.50 per daily unique visit** [^527^]
- At peak: McDonald's Japan (3,000 stores) could have paid up to **$900,000/day** [^527^]
- **Total Sponsored Visitors:** 500 million driven to sponsored locations [^527^]
- **Estimated CPV Revenue:** $75-$250 million for Niantic [^519^]
- **Lifetime Revenue:** $8 billion+ since 2016; $544 million in 2024 alone [^516^]
- **In-App Purchases:** 70-80% of total earnings [^516^]

#### Sponsors
- McDonald's (3,000+ stores in Japan alone)
- Starbucks (7,800 U.S. locations)
- Sprint
- 7-Eleven Mexico (1,800+ locations)
- GameStop, Toys R Us, EB Games

#### SMB Program
- **Standard PokéStop:** $30/month [^516^]
- **Premium Gym:** $60/month with one-hour raid scheduling [^526^]

### 11.3 Revenue Model

| Tier | Price | Features |
|------|-------|----------|
| Standard | $30/month | PokéStop placement |
| Premium | $60/month | Gym + monthly raid scheduling |
| Enterprise | Custom | Major brand partnerships |

### 11.4 Player Reception

- Positive -- sponsored locations add gameplay value (EX Raid access, special items)
- Players appreciate the real-world exploration incentive
- Integration is seamless -- part of core gameplay loop

### 11.5 Technical Implementation

- Requires location-based game infrastructure (GPS, AR)
- Partnership API for business owners to claim locations
- Geofencing to verify visits and deliver rewards
- Lure Module system for businesses to attract players

## 15. IAB In-Game Advertising Standards

### 15.1 Overview

The Interactive Advertising Bureau (IAB) and Media Rating Council (MRC) have established formal guidelines for in-game advertising measurement, brand safety, and creative standards.

### 15.2 IAB Creative Guidelines (March 2024)

Published as "Creative Guidelines and Best Practices in Advertising in Gaming" [^713^]:

#### Key Standards:
- **Audio Ads:** Optimal length 15 seconds, clear disclosure and CTA, placement/frequency caps [^713^]
- **Interactive Overlays:** 15-60 seconds, pair with chat CTAs, avoid stream obstruction [^713^]
- **Intrinsic/Native Ads:** Display and short video under 6 seconds, seamless integration, minimal elements, simple messaging [^713^]
- **Rich Media/Playable Ads:** Must allow exit within 30 seconds, prioritize player experience [^713^]

#### Ethical Requirements:
- User privacy and data protection
- Age-appropriate advertising
- Avoid stereotypes and offensive content
- Transparent sponsorships
- Sensitivity to current events [^713^]

### 15.3 IAB/MRC Measurement Guidelines

- **Intrinsic In-Game Advertising (IIG) Measurement Guidelines 2.0** [^714^]
- Count impressions after ads are loaded and begin rendering
- Viewability measured based on time and pixel thresholds in 3D environments
- Event-dependent impressions require user interaction measurement
- Advergame measurement restricted to opened/initialized applications [^714^]

### 15.4 Brand Safety Standards

- ESRB/PEGI content rating include/exclude lists
- COPPA filter for children-directed content
- IAB Spider & Bot list with double-pass filtering
- 100% App-Ads.txt covered publishers
- TCF 2.2 Verified
- TAG Brand Safety & Fraud Certification [^711^]

### 15.5 Measurement Partners

- **DoubleVerify, IAS, Moat/Oracle:** Viewability verification
- **HUMAN, Comscore:** Fraud detection
- **IAS, Pixalate:** Invalid traffic monitoring
- Certified viewable in-game impressions achieve **79% viewability** (vs. 56% web display) [^648^]

   - CPM: $4-$18
   - Adds realism to game environments

   - Best for: Sports arenas, urban environments, racing tracks

   - Direct revenue from item sales
   - High player demand for self-expression
   - Revenue share with brand partners
   - Best for: Clothing, accessories, vehicles, skins

### 18.2 Tier 2: Medium Investment (Higher Returns)

4. **Playable Ads**
   - eCPM: $20-$100+
   - 319% higher conversion than traditional ads
   - Interactive mini-games showcasing MEOK or partner content
   - Best for: User acquisition campaigns, partner promotions

5. **Sponsored Locations / Events**
   - Drives both engagement and real-world value
   - Location-based brand partnerships
   - Virtual + physical integration
   - Best for: Retail partners, restaurants, entertainment brands

6. **Virtual Influencer Partnerships**
   - 11x ROI potential
   - Authentic engagement with Gen Z
   - Cross-platform amplification
   - Best for: Brand awareness, community building

### 18.3 Tier 3: High Investment (Maximum Impact)

7. **Brand-Owned Virtual Worlds (on Roblox/Fortnite)**
   - 10M-100M+ visit potential
   - Ongoing content investment required
   - Full brand control over experience
   - Best for: Major brand partnerships, flagship activations

   - $20M+ revenue potential per event
   - Cultural moment creation
   - Requires significant technical infrastructure
   - Best for: Music labels, entertainment brands, major milestones

9. **Full Advergames**
   - Complete branded game experiences
   - Highest creative control
   - Significant development investment
   - Best for: Major product launches, long-term brand building

### 18.5 Key Success Factors

1. **Player Experience First:** Any advertising must enhance, not disrupt, the core game experience
