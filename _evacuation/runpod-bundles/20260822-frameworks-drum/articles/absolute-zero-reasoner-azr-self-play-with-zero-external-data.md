# Absolute Zero Reasoner (AZR): Self-Play with Zero External Data

- **Kind:** article | **Issuer:** Cheng et al. (BIGAI / Tsinghua) | **Region:** —
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** mined
- **Effective:** 2025

Self-play training that improves reasoning on math and coding with no gold labels or human-defined queries, outperforming models trained on thousands of expert examples. Evidence that capability can bootstrap from the model's own generations plus a verifier — the engine behind "auto-queue next tasks."
