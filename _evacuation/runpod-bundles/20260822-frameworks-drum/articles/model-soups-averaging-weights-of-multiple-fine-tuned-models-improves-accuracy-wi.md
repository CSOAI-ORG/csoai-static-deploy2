# Model soups: averaging weights of multiple fine-tuned models improves accuracy without increasing inference time

- **Kind:** article | **Issuer:** Wortsman, Ilharco, et al. (UW / Google) | **Region:** —
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** mined
- **Effective:** 2022

The foundational result that averaging weights of multiple fine-tuned models (uniform and greedy soups) improves accuracy at zero inference cost. Establishes the "merge then benchmark then keep if better" baseline: greedy soup selection is literally a keep-if-better loop over candidate merges.
