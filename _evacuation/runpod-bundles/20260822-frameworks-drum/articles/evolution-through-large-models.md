# Evolution through Large Models

- **Kind:** article | **Issuer:** Lehman, Gordon, Jain, et al. (MIT / IBM) | **Region:** —
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** mined
- **Effective:** 2022

Shows LLMs can act as variation operators in evolutionary search, producing novel solutions across domains without domain-specific training. Bridges "self-improving loop" and "merge-kit": the model proposes candidates, selection keeps what scores better.
