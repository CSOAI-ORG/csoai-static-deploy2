# ISO 21448 — SOTIF (Safety of the Intended Functionality)

- **Kind:** framework | **Issuer:** ISO | **Region:** ISO/IEC
- **Binding:** no | **Status:** active
- **Effective:** 2022

Safety of the Intended Functionality for road vehicles — the AV safety-standard complement to ISO 26262 (handles functional insufficiencies, not just failures). Complements the Tesla/AV sector.
