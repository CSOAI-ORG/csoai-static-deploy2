# Bletchley Declaration & Frontier AI Safety Commitments

- **Kind:** framework | **Issuer:** UK government / AI Safety Summit attendees (28 countries + EU) | **Region:** International
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** Active
- **Effective:** Nov 2023

First international declaration on frontier-AI risks; accompanying Frontier AI Safety Commitments bind signatory developers to external pre-deployment testing, vulnerability reporting, and transparency. The "external, independent pre-deployment testing" clause is a direct mandate for third-party evaluators like us.
