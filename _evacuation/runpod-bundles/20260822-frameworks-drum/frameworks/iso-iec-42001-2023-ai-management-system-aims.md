# ISO/IEC 42001:2023 — AI Management System (AIMS)

- **Kind:** framework | **Issuer:** ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42 | **Region:** Global, certifiable
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** Active — the first certifiable AI management-system standard
- **Effective:** Dec 2023 (first edition)

Specifies requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving an AI management system (AIMS), including AI risk management, lifecycle, and impact assessment controls. The certification backbone: our 3KB signed measurement cards map naturally onto AIMS clause 8 (AI risk assessment) evidence requirements.
