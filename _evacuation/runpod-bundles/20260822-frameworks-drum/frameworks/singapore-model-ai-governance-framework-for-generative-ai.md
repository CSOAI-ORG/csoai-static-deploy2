# Singapore Model AI Governance Framework for Generative AI

- **Kind:** framework | **Issuer:** Singapore IMDA / AI Verify Foundation | **Region:** Singapore + global
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** Active
- **Effective:** Proposed May 2024; finalised Jan 2025

Extends the Model Framework to generative AI — accountability, safety, provenance, and alignment for GenAI models and applications. GenAI-specific expectations (provenance, alignment) translate into concrete test axes for our model cards.
