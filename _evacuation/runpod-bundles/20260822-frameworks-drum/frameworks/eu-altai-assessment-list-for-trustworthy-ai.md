# EU ALTAI — Assessment List for Trustworthy AI

- **Kind:** framework | **Issuer:** European Commission (High-Level Expert Group on AI) | **Region:** EU (voluntary)
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** Active (reference; superseded in practice by AI Act conformity)
- **Effective:** Final, July 2020

A self-assessment checklist operationalising the seven requirements of the Ethics Guidelines for Trustworthy AI (human agency, robustness, privacy, transparency, diversity, societal well-being, accountability). The classic pre-AI-Act ethics checklist — a lightweight, widely recognised baseline for smaller clients' first measurement engagement.
