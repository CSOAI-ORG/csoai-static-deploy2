# Japan AI Utilization Guidelines

- **Kind:** framework | **Issuer:** Japan Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications (MIC) | **Region:** Japan
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** Active
- **Effective:** April 2024

Guidelines for AI *users* (businesses and citizens) — complementing the Business Guidelines with user-side duties and societal considerations. Covers the demand side — user-side attestation is an underserved measurement market.
