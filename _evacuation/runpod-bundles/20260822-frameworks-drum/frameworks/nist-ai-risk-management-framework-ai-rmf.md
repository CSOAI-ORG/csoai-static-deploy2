# NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF)

- **Kind:** framework | **Issuer:** — | **Region:** USA
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** no
- **Effective:** —

Voluntary 4-function framework — Govern, Map, Measure, Manage; mandated by National AI Initiative Act 2020; AI RMF 1.0 published Jan 2023; de-facto global baseline harmonized with EU AI Act; AI 600-1 adds GenAI profile.
