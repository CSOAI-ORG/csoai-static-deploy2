# Anthropic Responsible Scaling Policy (RSP)

- **Kind:** framework | **Issuer:** Anthropic | **Region:** Global (frontier developer)
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** Active
- **Effective:** v1 Sep 2023; v2 Oct 2024; v3 Sep 2025

Capability-threshold-based policy: assigns AI Safety Levels (ASL-2/3/4) triggered by dangerous-capability evaluations, with required safeguards (cybersecurity, CBRN, autonomy) before further scaling. The canonical "evaluation-gated deployment" model — ASL-style capability thresholds are the template for our certification tiers.
