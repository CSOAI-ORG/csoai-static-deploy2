# NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF) 1.0 — NIST AI 100-1

- **Kind:** framework | **Issuer:** U.S. National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) | **Region:** US + voluntary global
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** Active (1.0 published; 2.0 under development)
- **Effective:** 1.0, Jan 2023; 2.0 revision work in progress (2025–2026)

A voluntary, non-regulatory framework to help organisations manage AI risk through the core functions Govern, Map, Measure, Manage — with a companion Playbook of concrete actions. Its "Measure" function is the natural hook for independent, signed measurement services — map GSPC-style axes onto the RMF's risk-measurement expectations.
