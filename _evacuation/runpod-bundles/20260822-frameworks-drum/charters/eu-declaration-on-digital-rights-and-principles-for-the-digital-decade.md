# EU Declaration on Digital Rights and Principles for the Digital Decade

- **Kind:** charter | **Issuer:** European Parliament, Council and Commission | **Region:** —
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** In force (political declaration)
- **Effective:** Dec 15, 2022 (signed); OJ publication Jan 23, 2023

Eight chapters incl. human-centric digital transformation, freedom of choice, solidarity & inclusion, participation, safety/security/empowerment, and sustainability — the EU's umbrella values statement for the digital decade, covering trustworthy AI.
