# Seoul Declaration for Safe, Innovative and Inclusive AI

- **Kind:** charter | **Issuer:** AI Seoul Summit (27 governments incl. G7, EU, UK, Korea; co-hosted by Korea and UK) | **Region:** —
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** Voluntary (political commitment)
- **Effective:** May 21-22, 2024

Bletchley follow-up: commitments to international cooperation on safety science, innovation and inclusion, resilience, and a continuing summit-to-summit cadence (the AI Seoul Summit Business Group).
