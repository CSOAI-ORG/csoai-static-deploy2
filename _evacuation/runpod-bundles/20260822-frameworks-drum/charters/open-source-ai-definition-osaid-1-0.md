# Open Source AI Definition (OSAID) 1.0

- **Kind:** charter | **Issuer:** Open Source Initiative (OSI) | **Region:** —
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** In force (community standard)
- **Effective:** Oct 28, 2024 (after a 2-year co-design process)

Four freedoms for AI systems — use, study, modify, share — applied to models, requiring the "preferred form of modification" (training data, code, weights) and transparency about training data.
