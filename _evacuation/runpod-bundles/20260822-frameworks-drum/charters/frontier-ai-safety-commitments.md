# Frontier AI Safety Commitments

- **Kind:** charter | **Issuer:** 16 frontier AI companies (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, Microsoft, Meta, Amazon, IBM, Mistral, xAI, and others) at the AI Seoul Summit | **Region:** —
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** Voluntary (company commitments; partially diluted by some labs in 2025)
- **Effective:** May 21, 2024

Commitments to publish safety frameworks before releasing frontier models, set thresholds for severe risks, conduct red-teaming, fund AI safety research (20-30%, later 40% by some), and report incidents.
