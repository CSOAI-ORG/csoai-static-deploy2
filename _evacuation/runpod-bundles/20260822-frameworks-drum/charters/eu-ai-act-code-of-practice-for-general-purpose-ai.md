# EU AI Act Code of Practice for General-Purpose AI

- **Kind:** charter | **Issuer:** European Commission (AI Office) | **Region:** —
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** Voluntary (signatory code; quasi-binding via GPAI obligations)
- **Effective:** Draft published April 28, 2025; final May 2025

Operationalizes AI Act GPAI obligations — transparency & copyright, risk identification/assessment incl. systemic risk, technical mitigations, internal governance; 26+ companies signed (Google, Microsoft, OpenAI), Meta declined.
