# G20 AI Principles

- **Kind:** charter | **Issuer:** G20 (Osaka Summit, Japan) | **Region:** —
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** Voluntary (political commitment)
- **Effective:** June 2019

Endorses the OECD principles and adds a human-centred framing: responsible stewardship of trustworthy AI, inclusive design, and international cooperation on AI governance.
