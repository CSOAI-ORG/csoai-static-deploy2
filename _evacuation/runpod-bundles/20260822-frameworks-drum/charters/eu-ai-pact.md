# EU AI Pact

- **Kind:** charter | **Issuer:** European Commission | **Region:** —
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** Voluntary (signatory pledges; 1,000+ companies incl. Microsoft, OpenAI, Google)
- **Effective:** Launched Nov 2023; pledge acceptance through 2024-2025 ahead of AI Act application

Voluntary two-part pledges — early fulfillment of core AI Act obligations (governance, risk mapping, transparency, human oversight) plus extra commitments (AI literacy, cybersecurity, green AI).
