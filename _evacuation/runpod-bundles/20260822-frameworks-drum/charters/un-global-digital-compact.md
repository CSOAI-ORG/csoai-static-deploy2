# UN Global Digital Compact

- **Kind:** charter | **Issuer:** United Nations General Assembly (via Pact for the Future, resolution 79/1) | **Region:** —
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** In force (political commitment; 2030 horizon)
- **Effective:** Sept 22, 2024 (adopted)

AI chapter commits to inclusive AI governance and human-centric design, including the first universal commitment to "AI that is safe, secure, and trustworthy", with 2030 goals on AI literacy, capacity building, and global governance dialogue.
