# Council of Europe Framework Convention on Artificial Intelligence (CETS 225)

- **Kind:** charter | **Issuer:** Council of Europe (47 members + observer states) | **Region:** —
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** In force (treaty — legally binding for ratifiers; 5-ratification threshold met 2025)
- **Effective:** Adopted May 17, 2024; opened for signature Sept 5, 2024 (Vilnius); entered into force Aug 1, 2025

The first legally binding international treaty on AI: human rights, democracy, and rule of law across the AI lifecycle; risk-based approach, transparency, accountability, remedies, non-discrimination; applies to public and private actors.
