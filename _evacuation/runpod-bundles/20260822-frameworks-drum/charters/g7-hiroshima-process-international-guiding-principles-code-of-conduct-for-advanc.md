# G7 Hiroshima Process — International Guiding Principles & Code of Conduct for Advanced AI Systems

- **Kind:** charter | **Issuer:** G7 (Japan presidency; Hiroshima AI Process) | **Region:** —
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** Voluntary (political commitment)
- **Effective:** Oct 30, 2023 (endorsed by G7 leaders)

Eleven guiding principles and eleven concrete developer actions: risk identification/mitigation, red-teaming, incident reporting, information security, content watermarking, transparency reporting, and internal governance.
