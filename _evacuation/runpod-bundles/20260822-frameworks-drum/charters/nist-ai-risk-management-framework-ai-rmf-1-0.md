# NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0)

- **Kind:** charter | **Issuer:** U.S. National Institute of Standards and Technology | **Region:** —
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** Voluntary (framework)
- **Effective:** Jan 2023 (AI RMF 1.0; Generative AI Profile July 2024)

Seven characteristics of trustworthy AI (valid & reliable; safe; secure & resilient; accountable & transparent; explainable & interpretable; privacy-enhanced; fair with managed bias) organized in Govern–Map–Measure–Manage functions.
