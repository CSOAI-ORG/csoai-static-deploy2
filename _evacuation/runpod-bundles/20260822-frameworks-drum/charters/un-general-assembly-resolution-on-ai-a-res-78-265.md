# UN General Assembly Resolution on AI (A/RES/78/265)

- **Kind:** charter | **Issuer:** UN General Assembly (US-led; co-sponsored by 120+ states incl. China) | **Region:** —
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** In force (non-binding resolution)
- **Effective:** March 21, 2024

First UNGA resolution on AI: calls for safe, secure, and trustworthy AI systems, closing digital divides, international cooperation, and respect for human rights in AI development.
