# Singapore Model AI Governance Framework (incl. Generative AI edition)

- **Kind:** charter | **Issuer:** IMDA & PDPC (Singapore) | **Region:** —
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** In force (voluntary framework)
- **Effective:** Jan 2019 (v1), May 2020 (v2); Generative AI edition May 2024

Two-part guidance (internal governance + decision-making) spanning accountability, data, explainability, human oversight, safety, transparency; GenAI edition adds model development, deployment, safety, and incident reporting.
