# Vietnam Law on AI

- **Kind:** regulation | **Issuer:** Vietnam | **Region:** VN
- **Binding:** yes | **Status:** in force (grace)
- **Effective:** 1 Mar 2026 (grace to 1 Mar 2027)

Law No. 134/2025/QH15 — risk-based conformity assessment, national AI DB registration, penalties to VND 2bn.
