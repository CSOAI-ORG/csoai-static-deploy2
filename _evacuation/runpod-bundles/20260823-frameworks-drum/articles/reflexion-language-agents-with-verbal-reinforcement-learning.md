# Reflexion: Language Agents with Verbal Reinforcement Learning

- **Kind:** article | **Issuer:** Shinn et al. (Northeastern / MIT) | **Region:** —
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** mined
- **Effective:** 2023

Agents store verbal self-feedback after each episode and reuse it in the next attempt — a persistent, cheap improvement loop without gradient updates. The mechanism for a memory-and-revise cycle that compounds across benchmark rounds.
