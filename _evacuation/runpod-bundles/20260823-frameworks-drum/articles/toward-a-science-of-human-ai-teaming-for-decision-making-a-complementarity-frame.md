# Toward a science of human–AI teaming for decision making: A complementarity framework

- **Kind:** article | **Issuer:** Cleotilde Gonzalez & Jonas F. Donahue (Carnegie Mellon University / NSF) | **Region:** —
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** mined
- **Effective:** 2025

Proposes a formal complementarity framework for when and why human+AI teams outperform either alone, centered on how the two parties' memory, attention, and reasoning (MAR) differ. This is the theoretical spine for a "keep-if-better" division-of-labor rule: route work to the partner whose capabilities are complementary, and only delegate when joint performance beats isolated performance.
