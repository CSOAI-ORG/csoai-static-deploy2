# Socratic-Zero: Bootstrapping Reasoning via Data-Free Agent Co-Evolution

- **Kind:** article | **Issuer:** Wang, Jiao, et al. (arXiv) | **Region:** —
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** mined
- **Effective:** 2025

Two agents co-evolve with zero external data: one generates questions, the other answers, and mutual evaluation filters what is kept — a pure self-improvement loop with no human labels. This is the closest published analogue to a 90/10 auto-vs-ambiguous router where the system itself decides which tasks are answerable and which must escalate.
