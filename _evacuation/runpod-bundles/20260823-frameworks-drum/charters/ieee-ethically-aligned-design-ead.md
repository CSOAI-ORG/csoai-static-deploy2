# IEEE Ethically Aligned Design (EAD)

- **Kind:** charter | **Issuer:** IEEE Global Initiative on Ethics of Autonomous and Intelligent Systems | **Region:** —
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** Voluntary (consensus guidance)
- **Effective:** v2 (Dec 2017), v3 (April 2019)

Eight general principles (human rights, well-being, accountability, transparency, awareness of misuse, competence) plus committee-level recommendations spanning data, algorithms, law, and economics.
