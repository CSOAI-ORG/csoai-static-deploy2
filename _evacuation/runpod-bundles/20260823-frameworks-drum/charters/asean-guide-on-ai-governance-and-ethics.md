# ASEAN Guide on AI Governance and Ethics

- **Kind:** charter | **Issuer:** ASEAN (Digital Ministers Meeting, via ASEAN Digital Senior Officials) | **Region:** —
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** Voluntary (guide; national implementation)
- **Effective:** Feb 2, 2024

Five core principles — transparency & explainability, fairness & equity, security & safety, human-centricity, accountability — plus four governance components: internal governance, risk/impact assessment, operations management, and stakeholder interaction.
