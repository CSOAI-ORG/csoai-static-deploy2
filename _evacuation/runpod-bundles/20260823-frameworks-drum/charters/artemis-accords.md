# Artemis Accords (2020)

- **Kind:** charter | **Issuer:** NASA / US State Dept | **Region:** Space
- **Binding:** no | **Status:** voluntary (50+ signatories)
- **Effective:** 2020

Principles for Moon/Mars cooperation: transparency, interoperability, emergency assistance, space-resource utilisation, deconfliction of activities. The operating charter for commercial lunar activity.
