# UAE Charter for the Development and Use of AI

- **Kind:** charter | **Issuer:** UAE (AI Office / Cabinet; championed by Omar Sultan Al Olama) | **Region:** UAE
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** In force (national charter)
- **Effective:** Sept 2024 (approved by UAE Cabinet)

Twelve principles — human-centricity, inclusiveness, safety, accountability, transparency, privacy, fairness, sustainability, international cooperation, innovation, legal compliance, and continuous review — binding on federal entities and guiding AI use.
