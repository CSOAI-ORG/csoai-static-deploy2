# Toronto Declaration: Protecting the rights to equality and non-discrimination in machine learning systems

- **Kind:** charter | **Issuer:** Amnesty International & Access Now (multi-org civil society coalition) | **Region:** —
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** Voluntary (advocacy)
- **Effective:** May 2018

Applies binding international human rights law — especially the rights to equality and non-discrimination — to the design, development, and use of machine learning in both public and private sectors.
