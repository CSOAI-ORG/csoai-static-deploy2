# Rome Call for AI Ethics

- **Kind:** charter | **Issuer:** Pontifical Academy for Life (Vatican), joined by IBM, Microsoft, FAO, Cisco, and others; interreligious signing at Hiroshima, July 2024 | **Region:** —
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** Voluntary (signatory)
- **Effective:** Feb 28, 2020 (updated 2023)

Six principles — transparency, inclusion, responsibility, impartiality, reliability, security & privacy — plus calls for "algorethics", education, and regulation.
