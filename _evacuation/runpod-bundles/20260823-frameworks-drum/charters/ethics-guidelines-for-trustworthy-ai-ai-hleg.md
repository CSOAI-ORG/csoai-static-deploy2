# Ethics Guidelines for Trustworthy AI (AI HLEG)

- **Kind:** charter | **Issuer:** European Commission High-Level Expert Group on Artificial Intelligence | **Region:** —
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** Voluntary (soft law; fed into the EU AI Act)
- **Effective:** April 8, 2019

Seven requirements — human agency & oversight, technical robustness & safety, privacy & data governance, transparency, diversity/non-discrimination/fairness, societal & environmental well-being, accountability — plus the ALTAI assessment list.
