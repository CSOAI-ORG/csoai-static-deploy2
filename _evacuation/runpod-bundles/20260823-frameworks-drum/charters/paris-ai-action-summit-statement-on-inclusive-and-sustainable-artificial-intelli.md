# Paris AI Action Summit — Statement on Inclusive and Sustainable Artificial Intelligence for People and the Planet

- **Kind:** charter | **Issuer:** France (Élysée) — 60+ countries incl. China, India, EU; the US and UK declined to sign | **Region:** —
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** Voluntary (political commitment)
- **Effective:** Feb 11, 2025

Commitments to inclusive and sustainable AI: open-source/AI commons, interoperability, avoiding market concentration, labor and environmental protection, digital public infrastructure, and simplified governance.
