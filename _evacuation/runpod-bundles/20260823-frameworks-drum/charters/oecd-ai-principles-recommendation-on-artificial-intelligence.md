# OECD AI Principles (Recommendation on Artificial Intelligence)

- **Kind:** charter | **Issuer:** OECD (adopted by 42 countries, later 47+) | **Region:** OECD
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** In force (non-binding OECD Recommendation — soft law)
- **Effective:** May 22, 2019

Five value principles — inclusive growth/sustainable development/well-being; human-centred values & fairness; transparency & explainability; robustness, security & safety; accountability — plus national policy recommendations.
