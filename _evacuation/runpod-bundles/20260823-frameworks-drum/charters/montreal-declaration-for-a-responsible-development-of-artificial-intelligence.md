# Montreal Declaration for a Responsible Development of Artificial Intelligence

- **Kind:** charter | **Issuer:** University of Montreal (led by Yoshua Bengio / Forum on Socially Responsible AI), supported by the Fonds de recherche du Québec | **Region:** Canada
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** Voluntary (signatory)
- **Effective:** Dec 2018 (launched 2018 after 2017-18 public consultation)

Ten principles — well-being, respect for autonomy, protection of privacy, solidarity, democratic participation, equity, diversity and inclusion, prudence, responsibility, and sustainable development — for the responsible development of AI.
