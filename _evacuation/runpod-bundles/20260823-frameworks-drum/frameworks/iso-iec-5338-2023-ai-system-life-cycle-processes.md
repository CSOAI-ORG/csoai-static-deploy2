# ISO/IEC 5338:2023 — AI System Life Cycle Processes

- **Kind:** framework | **Issuer:** ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42 | **Region:** Global
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** Active
- **Effective:** 2023

Defines processes and lifecycle stages for AI systems (from ISO/IEC 15288 adaptation) — engineering governance across conception to retirement. Gives lifecycle checkpoints where continuous measurement (rather than point-in-time) fits — supports our living-attestation model.
