# Australia AI Ethics Principles

- **Kind:** framework | **Issuer:** Australian Government (DISR / CSIRO) | **Region:** Australia
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** Active
- **Effective:** Nov 2019

Eight principles (human-centred, inclusive, privacy, reliability, transparency, contestability, accountability) guiding responsible AI design/use. Short, enforceable-sounding checklist for Australian SMB measurement engagements.
