# NIST AI RMF Generative AI Profile — NIST AI 600-1

- **Kind:** framework | **Issuer:** U.S. National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) | **Region:** US + voluntary global
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** Active
- **Effective:** July 2024

Applies the AI RMF to generative AI specifically, mapping 12 gen-AI risk categories (hallucination, harmful bias, information security, CBRN uplift, etc.) to the RMF's Govern/Map/Measure/Manage functions. Provides a ready-made 12-category risk taxonomy we can test against — a strong candidate for per-axis measurement cards.
