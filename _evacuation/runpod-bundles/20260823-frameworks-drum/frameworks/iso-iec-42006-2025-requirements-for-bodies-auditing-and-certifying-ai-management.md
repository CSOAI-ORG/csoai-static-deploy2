# ISO/IEC 42006:2025 — Requirements for Bodies Auditing and Certifying AI Management Systems

- **Kind:** framework | **Issuer:** ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42 | **Region:** Global, certification bodies
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** Active (new)

Requirements for organisations providing audit and certification of AI management systems against ISO/IEC 42001 — the ISO/IEC 17021-style scheme for AIMS. Our accreditation pathway: a body certified to 42006 can offer AIMS certification audits — a concrete compliance product line.
