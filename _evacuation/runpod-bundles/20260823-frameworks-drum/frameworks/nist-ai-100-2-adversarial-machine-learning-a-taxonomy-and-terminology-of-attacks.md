# NIST AI 100-2 — Adversarial Machine Learning: A Taxonomy and Terminology of Attacks and Mitigations

- **Kind:** framework | **Issuer:** U.S. NIST | **Region:** US + voluntary global
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** Active (final)
- **Effective:** Final, March 2025

A taxonomy of 54+ attack/defence types against ML systems (evasion, poisoning, privacy, abuse) with mitigations, designed to plug into the AI RMF's risk management. A structured attack vocabulary that can underpin adversarial-evaluation legs (jailbreak probes, red-team scoring) with published terminology.
