# Google DeepMind Frontier Safety Framework (FSF)

- **Kind:** framework | **Issuer:** Google DeepMind | **Region:** Global (frontier developer)
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** Active
- **Effective:** May 2024; v2 Oct 2024; v3.0 Sep 2025

Defines critical capability levels (CCL1–4) and critical risk levels (CRL) with evaluation-gated safety measures for deployment (incl. manipulation and shutdown risks in v3). Another evaluation-gated framework — reinforces the market for independent, verifiable capability/risk scoring.
