# Illinois AI in Employment (HB 3773)

- **Kind:** regulation | **Issuer:** Illinois | **Region:** US-State
- **Binding:** yes | **Status:** in force
- **Effective:** 1 Jan 2026

AI discrimination in employment is a civil-rights violation; notice required.
