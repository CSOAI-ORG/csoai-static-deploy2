# UN Outer Space Treaty (1967)

- **Kind:** charter | **Issuer:** United Nations | **Region:** Space
- **Binding:** yes | **Status:** in force
- **Effective:** 1967

Treaty on Principles Governing the Activities of States in the Exploration and Use of Outer Space — the constitution of space law: non-appropriation, free access, state responsibility for national space activities incl. private actors. 115+ parties.
