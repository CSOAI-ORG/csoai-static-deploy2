# UN Moon Agreement (1979)

- **Kind:** charter | **Issuer:** United Nations | **Region:** Space
- **Binding:** yes | **Status:** in force (low adoption)
- **Effective:** 1979

Agreement Governing the Activities of States on the Moon — common heritage of mankind; low adoption (no major space power). Relevant to Moon-colonization claims.
