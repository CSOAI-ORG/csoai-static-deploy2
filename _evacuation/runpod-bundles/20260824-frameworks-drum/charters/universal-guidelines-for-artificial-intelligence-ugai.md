# Universal Guidelines for Artificial Intelligence (UGAI)

- **Kind:** charter | **Issuer:** The Public Voice (coalition co-founded by EPIC; drafted by Marc Rotenberg et al.) | **Region:** —
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** Voluntary
- **Effective:** Feb 2018 (adopted at Brussels "Public Voice" meeting)

Twelve guidelines including transparency, right to know, algorithmic accountability, data quality, auditability, human determination, and prohibition on secret profiling.
