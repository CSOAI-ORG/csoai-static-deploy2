# Google AI Principles

- **Kind:** charter | **Issuer:** Google | **Region:** —
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** In force (company policy)
- **Effective:** June 2018 (updated Feb 4, 2025)

Seven principles — socially beneficial; avoid creating/reinforcing unfair bias; built and tested for safety; accountable to people; privacy by design; high scientific standards; uses accordant with principles — plus 2025 update reframing responsibility/innovation.
