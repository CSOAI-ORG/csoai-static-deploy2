# CHAI Blueprint for Trustworthy AI (Implementation Guidance and Assurance for AI in Health Care)

- **Kind:** charter | **Issuer:** Coalition for Health AI (CHAI — 1,000+ organizations incl. Microsoft, Google, Mayo Clinic, Duke Health) | **Region:** —
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** Voluntary (guidance)
- **Effective:** Feb 2024

Blueprint for trustworthy health AI: safety, efficacy, equity, explainability, transparency, privacy/security — plus an assurance model, testing/evaluation guidance, and "health AI assurance" reporting.
