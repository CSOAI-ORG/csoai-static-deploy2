# Egyptian Charter for Responsible AI

- **Kind:** charter | **Issuer:** Egypt Ministry of Communications and Information Technology (MCIT, via the National Council for AI) | **Region:** Egypt
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** In force (national charter)
- **Effective:** Feb 2023 (launched; developed through national consultations)

Principles on human-centricity, transparency, fairness, privacy, safety, accountability, and sustainability for responsible AI development and use in Egypt.
