# UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence

- **Kind:** framework | **Issuer:** UNESCO | **Region:** Global
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** Active
- **Effective:** Nov 2021 (adopted by 193 member states)

First global normative instrument on AI ethics — values (human rights, fairness, sustainability) plus an implementation methodology (Readiness Assessment Methodology, Ethical Impact Assessment). Its Ethical Impact Assessment is a soft framework many non-OECD governments adopt — a market for independent assessment services.
