# Blueprint for an AI Bill of Rights

- **Kind:** framework | **Issuer:** White House Office of Science and Technology Policy (OSTP) | **Region:** US
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** Active (policy guidance, non-binding)
- **Effective:** Oct 2022

Sets five principles for automated systems — safe/effective systems, algorithmic discrimination protections, data privacy, notice/explanation, and human alternatives/fallback. A concise US checklist for "human-in-the-loop" and non-discrimination attestation services.
