# Canada Voluntary Code of Conduct on the Responsible Development and Management of Advanced Generative AI Systems

- **Kind:** framework | **Issuer:** Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada (ISED) | **Region:** Canada
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** Active (interim until AIDA in force)
- **Effective:** Sep 2023

Six commitments for GenAI developers: risk assessment, data quality, transparency, testing/monitoring, incident reporting, watermarking. The "testing and monitoring" commitment is a direct hook for ongoing signed measurement subscriptions in Canada.
