# International AI Safety Report

- **Kind:** framework | **Issuer:** UK DSIT, chaired by Prof. Yoshua Bengio (2025) and co-chairs (2026) | **Region:** International (100+ experts)
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** Active (annual)
- **Effective:** 1st ed. Jan 2025; 2nd ed. 2026

The scientific consensus report on frontier-AI capabilities, risks, and mitigations — effectively the evidence base that shapes national AI safety frameworks. Its risk taxonomy is a defensible basis for defining measurement axes that regulators expect to see tested.
