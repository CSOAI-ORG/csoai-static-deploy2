# ISO/IEC 23894:2023 — AI Risk Management Guidance

- **Kind:** framework | **Issuer:** ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42 | **Region:** Global
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** Active
- **Effective:** Feb 2023 (first edition); Amendment 1 2025

Guidance for managing AI-specific risk within any organisation — complements ISO 31000 and 42001 with AI-specific risk sources and treatment planning. Defines the risk-language we should speak in audit packs; pairs with our 16-axis measurement to evidence "risk sources identified."
