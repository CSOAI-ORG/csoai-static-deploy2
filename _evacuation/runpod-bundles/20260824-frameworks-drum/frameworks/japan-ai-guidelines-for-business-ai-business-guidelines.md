# Japan AI Guidelines for Business (AI Business Guidelines)

- **Kind:** framework | **Issuer:** Japan METI + MIC | **Region:** Japan
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** Active
- **Effective:** Ver 1.0, April 2024

Practical, risk-based guidelines for AI businesses across the value chain (developers, providers, users) — flexible governance, risk assessment, transparency, safety. Japan's "risk-based, no hard law" posture makes voluntary measurement/attestation a natural fit for Japanese enterprises.
