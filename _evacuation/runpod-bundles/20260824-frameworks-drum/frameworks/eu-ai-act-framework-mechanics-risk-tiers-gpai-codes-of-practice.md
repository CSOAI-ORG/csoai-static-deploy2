# EU AI Act — Framework Mechanics (Risk Tiers, GPAI, Codes of Practice)

- **Kind:** framework | **Issuer:** European Parliament & Council (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) | **Region:** EU market (extraterritorial effect)
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** Active (phasing in)
- **Effective:** Adopted June 2024; in force Aug 2024; phased application (GPAI obligations Aug 2025, full application Aug 2026/2027)

Risk-tiered governance: prohibited practices (unacceptable), high-risk (conformity assessment + harmonised standards), limited/transparency, and general-purpose AI model (GPAI) obligations with systemic-risk thresholds — enforced via codes of practice and market surveillance. The world's first binding AI governance regime — conformity-assessment evidence and GPAI systemic-risk testing are exactly the signed measurement services we provide.
