# IEEE 7001-2021 — Transparency of Autonomous Systems

- **Kind:** framework | **Issuer:** IEEE Standards Association | **Region:** Global
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** Active
- **Effective:** Published March 2022 (PAR 2016; approved Dec 2021)

Defines measurable, testable levels of transparency for autonomous systems, enabling objective assessment and compliance determination. Testable transparency levels map directly to our explainability/transparency axes.
