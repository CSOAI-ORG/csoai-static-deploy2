# MITRE ATLAS (Adversarial Threat Landscape for AI Systems)

- **Kind:** framework | **Issuer:** MITRE | **Region:** Global
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** Active
- **Effective:** Ongoing since 2020 (living knowledge base)

Knowledge base of adversary tactics/techniques against AI systems (model-level analogue of MITRE ATT&CK), with case studies and mitigations. The mapping vocabulary for adversarial measurement — our jailbreak/red-team findings can be tagged to ATLAS techniques.
