# ISO/IEC 42005:2025 — AI System Impact Assessment

- **Kind:** framework | **Issuer:** ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42 | **Region:** Global
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** Active (new)
- **Effective:** May 2025

Specifies the process and content of AI system impact assessments — the successor discipline to AI impact assessments, integrated with AIMS. The assessment artefact ISO-certified orgs must produce — a natural deliverable for our measurement/attestation services.
