# ETSI ISG SAI — Securing Artificial Intelligence (GR SAI series)

- **Kind:** framework | **Issuer:** ETSI (Industry Specification Group on Securing AI) | **Region:** Europe/global telecom & ICT
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** Active (deliverables published)
- **Effective:** 2019–2023 (GR SAI 001–009: threat landscape, mitigation framework, data supply chain, hardware security, etc.)

Standards deliverables defining AI security threats, mitigations, and mitigation-performance evaluation for AI-based systems. ETSI SAI 005 defines mitigation-performance measurement — a Europe-grade benchmark for evaluating how well mitigations work.
