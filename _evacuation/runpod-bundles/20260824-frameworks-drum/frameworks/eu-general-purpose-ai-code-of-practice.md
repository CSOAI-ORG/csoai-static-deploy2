# EU General-Purpose AI Code of Practice

- **Kind:** framework | **Issuer:** European Commission AI Office | **Region:** EU (GPAI providers)
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** Active
- **Effective:** Final, July 2025

Operationalises GPAI duties under the AI Act — transparency, copyright, and safety/security commitments for general-purpose AI models (incl. systemic-risk models). Signatories must show risk assessments and mitigations — our per-axis evaluation results are directly usable compliance evidence.
