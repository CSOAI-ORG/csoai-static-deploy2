# UK AI Safety Institute (AISI) — Approach to Evaluations

- **Kind:** framework | **Issuer:** UK AISI (now AI Security Institute, DSIT) | **Region:** UK + international
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** Active
- **Effective:** Feb 2024 (published approach); ongoing

The UK's official methodology for pre-deployment safety evaluations of frontier models — capabilities, safeguards, societal impacts, and national-security risk assessments. Aligning our evaluation legs with AISI methodology gives us credibility and potential evaluation-partnership roles.
