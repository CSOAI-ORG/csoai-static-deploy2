# ISO/IEC 42001:2023 — Information technology — Artificial intelligence — Management system

- **Kind:** article | **Issuer:** ISO / IEC JTC 1/SC 42 | **Region:** —
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** mined
- **Effective:** 2023

The first certifiable AI management system standard, built on the Plan-Do-Check-Act (PDCA) continuous-improvement cycle. The formal governance loop the framework maps onto: plan AI objectives → do (operate) → check (measure/audit) → act (correct and improve), with documented AI policy and risk processes.
