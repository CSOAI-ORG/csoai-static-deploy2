# Basilisk: An Evolutionary AI Red-Teaming Framework for Systematic Security Evaluation of Large Language Models

- **Kind:** article | **Issuer:** Zenodo (open framework) | **Region:** —
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** mined
- **Effective:** 2025

Evolutionary red-teaming: a population of attack prompts evolves, selected by whether they break the target — a mirror-image keep-if-better loop for adversarial testing. The audit complement to a merge loop: attacks that win get kept, defenses that block them get promoted.
