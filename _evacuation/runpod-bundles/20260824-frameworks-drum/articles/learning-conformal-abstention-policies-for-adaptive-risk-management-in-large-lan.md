# Learning Conformal Abstention Policies for Adaptive Risk Management in Large Language and Vision-Language Models

- **Kind:** article | **Issuer:** Tayebati, Kumar, et al. (arXiv) | **Region:** —
- **Binding:** — | **Status:** mined
- **Effective:** 2025

Uses conformal prediction to learn when a model should abstain (say "I don't know" / escalate) with guaranteed risk control. Provides the statistically grounded 90/10 auto-vs-ambiguous routing rule: auto-answer within a risk budget, escalate outside it.
