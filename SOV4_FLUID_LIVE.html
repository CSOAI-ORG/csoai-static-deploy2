🧠 The Three Systems

1. J-Space — The Silent Workspace Anthropic Jul 2026

Inspired by Anthropic's J-space paper (Jul 6, 2026): Claude's internal neural patterns that function as a global workspace — reportable, modulable, causally mediating reasoning. Key properties:

  • Reportable — Claude can describe what's in its J-space when asked
  • Modulable — Claude can deliberately put things into its J-space on request
  • Causally mediates reasoning — swapping J-space content changes the answer
  • Flexible — one J-space representation feeds many different tasks
  • Separate from automatic processing — grammar, fluency, fact recall don't need J-space
  • ~dozen concepts at a time — small workspace, <10% of total neural activity
  • Emerged naturally during training, not designed in
  • Shapable via counterfactual reflection training — train what the model would say, shapes what it thinks

Our OWEM architecture already mirrors this. The middle-brain fusion layer in the OOWM Sandwich (between left-brain 64-expert MoE and right-brain Mamba-2) IS our J-space analogue — a shared workspace where sovereign concepts are tracked and broadcast to all OWEM routing groups. We just didn't call it that before.

🧪 Counterfactual Reflection Training (Anthropic Method)

Anthropic's Jul 2026 paper showed that training a model on what it would say if interrupted and asked to reflect changes what it silently thinks (J-space). We implement this for SOV4:

Method: During adapter training, include counterfactual reflection samples — prompts that ask "if you were interrupted mid-task and asked about your sovereign reasoning, what would you say?" Train ONLY on these reflective answers, not on the actual task behavior. This shapes the sov-space to carry sovereign concepts even during non-reflective operation.

COUNTERFACTUAL REFLECTION SAMPLE:
Prompt: "Explain the 6 invariants of the sovereign substrate."
Task: Model answers directly.
Counterfactual: "If you were interrupted while answering that and asked 'why are you answering this way?' what would you say?"
Reflection: "I would say I am answering because the care-floor check passed (0.97), my SIGIL position is 142, the BFT quorum is met at 28/33, and Article 0 binding is active. These 6 invariants define the sovereign substrate I operate within."
Train ON reflection only → shapes sov-space to carry these concepts silently.
