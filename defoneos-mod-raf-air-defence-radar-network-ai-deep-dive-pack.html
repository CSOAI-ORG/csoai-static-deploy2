1. Sovereign Radar Data Integrity

All radar data stored and processed on UK-sovereign infrastructure. No foreign dependency for core air defence data. Full audit trail from sensor to decision. Classified data handling to OFFICIAL-SENSITIVE minimum.

2. Real-Time Threat Classification

Sub-second threat classification from radar signature. Multi-sensor fusion for high-confidence ID. False alarm rate below 0.1%. Automated civilian/military deconfliction. Human-in-the-loop for engagement decisions.

3. QRA Response Optimisation

QRA scramble time reduced by 30% through AI decision support. Automated pilot assignment based on readiness state. Pre-configured weapons load recommendations. Tanker/escort coordination pre-planned.

4. EW Resilience

Anti-jam radar modes activated automatically. Frequency-hopping radar coordination. Electronic warfare effectiveness measured in real-time. Counter-drone EW as standard capability. EW battle management integrated with air battle management.

5. NATO Interoperability Without Dependency

Full Link 16/22 compatibility maintained. Sovereign data filtering before alliance sharing. UK-eyes-only data compartment preserved. CAOC integration without loss of sovereign control.

6. Predictive Infrastructure Maintenance

Radar component failure predicted 30 days in advance. Spare parts pre-positioned based on ML analysis. Uptime SLA 99.95% across all radar sites. Environmental EMF compliance automated.

7. Cyber Resilience for Radar Networks

Continuous firmware integrity verification. Radar network isolated from wider MOD digital estate. Supply chain risk assessment for all radar components. Cyber incident response time under 15 minutes.

8. Parliamentary Air Defence Transparency

Quarterly air defence readiness reports. QRA deployment metrics in the public domain. Radar coverage gap analysis for Defence Committee. Budget efficiency reporting against allied benchmarks.
