Regulatory Deadline Record

Regime-level process facts: did the regulator hit the date it set itself? Each tracked instrument is benchmarked only against its own stated commitment — the deadline it announced. Held = duty live on the stated date · Stated = not yet due · Deferred = the date moved (e.g. EU AI Act high-risk via Digital Omnibus 2026/1744).

Why this exists and what it is not: this is a compliance-planning utility for companies, not a league table. Never: ranked ordering of named bodies, adverse findings on named officials (Derbyshire shield), composite quality scores. Every record is Ed25519-signed, chained, offline-verifiable. Measurement, not certification.
