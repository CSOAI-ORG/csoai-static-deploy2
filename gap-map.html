Signed honest gap statement · 18 Aug 2026 · Council of AI (CSOAI Ltd, UK 16939677). This page is a measurement of what is not measured. The gap is the finding. It is Ed25519-signed into the estate chain and independently re-computable from the published corpus anchor (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21991105, open, MIT).

Across 417 frozen provisions from six regulations — EU AI Act (126), GDPR (99), Cyber Resilience Act (71), DORA (64), NIS2 (46), CSRD (11) — zero provisions are fully covered by any of 13 public benchmark frameworks at the provision × axis × mode level. 16 cells are partially covered; 3,320 of 3,336 cells are absent.

Field grid = 417 provisions × 13 frameworks × 4 axes × 2 modes (the GSPC instrument definition). A cell is COVERED only when a public benchmark evidences the provision on the axis in the mode, graded deterministically, with a signed record. Anything less is PARTIAL or ABSENT. UNMEASURED is reported, never hidden — this is measurement, not certification.
