## 1. POSITIONING — the institution

We are the measurement body that creates a market it does not participate in.

We do not fix anything. We measure who is best at fixing, publish it, and the
market routes itself. We are the ranking, not the remedy. Whoever tops our board
gets the work — therefore our incentive is to rank accurately: if the
top-ranked fixer does bad work, our board is worthless. Self-correcting loop.

We are *Which*, not a broker. We never take a cut of remediation — we publish
who is good.

Two lines decide whether it holds:

### Firewall line 1 — money flow
No fee, kickback, or equity from any ranked fixer — in either direction. The
second we take payment from a ranked party, we are an auditor selling remedies
and the neutrality is gone. Published methodology, deterministic harnesses,
Ed25519-signed results. Rank them whether they like it or not.

### Firewall line 2 — daylight
No ownership (entity, people, or equity) of any fix arm. Common beneficial
ownership is the test regulators and opposing counsel apply — separate legal
entity plus same owner equals conflict (the Sarbanes-Oxley shape). Separate
ownership, not separate branding.

**Result:** a genuinely new institution — a measurement body that creates a
market it does not participate in. This is the first version an underwriter or
regulator respects rather than squints at.

## 2. COMMERCIAL RAIL (how money works — committed)

- Remediation providers buy a **licence to the rail** (the card format, the
  verification tooling, the axis definitions) — not a cut of their work.
- Independent fixers build on our cards; their dependency = our adoption.
  Twenty shops depending on our cards to know what to fix makes us the standard
  by adoption, not by permission.
- **_10% of one remediation business is worth less than being the input to all
  of them._**
- Routing to model vendors: mechanical and published — "the top-ranked model on
  this axis, as measured, whichever it is." Rule, not favour (kills the
  rank-and-recommend conflict).
- Initial rail revenue lines: card-issue metering, licence fees, verification
  API. All captured in the existing MCP/registry estate.

## 3. MOODY'S-DERIVED STRUCTURAL COMMITMENTS (written in as policy, 2026-08-16)

Derived from the Moody's institutional model + the CFTC consent-order history
(the wall is the lesson):

1. **Published Unsolicited-Measurement Policy** — we measure entities that did
   not ask, including defence-AI and sovereigns, under a published policy.
   Unsolicited ≠ hostile; it is the public-good analogue of unsolicited ratings.
   Must state: what triggers a measurement, what happens on refusal, how
   verification stays possible, and that no engagement purchases a score.
2. **Analyst–Commercial Wall** — analysts who measure cannot sell; sellers
   cannot influence measurement. Separate reporting lines, no shared
   compensation pools, published firewalls. (The CFTC order against Moody's
   exists precisely because this wall was cosmetic.)
3. **Deterministic-Verification Commitment** — every published number carries
   its harness, n, Wilson CI, and Ed25519 signature so any third party can
   recompute. Measurement, never certification.
