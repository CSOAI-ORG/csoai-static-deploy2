## TEMPLATE 1 — ONE-PAGE MUTUAL NDA

**NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT** dated **[____]**

Between:
1. **CSOAI Ltd** (UK company no. 16939677) of **[registered address]** ("CSOAI"); and
2. **[Counterparty]** of **[address]** ("Recipient").

**1. Purpose.** The parties discuss a potential **[business relationship — describe]**.
**2. Confidential Information.** "Confidential Information" means all non-public
information disclosed by either party (whether oral, written, graphic or in machine-
readable form) that is identified as confidential or, by its nature and the circumstances,
is reasonably to be understood as confidential — including trade secrets, source code,
data corpora, measurement methods, calibration data, business plans and customer lists —
and all notes, analyses and compilations derived from it.
**3. Obligations.** Recipient will (a) keep the Confidential Information confidential;
(b) use it solely for the Purpose; (c) not disclose it except to those of its personnel
bound by equivalent confidentiality who need it for the Purpose; and (d) protect it with
at least the same care as its own confidential information of like kind.
**4. Exclusions.** Not Confidential Information: (a) already public through no breach;
(b) lawfully received from a third party without restriction; (c) independently developed;
(d) required to be disclosed by law (with prompt notice where permitted).
**5. Return.** On request or termination, Recipient returns or securely destroys the
Confidential Information and any copies, certifying in writing.
**6. Term.** Obligations survive for **[5]** years (or, for trade secrets, indefinitely
while they remain secret).
**7. No licence.** Nothing grants any intellectual-property rights; Confidential
Information remains the disclosing party's property.
**8. Law.** England and Wales.
**Signatures:** CSOAI Ltd: ______ Recipient: ______

## TEMPLATE 2 — CONTRACTOR IP ASSIGNMENT + CONFIDENTIALITY CLAUSE
*(Insert into any contractor/service agreement. Without written assignment, a UK
contractor owns the IP in their work by default — this closes that gap.)*

**9. Intellectual Property.** All rights, title and interest (including all IP rights) in
any work product, deliverables, source code, documentation and materials created by
Contractor in connection with this Agreement ("Work Product") vest in and are hereby
**irrevocably assigned** to **CSOAI Ltd**. Contractor will execute any documents and do
all things reasonably requested to perfect and record CSOAI's ownership.
**10. Confidentiality.** Contractor will treat all non-public CSOAI information —
including the data corpus, measurement methods, axis/calibration definitions and any
not-yet-published material — as **confidential and as trade secrets of CSOAI**, will not
disclose or use it except to perform the Work, and on termination will return or destroy
all copies within **[14]** days and certify in writing.
**11. Representations.** Contractor warrants it has the right to assign the Work Product
and has not granted conflicting rights.
