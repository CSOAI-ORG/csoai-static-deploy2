📋 12 Entry Points

Deterrent

UK Nuclear Deterrent — CASD, Trident D5, Dreadnought Programme

Continuous At Sea Deterrent (CASD) — Vanguard-class submarine patrol cycle. Trident D5 missile system — Lockheed Martin, US/UK cooperation. Dreadnought-class replacement programme — 4 boats, BAE Systems Barrow-in-Furness. Warhead programme — AWE Aldermaston, Mk4A warhead, US/W76-1 common design. Penetration aid development. Fire control system — Mk7. Missile compartment — Common Missile Compartment shared with US Columbia-class. Programme cost — £31bn+ (2015 prices). Defence Nuclear Board oversight.

AWE

AWE plc Operations — Aldermaston, Burghfield, Production

Atomic Weapons Establishment — UK's nuclear warhead design, manufacture, and maintenance facility. Aldermaston — warhead assembly, detonator production, high explosive manufacture, hydrodynamic testing. Burghfield — warhead refurbishment, final assembly, magazine storage. AWE plc — Lockheed Martin, Serco, Jacobs joint venture (until 2025). AWE Transition — MOD in-house management from 2025. Nuclear Weapons Council — US/UK warhead cooperation. Special Nuclear Material management. PFPE (Precision Firing and Fuzing Electronics).

Submarine

Submarine Reactor Programme — PWR3, Rolls-Royce, Derby

Nuclear submarine reactor programme — Pressurised Water Reactor 3 (PWR3). Rolls-Royce submarine reactor facility — Raynesway, Derby. Reactor core manufacture — enriched uranium fuel. Steam raising equipment. Primary and secondary coolant circuits. Reactor protection system — safety-critical software. Refuelling complex — Devonport, HMNB Clyde. Reactor compartment manufacture — BAE Systems. Waste management — spent fuel rods, intermediate level waste. Reactor decommissioning — defuel and store.

Waste

Nuclear Waste Management — ILW, LLW, Decommissioning

Nuclear waste management across defence nuclear estate. Intermediate Level Waste (ILW) — interim storage at AWE, Devonport, Rosyth, Sellafield transfer. Low Level Waste (LLW) — Drigg repository, Lillyhall. Very Low Level Waste (VLLW) — site clearance. Radioactive Waste Management Committee — MOD representative. Nuclear Decommissioning Authority — defence sector cooperation. Legacy waste — contaminated soils, redundant facilities. Active liquid waste treatment. Effluent monitoring. Environmental permits.

ONR

ONR Regulation — Nuclear Installations Inspectorate, Safety Cases

Office for Nuclear Regulation — MOD nuclear sites licensing regime. Nuclear Installations Act 1965 — defence site exemptions (s.1(6) and s.2(6)). ONR MOD Inspectorate — dedicated defence nuclear inspectors. Safety case regime — pre-operational safety case, periodic safety review. Emergency Planning — ONR/DEFRA/SEPA joint assessment. Conventional nuclear safety — HSE regulations. Transport of radioactive materials — ONR Certificates of Approval. INES classification — incident reporting. Regulatory engagement strategy.

Security

Nuclear Security — AWE Police, Guard Force, Insider Threat

Nuclear security across defence nuclear estate — AWE Constabulary, MOD Guard Force, Royal Marines nuclear security. Insider threat programme — personnel vetting (DV/SC), behaviour monitoring, access control. Material accountancy — nuclear material balance, inventory verification, IAEA safeguards (UK voluntary offer). Physical protection — blast protection, vehicle barriers, CCTV, access control systems. Cyber security — nuclear facility SCADA protection, air-gapped systems. Transport security — armed escorts, route planning, tactical response.

Skills

Nuclear Skills Pipeline — SSN/SSBN Workforce, STEM Recruitment

Nuclear skills crisis — UK defence nuclear workforce shortage. Engineer recruitment — mechanical, electrical, nuclear, systems, safety case. AWE apprenticeships — 4-year programmes, Aldermaston/Burghfield. Rolls-Royce reactor engineers — Derby graduate scheme. BAE Systems submarine engineers — Barrow-in-Furness. Nuclear Skills Alliance — cross-sector strategy. Academic pipeline — Imperial, Manchester, Lancaster, Birmingham nuclear departments. Security cleared workforce. Talent retention — private sector competition.

Environment

Environmental Remediation — Site Clean-Up, Contamination, Monitoring

Nuclear site environmental remediation — Aldermaston, Burghfield, Rosyth, Devonport legacy contamination. Groundwater monitoring — tritium, uranium, americium. Soil remediation — contaminated land management under Environmental Permitting Regulations. Radiation monitoring — off-site dose assessment, environmental sampling programme. SSSI protection — Aldermaston site biodiversity. River Kennet water quality. Air emissions monitoring — stack discharges. Annual environmental report — public accountability. BNFL legacy sites cooperation.

Procurement

Nuclear Procurement — GOCO Model, Contract Management, Value for Money

Defence nuclear procurement — MOD's largest single equipment programme. AWE management contract — transition from GOCO (Government Owned, Contractor Operated) to MOD in-house. Dreadnought submarine build — BAE Systems, £31bn programme. Trident missile system — FMS (Foreign Military Sales) route through US Navy. Rolls-Royce reactor contracts — sole source, long-term partnering. Nuclear supply chain — UK SME integration, social value. SSRO (Single Source Regulations Office) oversight. Nuclear decommissioning contracts.

Emergency

Emergency Planning — REPPIR, Counter-Proliferation, Incident Response

Nuclear emergency planning — Radiation (Emergency Preparedness and Public Information) Regulations 2019. Emergency Planning Zone — 10km radius around AWE Aldermaston/Burghfield. Counter-proliferation — anti-smuggling, export controls, technology transfer restrictions. Incident response — nuclear accident, transport incident, radiological dispersal device. NARIC (National Arrangements for Incidents involving Radioactivity). Emergency exercises — regular testing of response plans. Public information — leaflet distribution, community engagement. International Atomic Energy Agency cooperation.

Data

Nuclear Material Accounting and Control — real-time inventory tracking. ONR information sharing — safety case data, inspection reports. 🚫 Red Lines (Never Crossed)

  • ❌ No nuclear weapon use without Prime Ministerial authorisation — UK nuclear deterrent operates under Prime Minister's sole authority via Letters of Last Resort; AI cannot influence or recommend nuclear weapon deployment decisions
  • ❌ No nuclear material accounting bypass — all Special Nuclear Material movements must be recorded in real-time; AI cannot override material accountancy controls or mask material balance anomalies
  • ❌ No safety case modification without ONR approval — nuclear safety cases at MOD-licensed sites require formal ONR consent for any modification; AI-generated safety case changes cannot be implemented without regulator sign-off
  • ❌ No nuclear facility access without appropriate vetting — all personnel accessing TOP SECRET nuclear material or facilities must hold current DV clearance; AI access control must not bypass vetting requirements
  • ❌ No nuclear data transfer across classification boundaries without proper authority — classified nuclear data (SECRET and above) cannot be processed by systems without appropriate accreditation; AI models must not train on classified nuclear data
  • ❌ No nuclear waste disposal without Environment Agency/SEPA permit — all nuclear waste management operations require regulatory permit; AI cannot authorise waste disposal or modify waste management plans without regulatory approval
