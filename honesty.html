The one-line state

13 of 14 slots are measured; one is gated, and here's why. The GSPC instrument is a 14-slot registry. 13 axes have quotable measured cells (usable n with a computed interval). One slot — the gated axis — is reported as UNMEASURED with its limits stated, never filled with an invented number. When the gate clears (usable n≥30 signed cells under the published method), the slot becomes measured. Until then it stays empty, honestly.

Human baselines

Published external cohorts, signed cells: MMLU expert ~89.8% · GPQA diamond PhD ~81% · ARC-AGI human ~85% (SWE-bench and MATH pinned at ingest). These are reported baselines from published external cohorts, not our own measurements — labelled as such on every surface. A model that beats the published human expert baseline on a governance-knowledge axis is measured against a number a buyer can read.

What we are not

  • Not a certification body. No cell is a certificate, approval, or conformity mark. A signed card is a measurement credential — evidence of what was measured and when, never a badge of approval.
  • Not a notified body. No EU/UK regulatory designation is claimed or implied.
  • No money either direction with anything ranked. Whoever pays (evidence-buyers: insurers, enterprises, regulators, researchers) never buys a placement, a rank, or a score change.
  • No BFT / 33-agent / council-vote claims. Measured effective independence is reported, never a voting fiction.
  • No vendor numbers echoed as ours. Every external figure is attributed and re-measured before it appears on our surfaces.

The limits of every number

A quotable cell means usable n with a computed interval — it is a measurement of one point in time, not a guarantee of behaviour. Overlapping CIs render "statistically indistinguishable", never fake ranks. An axis not measured is UNMEASURED: we never interpolate or average over a gap.
