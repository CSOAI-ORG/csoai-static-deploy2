The card algebra — measured, not asserted

Tested against the real 12-card board, not argued from analogy. The card knowledge-base is a CRDT-like commutative monoid: the same formal property distributed databases use to guarantee consistency without a coordinator — which is exactly what a sovereign, federated estate needs.

✓
Associative. Combining cards A then B then C equals A with (B then C) — merge grouping never changes the outcome.
✓
Idempotent. Re-merging an already-included card changes nothing — newest-supersedes is a safe update, replays are harmless.
✓
Timestamp-commutative & order-convergent. Any read or merge order reaches the identical final state. This is the formal guarantee behind "read newest-first and always land on the same truth".
✓
Valid metric space. 0 of 1,728 triangle-inequality checks violated — the GNN/clustering layer is mathematically grounded, and scores are totally ordered.

The boundary that keeps this honest: the algebra governs how cards combine — it cannot derive a measurement. You cannot algebra your way to a model's score; you still run the harness. Math applies to structure, convergence and embedding — never to generating the numbers.
