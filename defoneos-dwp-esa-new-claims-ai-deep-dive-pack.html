📋 12 Entry Points

Claims

New Claims Journey — Application, Eligibility, Assessment

New ESA claims via UC or legacy route. Eligibility: limited capability for work (LCW). GP fit note required. ESA50 questionnaire. Face-to-face or telephone WCA. Contribution-based ESA (Class 1 NI) or income-based (means-tested). UC migration pathway. Claim submission, evidence gathering, initial assessment decision. Digital-first journey with assisted digital support.

WCA

Work Capability Assessment — LCW/LCWRA Determination

WCA by Health Care Professional (HCP) from Maximus (CHAI). LCW: scored activities (15 descriptors, 15 points) or exceptional circumstances. LCWRA: severe conditions, 9 descriptors — risk to health, uncontrollable episodes, significant disability. Descriptors: physical and mental health. LCWRA automatically accepted for terminal illness (SR1). Mandatory reconsideration and appeal rights.

Assessment

Assessment Process — HCP, Evidence, Decision

Assessment provider: Maximus (Centre for Health and Disability Assessments). HCP qualification — doctor, nurse, physiotherapist, paramedic, occupational therapist. Evidence sources: GP, hospital consultant, specialist nurse, support worker. ESA50 self-assessment form. Face-to-face, telephone, or paper-based assessment. Decision Maker (DM) at DWP. Quality audit of HCP reports. Complaints and feedback mechanism.

Rates

Payment Rates — ESA Components, Premiums

Assessment rate: up to 13 weeks — £90.50/wk (25+), £71.70/wk (under-25). Main phase: LCW — no additional component (UC equivalent). LCWRA — additional component £416.19/mo (UC). ESA support group: £90.50/wk + support component £47.70/wk. WRAG: £90.50/wk + work-related activity component £47.70/wk (legacy). Premiums: severe disability, enhanced disability, carer. Christmas bonus. Pension age interaction.

Support Group

Support Group — LCWRA Conditions, Activities

Support group (legacy ESA) = LCWRA (UC). 9 descriptors: mobilising, transferring, reaching, picking up, eating/drinking, managing toilet needs, dressing/undressing, remaining conscious, communicating. Severe mental health conditions. Terminal illness (SR1). Learning disability. Significant risk to physical or mental health if required to work. No work-related requirements. Higher payment component. Permitted work allowed up to £183.50/wk.

WRAG

Work-Related Activity Group — LCW, Work Preparation

WRAG (legacy) = LCW (UC). Scored 15+ points on physical/mental descriptors. Work-focused interview requirement. Work preparation activity. Work-related activity plan. Conditionality — lighter touch than JSA. No sanctions in ESA WRAG (legacy). UC LCW: work preparation requirements only. Work coach support. Voluntary work, permitted work. Training and skills support. Health condition management.

Fit Notes

Fit Notes — GP Certification, Digital Fit Notes

Med3/Med10 fit note from GP or hospital doctor. Digital fit notes (April 2022). Fit note: 'not fit for work' or 'may be fit for work with adjustments'. Duration: 2 weeks to 3 months+. Repeat fit notes. Employer notification. DWP acceptance for ESA claim. Fit note does not determine WCA outcome — separate assessment. Abolition of sick notes for ESA (proposed reform). Fit note evidence for WCA. GPs not gatekeepers to benefits.

Migration

UC Migration — Legacy ESA to Universal Credit

Managed migration from income-based ESA to UC. Migration notice issued by DWP. 3-month deadline to claim UC. Transitional protection (TP) payments — SDP, enhanced disability premium. Natural migration via change of circumstances. Gateway conditions. TP erodes over time. Severe disability premium transitional element. Carer element. ESA contribution-based continues alongside UC. Run-on payments. Welfare rights adviser support.

Fraud

Fraud Detection — Overpayments, Error, Recovery

ESA fraud rate ~2.8%. Common fraud: undeclared partner income, capital limits, work capability misrepresentation. Detection via HMRC RTI, DWP data matching, UC cross-checks. Investigation officers. Civil penalty regime. Prosecution for deliberate fraud. Overpayment recovery from ongoing benefit. Voluntary disclosure. Error (official and claimant). Accuracy improvement programme. Counter-fraud strategy. Biometric verification.

Carers

Carers Interaction — Carer's Allowance, Carer Element

ESA and Carer's Allowance interaction — cannot receive both (overlapping benefits rule). Carer premium in income-based ESA. UC carer element £198.31/mo. Carer assessment for WCA — caring responsibilities considered. Carer's credit for NI. Carer's passport to other benefits. Young carers. Parent carers. Respite care interaction. Carer support services. DWP/Carers UK data sharing.

Data

Data Governance — Caseload, Outcomes, Statistics

~2.6M ESA claimants (legacy). ~1.8M in support group. WCA outcomes: ~60% LCWRA, ~15% LCW, ~25% fit for work. Average claim duration. Off-flows: employment, UC migration, reassessment. Reassessment cycle: 6-36 months. Appeal overturn rate ~70% (with representation). Statistical bulletins. FOI readiness. Caseload by region, condition, age group. Provider performance data. HCP report quality metrics.

Parliamentary

Parliamentary Evidence — Scrutiny, Reform, Evidence

W&P Select Committee evidence — WCA fitness for purpose, UC migration, ESA reassessment. PQ evidence: claimant numbers, WCA outcomes, appeal rates. Terminal illness fast-track (SR1). Mental health descriptors. Assessment provider performance. Cost of WCA programme. Sanctions in UC (not legacy ESA). Work capability reform proposals. ESA expenditure. Disabled people's employment rate. White Paper contributions.

⚡ 8 Transformation Priorities

1. Claim Routing

AI-powered routing: new ESA claim → UC or legacy pathway. Eligibility pre-check — NI contribution verification via HMRC RTI, income-based means test. Automatic fit note validation. Digital claim form auto-population from GP records. WCA referral automation. Evidence gathering assistant. Target: claim-to-assessment from 13 weeks to 6 weeks, 90% digital claims by 2028.

2. WCA Quality

HCP report quality scoring — descriptor accuracy, evidence consideration, reasoning quality. Bias detection in WCA outcomes — condition, demographic, geographic. Automated evidence cross-check against GP/hospital records. HCP performance dashboard. Report consistency audit. DM decision support. Appeal prediction model. Target: WCA overturn rate from 70% to under 30%, HCP report quality score above 90%.

3. Support Group Intelligence

LCWRA condition clustering — identify high-risk claimants. Terminal illness fast-track monitoring. Mental health descriptor analysis. Severe condition early identification. Support group exit pathway — voluntary work readiness. Health improvement tracking. Care plan integration. Target: zero delays for terminal illness claims, support group accuracy above 95%, voluntary employment uptake 20%.

4. Permitted Work

Permitted work eligibility monitoring — £183.50/wk threshold. Work start/end notification automation. Earnings verification via HMRC RTI. Impact on ESA payments calculation. Supported permitted work tracking. Transition planning to full employment. Employer engagement. Target: permitted work uptake from 3% to 12% of support group, zero overpayment from permitted work earnings.

5. UC Migration Support

Managed migration digital assistant — timeline, eligibility, TP calculator. SDP/SEAP transitional element calculation. Migration notice portal. Claim completion tracker. TP erode calculator. Legacy benefit interaction checker. Welfare rights adviser integration. Carer element assessment. Target: 100% managed migration completion, zero TP errors, migration satisfaction above 85%.

6. Fraud Detection

Real-time earnings matching via HMRC RTI. Capital limit monitoring. Partner income cross-check. Living together as married detection. Work capability misrepresentation flagging. Investigation prioritisation scoring. Recovery optimisation. Voluntary disclosure promotion. Target: fraud rate from 2.8% to under 1.5%, overpayment recovery rate above 85%, investigation-to-resolution under 90 days.

7. Carer Integration

Carer's Allowance interaction checker — overlapping benefits prevention. Carer element eligibility assessment. Caring hours verification. Carer's passport benefit routing. Respite care coordination. Young carer identification. Parent carer support. Carer-to-employment pathway. Target: zero carer benefit overlap errors, carer element uptake from 45% to 80%, carer employment support engagement 25%.

8. Parliamentary Intelligence

Real-time ESA dashboard — claimant numbers, WCA outcomes, support group/WRAG split, appeal rates. Automated W&P Committee reporting. PQ evidence generator — caseload stats, WCA performance, migration progress. Reassessment scrutiny pack. Terminal illness fast-track metrics. Target: monthly automated parliamentary briefing on ESA/WCA/UC migration performance.

🚫 Red Lines (Never Crossed)

  • ❌ No WCA without HCP assessment — every work capability assessment must be conducted or reviewed by a qualified Health Care Professional; AI may assist but cannot replace clinical judgment
  • ❌ No delayed payments — ESA payments must be made within statutory timeframes; any delay requires immediate claimant notification with reason and resolution date
  • ❌ No reassessment of terminal illness — SR1 terminal illness claims are fast-tracked to LCWRA with no further assessment; no review or reassessment permitted
  • ❌ No UC migration without TP calculation — every managed migration from legacy ESA must include transitional protection assessment before legacy benefit termination
  • ❌ No fraud investigation without notification — claimants must be informed of fraud investigation before any benefit suspension or recovery action is initiated
  • ❌ No SGA restriction without earnings evidence — substantial gainful activity restriction requires documented HMRC earnings evidence, not assumption or inference
