ProvBench

Research question: does an EU AI Act Article 50 provenance marking survive real-world transforms? ProvBench is a pre-registered benchmark — every prediction was written down before measurement, and disagreements are published.

Headline measured result

  • 0 of 20 assets' embedded manifests survived any of the measured transform cells (0 of 180 cells, manifest_present, embedded-only configuration).
  • One-sided 95% Clopper–Pearson upper bound on per-asset survival: 13.9% (asset-clustered).
  • Sidecar configuration: 100% survival — the embedded/sidecar gap is separated by +100 points with non-overlapping clustered confidence intervals.

Conclusion: embedded metadata does not survive the real world. If Article 50 compliance matters to you, the marking architecture — not the model — is the decision.
