Article 43 — internal control, done with evidence

EU AI Act Article 43 allows most Annex III systems to use the internal-control conformity assessment procedure — self-assessment. The law permits the route; what has been missing is credible self-assessment evidence.

What credible self-assessment looks like

  1. Frozen harness — the tests are published before the run, so the run cannot be tuned to pass.
  2. Deterministic execution — pinned seeds, published splits, confidence intervals on every figure.
  3. Signed artefacts — Ed25519-signed result records a third party can verify without trusting us.
  4. Corpus-watch — when the provision text changes, the affected measurements re-fire. Self-assessment that goes stale is not assessment.

Boundary
