⚡ 8 Transformation Priorities

1. Digital Backbone First

Cloud-native infrastructure across all Army formations. Edge computing at section level. Tactical networking modernisation. Data-centric operations baseline.

2. Human-Machine Teaming

Robot wingman at platoon level. Autonomous logistics at company level. Swarm capability at battalion level. Human-in-the-loop governance throughout.

3. Soldier Lethality

AI-assisted target acquisition. Ballistic computation in real-time. Electronic warfare protection. Enhanced night operations. Precision engagement for dismounted infantry.

4. Training Revolution

VR/AR simulation replacing live-fire ranges. Adaptive training pathways. Combat readiness AI scoring. Synthetic environment at every barracks.

5. Welfare & Ethics

Mental health early-warning system. PTSD prevention through workload monitoring. Ethical AI governance for autonomous systems. Dignity in digital surveillance.

6. Recruitment Modernisation

AI-driven recruitment targeting. Skills-matching for digital roles. Retention prediction. Diversity analytics. Career pathway optimisation.

7. Parliamentary Accountability

Real-time programme dashboard. Capability delivery milestones. Procurement transparency. Spending metrics. Select Committee-ready reporting.

8. AUKUS Interoperability

Common data standards with US/AU allies. Interoperable autonomous systems. Joint training simulations. Shared threat libraries. Secure coalition networking.

🚫 Red Lines (Never Crossed)

  • ❌ No autonomous lethal engagement without human-in-the-loop authorisation — every engagement requires human decision
  • ❌ No soldier biometric surveillance without consent and welfare benefit — wearable data serves the soldier first
  • ❌ No AI-driven recruitment discrimination — algorithmic fairness testing required on all recruitment models
  • ❌ No mental health data used against soldiers — welfare AI serves wellbeing, never career penalty
  • ❌ No autonomous system deployed without safety certification and ethical review board approval
  • ❌ No training simulation without informed consent and opt-out for psychologically distressing scenarios
