⛔ 6 Sovereign Red Lines

  • 🚫 No Senedd legislative competence ruling automated without Llywydd (Presiding Officer) and Counsel General human review — AI assists, never decides.
  • 🚫 No NHS Wales clinical decision initiated by AI without consultant clinician sign-off — patient safety is sovereign human territory.
  • 🚫 No Welsh language policy recommendation made without Welsh Language Commissioner approval and bilingual community consultation.
  • 🚫 No fiscal framework negotiation position recommended without Welsh Government Finance Minister and Treasury bilateral approval.
  • 🚫 No Future Generations Act compliance scoring published without Future Generations Commissioner review and public body right of response.
  • 🚫 No cross-border data sharing between Welsh and English systems without existing inter-governmental agreement and DPIA compliance.
