Enforcement notices, criminal prosecution. Annual compliance report. Water Supply (Water Quality) Regulations 2016. Inspector audit programme. Sampling frequency schedules.

🚫 Red Lines (Never Crossed)

  • ❌ No water supply without verified compliance across all 48 DWI parameters — every parameter must meet limits before water reaches consumers
  • ❌ No reduction in monitoring frequency below regulatory minimum without DWI approval — sampling schedules non-negotiable without formal consent
  • ❌ No consumer notification delayed beyond 24hrs for boil water notices — public health demands immediate communication of contamination risk
  • ❌ No lead pipe left in service beyond 2036 target without approved deferral — 10ppb limit is a hard deadline requiring DWI-approved exception
  • ❌ No emerging contaminant risk dismissed without evidence-based assessment — PFAS, microplastics, pharmaceuticals require proactive evaluation
  • ❌ No private supply data shared without local authority data-sharing agreement — GDPR and Water Industry Act mandate formal data governance
