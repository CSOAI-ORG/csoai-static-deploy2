📋 12 Entry Points

Claims

Claims Journey — Digital Application, ID Verification, Journal

UC digital-first claim via GOV.UK. Identity verification through GOV.Verify or in-Jobcentre alternative. Online claim form: personal details, housing, children, health, work history. Joint claim for couples. Claimant commitment created at first interview. Digital journal for communication. Assisted digital support at Jobcentre Plus. Paper claims available for exceptional circumstances. Claim date determines first assessment period. Rapid reclaim within 6 months. Linked claims for bereavement.

Payments

Payment Cycle — Monthly Assessment Periods, 5-Week Wait, Advance Payments

Monthly assessment periods — fixed calendar dates. Standard allowance: single 25+ £393.45/mo, couple 25+ £617.60/mo. First payment after 5-week wait — 1 month assessment period + 7 days processing. Advance payments available — up to 100% of estimated UC, repaid over 24 months. Alternative payment arrangements: fortnightly, housing paid direct. Hardship payments. Payment dates adjustable. Bank account verification. Payment on non-banking days.

Work

Work Allowances — Earnings Taper, Work Conditionality Groups

Work allowances: no housing element — £379/mo (lower), £631/mo (higher); with housing element — £379/mo (lower), £0 (higher, abolished for housing). Taper rate: 55% of net earnings above work allowance deducted from UC. Conditionality groups: all work-related requirements, work-focused interviews, work preparation, no requirements. Work coach assigns group. In-work conditionality — earnings threshold (AET). Minimum income floor for self-employed. Couples: both partners assessed.

Housing

Housing Element — LHA Rates, Direct Payments to Landlords

Housing element: Local Housing Allowance (LHA) rates for private rented sector. Social housing: actual rent (subject to bedroom tax/removal of spare room subsidy). Direct payments to landlords — claimant pays rent, or landlord receives direct if arrears or vulnerability. LHA rates set annually, frozen 2020-2024, reset 2024. Shared accommodation rate for under-35s. Benefit cap interaction. Housing costs for temporary accommodation. Service charges. Non-dependant deductions. Supported housing exemption.

Childcare

Childcare Element — 85% Costs, Max 2 Children, Work Requirement

Childcare element: up to 85% of eligible childcare costs. Maximum: £1,014.63/mo for 1 child, £1,739.37/mo for 2+ children. Registered/approved childcare providers only. Both parents (or lone parent) must be in work. 'Two-child limit' — no UC child element for 3rd+ child born after April 2017 (exceptions: multiple births, adoption, kinship care). Child element: £333.33/mo per child. Disabled child element. Free childcare hours interaction (15/30 hours). Upfront childcare costs — claim before starting work.

Health

Health Journey — WCA, Fit Notes, LCW/LCWRA

Health journey: GP fit note triggers WCA referral. ESA50 questionnaire. Work Capability Assessment by assessment provider (Maximus/CHAI). Limited Capability for Work (LCW): scored activities, 15 points. LCWRA: severe conditions — 9 descriptors, risk to health, uncontrollable episodes. LCWRA component: £416.19/mo. No work-related requirements for LCWRA. LCW: work preparation only. WCA reassessment cycle. Mandatory reconsideration and appeal rights. Terminal illness fast-track (SR1). Mental health descriptors. Fit note evidence integration.

Sanctions

Sanctions — Conditionality, Compliance, Hardship Payments

UC sanctions regime: failure to comply with claimant commitment. Sanction levels: low, medium, high, highest. Highest level sanction: 3+ years (refusing employment without good reason). Higher-level sanctions: leaving employment voluntarily, failure to accept offer of work. Lower-level: missed appointment, failure to participate. Compliance interview before sanction. Hardship payments available during sanctions. Sanction referral to Decision Maker. Good reason exceptions. Vulnerable claimant protections. Sanction rate ~4% of UC claimants. Appeal rights.

Migration

Migration — Legacy to UC Managed Migration, Transitional Protection

Managed migration: DWP issues migration notice to legacy benefit claimants. 3-month deadline to claim UC. Transitional protection (TP) payments — ensures no cash loss at point of migration. TP elements: SDP, enhanced disability premium, child additions, carer premium. TP erodes over time as UC rates increase. Natural migration via change of circumstances. Voluntary migration. Migration timetable — completion by 2028/29. ESA contribution-based continues alongside UC. Run-on payments for 2 weeks. Welfare rights adviser support. TP calculator.

Fraud

Overpayments — Fraud, Error, Recovery, Civil Penalties

UC fraud and error: ~4.9% of total UC expenditure. Common fraud: undeclared earnings, living together as married, undeclared capital. Detection via HMRC RTI (Real Time Information), DWP data matching, third-party intelligence. Overpayment recovery: deduction from ongoing UC (up to 25%), direct earnings attachment, civil money proceedings. Civil penalty: £50 for fraud. Prosecution for deliberate fraud. Official error recovery. Claimant error. Voluntary disclosure. Counter-fraud strategy. Benefit fraud hotline. Fraud investigation process.

Digital

Digital Journal — UC Online Account, Messaging, Commitments

UC online account: GOV.UK sign-in. Digital journal: claimant-to-work coach messaging, appointment tracking, payment history, claimant commitment view. Document upload. Change of circumstances reporting. To-do list. Payment statements. Sanction notifications. Work search activity log. Journal as evidence in appeals. Mobile accessibility. Assisted digital for digitally excluded claimants. UC full service vs legacy service. Account security. Data retention. Journal entries as official record.

Data

Data Governance — Claimant Data, Analytics, Caseload Statistics

UC caseload: ~6.2M households (2025). UC expenditure: ~£80bn/year. Statistical releases: monthly caseload, demographics, conditionality groups, sanctions, payments. FOI readiness. Data protection (UK GDPR). Claimant data sharing with HMRC, local authorities, NHS. Analytics: payment accuracy, fraud rates, WCA outcomes, work coach caseload. UC dashboard. DWP management information. Research publications. UC experimental statistics. Open data commitments. Data retention policy (6 years).

Parliamentary

Parliamentary — W&P Committee, PQs, Reform

Work and Pensions Select Committee scrutiny — UC design, 5-week wait, sanctions, migration. Parliamentary Questions: caseload, expenditure, fraud, WCA outcomes, migration progress. UC reform proposals: removing 2-child limit, reducing taper rate, increasing work allowances. National Audit Office reports. Social Security Advisory Committee. UC evaluation programme. Benefit cap reviews. LHA uprating. Childcare element adequacy. Universal Support. UC and devolution (Scotland, Northern Ireland). White Paper contributions.

⚡ 8 Transformation Priorities

Digital-first UC claim journey — AI eligibility routing, auto-population from HMRC/DWP records. ID verification: biometric, GOV.Verify, in-Jobcentre face-to-face. Claim form intelligent pre-fill from existing DWP data. AI triage: route claimants to correct conditionality group. Document upload AI validation. Joint claim automation for couples. Target: claim completion time from 45 minutes to 15 minutes, 95% digital claims by 2028, zero incomplete claims reaching work coaches.

2. Payment Accuracy

Real-time earnings matching via HMRC RTI — prevent overpayments from delayed employer reporting. Assessment period earnings allocation AI. Capital limit monitoring. Housing element verification against local authority data. Childcare cost verification against provider records. Payment calculation audit trail. Overpayment prevention scoring. Target: fraud and error from 4.9% to under 2.5%, overpayment recovery rate above 90%, zero payment processing errors from RTI data.

3. Work Coach Intelligence

4. Housing Verification

Landlord/tenancy AI validation — cross-check against local authority housing registers, landlord registries. LHA rate auto-application. 5. Health Assessment Quality

Automated evidence cross-check against GP/hospital records. HCP performance dashboard. Report consistency audit. DM decision support. Appeal prediction model. Reassessment scheduling optimisation. Target: WCA overturn rate from 70% to under 30%, HCP report quality score above 90%, WCA-to-decision time from 12 weeks to 6 weeks.

6. Fraud Detection

Multi-source data matching: HMRC RTI, bank data, local authority, NHS, DVLA. Behavioural analysis — spending patterns, account activity, lifestyle indicators. Living together as married detection — shared addresses, financial links, utility accounts. Capital limit monitoring — property, savings, investments. Self-employment earnings verification. Investigation prioritisation scoring. Target: UC fraud rate from 4.9% to under 2.0%, detection-to-resolution under 60 days, false positive rate below 5%.

7. Migration Support

Managed migration digital assistant — timeline, eligibility, TP calculator. SDP/SEAP transitional element calculation. Legacy benefit interaction checker — identify overlaps, conflicts, underpayments. Migration notice portal — claim progress tracker. TP erode calculator — forecast future UC amounts. Welfare rights adviser integration. Carer element assessment. Run-on payment automation. Target: 100% managed migration completion by 2029, zero TP calculation errors, migration satisfaction above 85%, zero cash-loss at migration point.

Real-time UC dashboard — caseload, expenditure, conditionality groups, sanctions, WCA outcomes, migration progress. Automated W&P Committee reporting packs. PQ evidence generator — caseload stats, fraud rates, payment accuracy, childcare uptake. NAO audit-ready data. 🚫 Red Lines (Never Crossed)

  • ❌ No benefit suspension without notification — UC payments cannot be suspended or reduced without prior written notification to the claimant via journal or post, with reason, amount, and appeal rights
  • ❌ No sanctions without compliance interview — every UC sanction must be preceded by a compliance interview where the claimant can explain good reason; AI may flag but cannot sanction
  • ❌ No WCA without qualified HCP — every work capability assessment must be conducted or reviewed by a qualified Health Care Professional; AI may assist but cannot replace clinical judgment
  • ❌ No migration without TP calculation — every managed migration from legacy benefits to UC must include transitional protection assessment before legacy benefit termination
  • ❌ No advance repayment causing hardship — advance payment deductions must not cause the claimant to fall below the hardship threshold; repayment schedules must be affordable
  • ❌ No fraud investigation without claimant notification — claimants must be informed of fraud investigation before any benefit suspension, recovery action, or civil penalty is initiated
