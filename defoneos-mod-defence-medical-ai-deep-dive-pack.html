🚫 Red Lines (Never Crossed)

  • ❌ No automated medical discharge without medical officer review, second opinion, and appeal pathway
  • ❌ No mental health diagnosis override without psychiatrist and multidisciplinary team assessment
  • ❌ No AFCS/War Pension denial without human compensation case officer review and tribunal right
  • ❌ No combat casualty triage override without deployed medical officer determination
  • ❌ No DMICP record access for non-clinical purposes without Caldicott guardian approval
  • ❌ No pre-deployment fitness assessment override without senior medical officer and commanding officer
