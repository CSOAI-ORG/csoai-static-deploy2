📋 12 Entry Points

Assessment

LCW/LCWRA Assessment — WCA AI for ESA

WCA scoring against LCW and LCWRA descriptors. 15-point threshold for LCW. LCWRA categories — mobilising, continence, coping, substantial risk. Descriptors across physical, mental, cognitive, sensory functions. Majority of days rule. Terminal illness fast-track (SR1). WCA outcome routing to UC or ESA requirements. Mandatory reconsideration before appeal. Assessment provider management (Maximus/Capita). Risk-stratified reassessment.

Eligibility

Contribution-Based ESA — NI Contribution Test, New-Style ESA

New-style ESA requiring Class 1 or Class 2 NI contributions. Two tax-year contribution window — Year 1 and Year 2. LEL sufficient contributions test. Credits-only exclusion from contribution-based ESA. Income-based ESA phased out in UC full-service areas. NI verification via HMRC RTI. Self-employed Class 2 route. Overlapping benefit rule with contributory JSA and IB.

Migration

UC Transition Protection — ESA to UC Managed Migration

Managed migration under UC (Managed Migration Pilot and Rollout) Regulations 2019. Transitional protection — difference between ESA award and UC entitlement at migration. SDP gateway — SDP element added to UC transitional component. Erosion rules — no real-terms increase. Minimum 1-month notice. Legacy benefit termination on deadline. Natural migration detection on change of circumstances. Voluntary migration path. WRAG to UC LCW. Support to UC LCWRA.

Work Groups

WRAG to LCW — Work-Related Activity Group Transition

ESA WRAG component (£90.50/wk) mapped to UC LCW element (nil after April 2017 for new claims). WRAG claimants with LCW descriptor migrating to UC with work-related requirements. WFI requirement carryover. Work preparation plan transfer. WRAG to LCWRA re-referral on health deterioration. 365-day contributory WRAG time-limiting. Permitted work carryover (£167/wk). Compliance and sanction transition. Safeguarding for complex needs.

Enhanced Protection

Support Group to LCWRA — Enhanced Protection

Support Group migrating to UC LCWRA with enhanced transitional protection. LCWRA element (£416.19/mo) plus transitional top-up. No reassessment during managed migration. Descriptors mapped to LCWRA — mobilising, continence, coping, substantial risk. SR1 fast-track retaining LCWRA without WCA. 24-month reassessment protection. GP/consultant evidence preservation. Rights briefing. Overlapping disability benefit interaction (PIP, AA).

Work Rules

Permitted Work — Supported Work, Limits, Disregard

Permitted work lower limit (£167/wk) — up to 16 hours/week. Higher limit (£183.50/wk) — LCW only, not LCWRA. Supported permitted work — no limit for supervised programme. ESA earnings disregard (£20/wk for WRAG). UC transition carryover. Therapeutic work exception. WFI trigger after 26 weeks. Supported employment accreditation. HMRC RTI verification.

Fraud

ESA Fraud Detection — Overpayment, Identity, Compliance

Overpayment detection via HMRC RTI cross-match. DWP CIS identity verification. NI contribution fraud — fictitious employer flags. WCA fraud — inconsistent medical evidence and GP cross-check. Concurrent ESA/UC detection. Permitted work exceedance monitoring. Compliance interview trigger from analytics. Prosecution evidence chain. Civil penalty for negligent misrepresentation. Recovery from UC transitional protection.

Digital

Work Coach Digital — WFI Scheduling, Action Plans

Digital WFI scheduling for WRAG/LCW. Online journal for ESA-to-UC migrated claimants. Action plan builder with condition-aware goals. Digital fit note submission. Video WCA option. Claimant portal for permitted work reporting. Automated compliance notifications. Multi-channel — web, phone, face-to-face. Accessibility-first design.

Mental Health

Mental Health — WCA Descriptors, Fluctuating Conditions

Mental health descriptors — initiating personal action (14), social engagement (15), getting about (16), coping with change (17), going out (18). Fluctuating condition rule — majority of days. Evidence weighting — psychiatrist, CPN, CMHT. Cognitive descriptors — memory, concentration, executive function. Substantial risk test. Specialist assessor. Tribunal medical member. 12-36 month reassessment. NHS records integration (with consent).

Carer

Carer Premium — ESA Carer Premium, CA Interaction

ESA carer premium (£42.75/wk) for CA recipients. Carryover to UC carer element (£198.31/mo). SDP interaction during migration. CA earnings limit (£151/wk) compliance. Underlying entitlement when overlapping benefit prevents payment. Permitted work — carer as limited capability. CA and ESA support group dual entitlement. UC transition protection. Young carer pathway for under-25s.

Data

ESA Data Governance — Caseload, UC Migration Tracking

ESA caseload monitoring (~2.4M legacy). UC migration tracking — managed, natural, voluntary. WCA outcomes — LCW, LCWRA, fit for work by condition group. Contribution vs income-based split. WRAG 365-day time-limit tracking. UC migration completion by region. Quarterly statistics publication. SDP gateway analytics. Overpayment and fraud recovery metrics.

Parliamentary

Parliamentary Reform — ESA Policy Evidence, WCA Review

Policy reform evidence from UC migration outcomes. WCA descriptor review against disability prevalence. Independent review recommendations. UC managed migration Parliamentary scrutiny. LCW element restoration — £0 for new UC claimants. SDP gateway legislative review. Contribution-based reform — NI credit-only exclusion. Appeals data for Parliamentary questions. Cross-party briefing packs. International comparison with EU disability models.

⚡ 8 Transformation Priorities

1. WCA Digital Transformation

End-to-end digital WCA with remote assessment option. AI-assisted descriptor scoring from medical evidence. Digital fit note integration. Fluctuating condition automation via majority-of-days rule. Full audit trail through mandatory reconsideration to tribunal. Target: 80% digital-first by 2028, reducing average time from 14 weeks to 6 weeks.

2. UC Managed Migration Automation

Automated transitional protection calculation. SDP gateway verification and auto-enrolment. Migration notice scheduling with 1-month lead time. Legacy benefit termination with zero-gap protection. Natural migration detection from CoC triggers. Cohort analytics and completion tracking. Target: 100% completion by 2028 with zero income gap exceeding 1 assessment period.

3. Contribution-Based Eligibility AI

Real-time NI verification via HMRC RTI. Two-year contribution window validation against LEL. Self-employed Class 2 auto-check. Credits-only claimant detection and UC routing. Contribution gap identification with voluntary credit guidance. Overlapping benefit detection (contributory JSA, IB). Target: 48-hour eligibility determination for 95% of new-style ESA claims.

4. Mental Health Descriptor Reform

Specialist mental health assessor framework. Fluctuating condition evidence — GP, CPN, CMHT. Substantial risk automation from clinical records. Cognitive descriptor expansion for neurodevelopmental conditions. 12-36 month reassessment. Tribunal medical member requirement. Target: Reduce mental health WCA overturn rate from 73% to below 30%.

5. Permitted Work Automation

Real-time HMRC earnings monitoring. Automated threshold alerts (£167/£183.50 per week). Supported work provider accreditation. Permitted work-to-UC carryover rules. WFI scheduling after 26-week trigger. Therapeutic work exception verification. Target: Zero false-positive permitted work breaches via automated RTI monitoring.

6. Fraud Detection Modernisation

HMRC RTI earnings cross-match for concurrent ESA/UC fraud. DWP CIS identity verification. NI contribution anomaly detection. WCA evidence inconsistency flagging. Compliance interview AI triage. Overpayment recovery from UC transitional protection. Target: Detect 95% of overpayments within first period, reducing average debt from £3,200 to under £800.

7. Cross-Benefit Single Record

Single record across ESA, UC, PIP, Carer's Allowance, Housing Benefit. Automated eligibility cascade from WCA outcome. SDP and carer premium auto-calculation during migration. No claimant navigating multiple systems. Legacy-to-UC transition with full data carryover. Target: Zero data re-entry during ESA to UC migration.

8. Parliamentary Evidence Pipeline

Automated ESA quarterly statistics. UC migration dashboard for Select Committee. WCA descriptor review evidence from analytics. LCW element restoration fiscal modelling. SDP migration Parliamentary reporting. Cross-party briefing pack generator. Target: Real-time Parliamentary reporting with quarterly automated briefing packs.

🚫 Red Lines (Never Crossed)

  • ❌ No ESA claimant assessed without right to submit medical evidence — every claimant must have opportunity to provide GP, consultant, specialist evidence before WCA determination
  • ❌ No WCA decision without reconsideration right within 1 month — mandatory reconsideration offered in writing with clear appeal route to First-tier Tribunal
  • ❌ No UC migration without confirmed transitional protection — every managed migration must include verified amount before legacy benefit termination
  • ❌ No mental health descriptor applied without specialist assessment option — claimants with mental health conditions must be offered assessment by mental health-qualified professional
  • ❌ No permitted work limit enforced without supported employment pathway — claimants breaching limits must be offered supported employment referral before sanctions
  • ❌ No fraud finding without verified NI/HMRC evidence chain — every fraud determination must be backed by HMRC-verified contribution or earnings evidence
