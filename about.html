About CSOAI

CSOAI (Council of Sovereign AI) is an independent measurement body for AI compliance with statute. We publish measurement harnesses, run them, and sign every result so a third party can verify the number without trusting us.

What we are

  • A measurement body. A published harness + a signed result + a public recompute path. Every figure on this site traces to those three things.
  • UK-incorporated. CSOAI Ltd · UK company 16939677. The measurement runs on free-tier substrate (HF free, Kaggle T4, Kaggle free, Groq free, Ollama). The harness is on GitHub.
  • Independent of the regulator and the regulated. We do not certify, we do not accredit, we do not designate. Where the law requires a notified body, we hand the harness to them; we do not pretend to be one.

What we measure

  • EU AI Act — Regulation 2024/1689. 417 statutory provisions crosswalked across the EU, UK and US. Per-Article walk on the hub.
  • EU Cyber Resilience Act — Regulation 2024/2847. Connected products with digital elements. See CRA.
  • EU AI Act + Machinery Regulation interaction — Reg 2026/1744. One gate, not two.
  • UK GDPR + DPA 2018 — crosswalked to the EU AI Act where the same data subject is engaged.
  • US NIST AI RMF 1.0 + Colorado SB 24-205 — high-risk consumer AI.
  • International — ISO/IEC 42001, ISO/IEC 23894, C2PA §2.4, Council of Europe AI Framework Convention.

Operating principles

  1. Measured, not claimed. Every number on this site is a measurement on a published harness. A claim with no measurement is retracted.
  2. Recompute-able, not trusted. The harness is open. Given the inputs, you can rerun the measurement and get the same number up to model variability.
  3. Signed, not asserted. Every result is Ed25519-signed. The public key is at /.well-known/agent.json.
  4. Statute-anchored, not opinion. The items are rooted in the article text of the regulation. We measure against statute; we do not opine on what statute means.

Programme: DEFONEOS

DEFONEOS is the public-facing programme of UK-sector deep-dive packs. Each pack covers a single UK public body (a regulator, a department, an inspectorate) via 12 entry points × 8 priorities × 6 MCPs. The packs are shipped at defoneos.html and on csoai.org/defoneos.
