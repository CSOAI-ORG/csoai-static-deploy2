🚫 Red Lines (Never Crossed)

  • ❌ No flood warning suppressed or delayed regardless of confidence level
  • ❌ No development approved in Flood Zone 3 without Environment Agency and LLFA written consent
  • ❌ No automated flood defence control without human override capability within 30 seconds
  • ❌ No vulnerable person data shared between agencies without GDPR lawful basis and individual notification
  • ❌ No managed realignment without community consultation and compensation framework
  • ❌ No nature-based solution deployed without hydrological modelling proving equivalent protection standard
