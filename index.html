How to check us

Three steps. No permission needed.

We do not ask you to trust us. We give you the receipts and the key, and you check the chain yourself.

1

We measure

Frozen, published benchmarks against a deterministic gate. No model judges another, no vendor pays to be ranked.

2

We sign

A 3KB card, Ed25519-signed and timestamp-anchored. The harness ships with the card, so the run is reproducible.

3

You verify

Client-side, loginless, against the published key. If you can check it, it is evidence; if you cannot, it is not.

Our own hardware

Measured on our own fleet

We do not rent judgement. The measurements run on hardware we control — arena workers, heavy workers and the always-free substrate — with live status from our fleet API.

Open-source measurement stack

The receipt format is public. The axes are not.

Signed measurement should be reproducible anywhere, by anyone — without paying or trusting us. So the signing spine and the deterministic harness skeletons are MIT-licensed and open: three packages, all E2E-verified, all with an honest UNSIGNED path when no key is configured.

inspect-signed-receipt

Ed25519-signed, hash-chained receipts for Inspect (UK AISI's standard eval framework) — the signing spine. No fork.
