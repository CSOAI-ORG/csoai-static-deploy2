How to check us

Three steps. No permission needed.

We do not ask you to trust us. We give you the receipts and the key, and you check the chain yourself.

1

We measure

Frozen, published benchmarks against a deterministic gate. No model judges another, no vendor pays to be ranked.

2

We sign

A 3KB card, Ed25519-signed and timestamp-anchored. The harness ships with the card, so the run is reproducible.

3

You verify

Client-side, loginless, against the published key. If you can check it, it is evidence; if you cannot, it is not.
