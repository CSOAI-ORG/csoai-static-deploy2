The GSPC instrument

Describe an AI system and the instrument reads it against frozen statutory provisions across four lenses — Governance, Safety, Provenance, Continuity — and tells you which bind, deterministically, with the provision cited. There is no inference call in the verdict path: the score is a predicate an auditor can recompute from the published harness.

An axis that cannot be measured is reported UNMEASURED — never coerced to a score. That honesty is the point: a benchmark whose first result flatters its author is not a benchmark.

The signed-measurement spine

Every result rides one spine: measure → gate → sign → time-anchor → verify → track drift.

  • Correctness gate — a wrong answer cannot leave the pipeline carrying a clean receipt. Legal claims are checked against exact statute; an ungrounded claim is attested but not verified, and cannot be signed as a clean measurement.
  • Signed cards — every measurement is Ed25519-signed and carries an RFC-3161 time anchor, so anyone can recompute the content and confirm when it was issued.
  • Drift-triggered re-attestation — when a regulation changes, every card that depended on the old statute text is flagged stale and queued for re-measurement. Living compliance, fail-closed: an unknown regulation is never reported as unchanged.
  • Agent-callable — an A2A agent can call the measurement and walk away with a signed measurement credential (never a certificate).

The Council — and its measured limits

The Council structure records governance decisions across a 33-seat panel with public minutes. We publish what we measure about it, including where it falls short: the panel's measured effective independence is low (correlated judges), so we do not claim Byzantine fault tolerance — an earlier claim to that effect was retracted and is not made here. Publishing our own resolution limit is the moat, not a footnote.

The MCP measurement stack

A stack of open, inspectable MCP servers that consume the spine as a signed attestation service — governance crosswalk, AI-bill-of-materials, injection scanning, Article 50 watermarking, and regulatory-drift watching. Each is a consumer of the neutral spine; none of them certifies anyone.

Honest by design

We show what is true and verifiable, not badges we don't hold. We are not a notified body and do not issue certificates; formal accreditations (e.g. ISO/IEC 42001) are a separate, multi-year track pursued as the platform matures. Rule-based tools defined solely by natural persons fall outside the Article 3(1) definition (Recital 12) — we say so rather than leave it unsaid.
