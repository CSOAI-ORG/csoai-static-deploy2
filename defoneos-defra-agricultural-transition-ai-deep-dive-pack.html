⚡ 8 Transformation Priorities

1. Farmer-First Transition

No farmer left behind in BPS to ELM transition. Adequate transition support. Simplified scheme design. Digital and non-digital access.

2. Environmental Outcome Payments

Payments linked to verified environmental outcomes. Not area-based. Measurable. Auditable. Preventing greenwashing.

3. Food Security Balance

Environmental goals balanced with food production. No blanket land-use mandates. Productive agriculture maintained where sustainable.

4. Digital-First with Inclusion

Digital scheme management as default. But paper and phone access maintained. No digital exclusion of rural communities.

5. Data Sovereignty

Farm data remains farmer-owned. No compulsory data sharing. Privacy-preserving analytics. Transparent data governance.

6. Innovation Enablement

Agri-tech adoption supported not mandated. Knowledge transfer funded. Small farm access to precision agriculture. Innovation without dependency.

7. Carbon Accountability

Agricultural carbon measured not just claimed. Verified sequestration. Preventing offset fraud. Net Zero pathway with rural reality.

8. Parliamentary Transparency

Real-time transition dashboard. Budget accountability. Environmental outcome reporting. Independent review. Select Committee readiness.

🚫 Red Lines (Never Crossed)

  • ❌ No BPS payment withdrawn without verified alternative ELM scheme access and transition support
  • ❌ No environmental outcome payment without independent verification and anti-fraud checks
  • ❌ No compulsory farm data sharing without explicit farmer consent and data sovereignty protection
  • ❌ No land use mandate without food security impact assessment and farmer consultation
  • ❌ No carbon offset claim without verified sequestration measurement and third-party audit
  • ❌ No digital-only scheme access without maintained non-digital alternatives for rural exclusion prevention
