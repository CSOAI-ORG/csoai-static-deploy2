🎯 Onboarding Process (5 Steps)

Express Interest

Choose your category and tier. 6 categories: Sovereign Operators (30d), Enterprise Customers (14d), Regulator Partners (90d), Academic Partners (60d), Sovereign Cloud Partners (45d), Defence Partners (180d). 4 tiers: Bronze free, Silver 1%, Gold 3%, Platinum 5%. SLA varies by category.

API: POST /v1/partner/onboard

Sign Charter Article 0 (Binding)

Ed25519-sign the charter. Signature published to proofof.ai/verify/partners/{partner_did}. Use treaty_generator.py tool for full treaty document.

Requirement: You accept the Article 0 binding. You cannot opt-out. To amend Article 0: 33/33 BFT + 5 human sigs.

BFT Council Approval

Proposal submitted via sovereign BFT council. Quorum required based on category (23/33 for Bronze/Silver/Gold, 33/33 for Platinum). SLA: 14-180 days depending on category.

Track your proposal status at vote-tally.html.

4-Tier Certification (Foundation → Director)

Complete all 4 certification tiers for at least the Foundation level. Each tier: Ed25519-signed, BFT-ratified, OTS-Bitcoin-anchored. Cost: 100% free.

Use cert-verification.html to view the workflow + training-tier-catalog.html for the 164 pathways.

Partner Onboarded

Receive: sovereign brand license, MCP infrastructure access, Watchdog signal access, SIGIL chain read/write, cross-certification rights. Plus Ongoing: 6-month reviews, Article 0 binding monitoring, partner tier progression.

Access all features via REST API.
