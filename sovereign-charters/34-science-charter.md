### UE5 Simulations
1. **The Peer Review**: You're reviewing a high-impact paper. Navigate the methodology, check the statistics, verify the data availability, assess the reproducibility, and write the review. The paper has a subtle statistical flaw — can you find it?
2. **The Reproducibility Challenge**: 10 papers from a high-profile journal. Attempt to reproduce each one. Access the data (or hit paywalls). Run the analyses. Document what replicates and what doesn't.
3. **The AI-Generated Paper**: A suspiciously perfect paper lands on your desk. The prose is flawless, the statistics are textbook, but something feels wrong. Investigate: is this AI-generated? What are the tells? What's the policy?
4. **The Research Fraud Case**: A prominent researcher's data shows impossible patterns (too-perfect distributions, duplicated images). Build the case: evidence gathering, statistical forensics, institutional reporting, retraction management.
