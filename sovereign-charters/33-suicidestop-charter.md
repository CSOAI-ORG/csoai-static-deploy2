## ARTICLE II — DOMAIN
### Scope
SuicideStop provides AI-assisted crisis resource routing: connecting individuals in distress to the appropriate crisis service based on location, language, age, and specific needs. Routes to: Samaritans (116 123 — UK/ROI, 24/7), Crisis Text Line/SHOUT (85258 — UK, 24/7 text), NHS 111 (Option 2 — mental health), CALM (0800 58 58 58 — UK, 5pm-midnight), Papyrus HOPELINEUK (0800 068 4141 — under 35s), Childline (0800 1111 — under 19s), and regional NHS crisis teams.

### Market: Not a commercial market. This is a public health infrastructure service operating at zero cost. The "market" is the 6,500+ annual suicide deaths in the UK — every life that can be reached faster through better routing.
### Barrier: Crisis services are fragmented. Callers don't know which number to call. Wait times vary wildly. Some services only operate specific hours. Sovereign Drop: One entry point that intelligently routes to the right service, right now.

### NO BLACK SWAN. No "disruption windows." No "Clean House Protocol." This is life-critical infrastructure, not a market opportunity. The only metric that matters: connection time to a qualified human counselor.

## ARTICLE III — FREE TRAINING (FOR SUPPORT WORKERS & COUNSELORS ONLY)

**CRITICAL: This training is for mental health professionals, crisis counselors, and support workers. It is NOT designed for the general public and does NOT qualify recipients to provide crisis intervention without professional supervision.**

| Tier | Name | Modules | Duration | Cert |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **T1** | Foundation | Active Listening, Suicide Awareness, Recognizing Crisis Signs, Safeguarding Basics, Crisis Service Landscape (UK) | 8 weeks | CASA-1 |
| **T2** | Practitioner | Crisis De-escalation, Risk Assessment (including self-harm), Supporting Bereaved Families, Trauma-Informed Practice, Multi-Agency Working | 14 weeks | CASA-2 |
| **T3** | Lead Auditor | Clinical Risk Management, Crisis Service Design, Suicide Prevention Strategy, Postvention Leadership, Service Quality Auditing | 18 weeks | CASA-3 |

### UE5 Simulations (FOR TRAINED PROFESSIONALS ONLY)
1. **The Crisis Call**: A simulated crisis call with a person experiencing suicidal ideation. Practice active listening, risk assessment, safety planning, and warm handoff to appropriate services. Supervised by qualified clinicians.
2. **The Multi-Agency Response**: Coordinate a crisis response across NHS crisis team, police, and voluntary sector. Navigate information sharing (with consent), risk management, and follow-up care planning.

### UBI Starter (FOR MENTAL HEALTH SUPPORT WORKERS): Foundation → Peer support marketplace (£300/mo). Lead Auditor → Crisis service contracts (£900/mo). NOTE: UBI is for qualified professionals only. This is NOT a "become a counselor by completing an app" pathway. Professional registration (BACP, UKCP, NMC, HCPC, SWE) is required for clinical roles.
