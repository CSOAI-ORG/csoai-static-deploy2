🚫 Red Lines (Never Crossed)

  • ❌ No mental health risk assessment without clinical oversight and Mental Health Act safeguards
  • ❌ No children's social care automation without qualified social worker review and child-centred assessment
  • ❌ No deprivation of liberty decisions without Mental Capacity Act compliance and best interests assessment
  • ❌ No substance misuse data processing without patient consent and addiction service confidentiality protocols
  • ❌ No crisis response AI without real-time clinical supervision and escalation pathways to emergency services
  • ❌ No digital therapeutics deployment without MHRA compliance and clinical evidence review
