Verify a signed record

Free, loginless, deterministic. Paste either (a) a full signed record JSON (with content_id, signature, pubkey, body) or (b) a raw content_id, signature, pubkey. We recompute content_id = sha256(canonical body) (RFC 8785) and check the Ed25519 signature. A valid signature asserts "this exact record was signed at time T" — never that the subject is endorsed.

Language lock: measurement, not certification. Verification is free forever.
