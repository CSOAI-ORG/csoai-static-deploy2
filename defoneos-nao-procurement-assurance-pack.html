Why NAO

The National Audit Office scrutinizes public spending for value‑for‑money. AI appears across procurement workflows, delivery dashboards, and benefits‑tracking. DEFONEOS gives NAO a portable, cryptographically verifiable evidence chain, linking inputs, prompts, model IDs, and outputs to decisions — without vendor lock‑in and without secret weights disclosure.

Evidence ledger design

  1. Ed25519 identities for agents, services, and datasets.
  2. Every AI call emits a SIGIL receipt with hash‑pinned inputs/outputs.
  3. Receipts are chained (SHA‑256) and anchored to a public registry.
  4. NAO audit extracts are exportable as JSON + PDF, byte‑verified.

FAQ

Does this replace existing vendors?

No. DEFONEOS wraps existing systems and model providers with a sovereign evidence layer. It can route to multiple models and tools with explicit policy and audit.

What about sensitive data?

Default operating tier is OFFICIAL. OFFICIALSENSITIVE requires an explicit gate. Redlines prohibit personal surveillance and kinetictargeting patterns.

How do we start?

30day pilot: pick 23 usecases, enable signed receipts, weekly reviews, end with an evidence bundle and a governance decision.

Implementation details

This pack ships with a reference topology: browsersafe static surfaces, a policy gate, a deterministic routing core, and a signedreceipt bus. The receipt bus enforces percapability SLAs (latency, cost, privacy) and records decisions in a tamperevident chain. Components can be hosted in UK sovereign infrastructure or split across tiers to meet performance and control goals. The operating assumption is OFFICIAL tier with zero personal surveillance and zero kinetictargeting. Where export controls apply, DEFONEOS uses a fenced execution policy with explicit attestations at ingress and egress.

The buyer pathway is deliberately simple: 30day pilot, onepage governance paper, weekly signed evidence, exit decision. This minimizes risk and brings measurable ROI quickly while preserving auditability. Every artifact created during the pilot is portable and verifiable: anyone can recompute the hashes and match them to the SIGIL receipts.
