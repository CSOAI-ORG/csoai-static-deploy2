⚡ 8 Transformation Priorities

1. WCA-UC Seamless Integration

Eliminate assessment duplication. Unified health journey tracking. Single health evidence submission across UC and disability benefits.

2. Mental Health-First Design

Mental health reasonable adjustments as default. Crisis intervention automation. Work coach mental health competency assessment. Therapy pathway integration.

3. Evidence Quality Automation

Medical evidence cross-referencing. GP report quality scoring. Contradictory evidence detection. Assessment consistency monitoring.

4. Transition Safeguard

ESA-to-UC health continuity protection. Transitional payment assurance. Disability premium preservation. No worse-off guarantee enforcement.

5. Appeals Automation

Mandatory reconsideration fast-track for health decisions. Tribunal case preparation. Descriptor scoring analysis. Case law matching.

6. Carer Journey Integration

Carer assessment coordination with cared-for person UC claim. Carer health impact monitoring. Employment support for carers.

7. Fraud Without Harm

Health claim integrity without punitive conditionality. Vulnerable claimant protection. Proportionate evidence requirements. Dignity-preserving verification.

8. Parliamentary Transparency

Real-time UC health outcomes dashboard. Benefit-related mortality monitoring. Assessment quality metrics. Independent review publication.

🚫 Red Lines (Never Crossed)

  • ❌ No health conditionality without verified medical evidence from qualified clinician
  • ❌ No sanction applied to claimant with active mental health crisis flag
  • ❌ No health data shared between DWP and NHS without GDPR Article 9 lawful basis and explicit consent
  • ❌ No WCA reassessment within 12 months of previous favourable decision without new evidence
  • ❌ No automated benefit suspension on health grounds without human review within 48 hours
  • ❌ No health-related benefit decision without accessible format for claimant with declared disability
