📋 12 Entry Points

ONR

ONR Regulation — Defence Nuclear Licensing, Safety Assessments

Office for Nuclear Regulation (ONR) oversees defence nuclear activities under Nuclear Installations Act 1965. Site licence conditions for AWE Aldermaston, Burghfield, Devonport, Raynesway. Safety assessment principles, fundamental safety objectives, emergency preparedness. Licence compliance inspections. Enforcement — improvement and prohibition notices. Annual report to Parliament. IAEA bilateral cooperation. ONR defence programme directorate.

AWE

AWE Operations — Aldermaston, Burghfield, Warhead Manufacturing

Atomic Weapons Establishment (AWE plc) — design, manufacture, and maintenance of UK nuclear warheads. Aldermaston: warhead design, physics, hydrodynamics, materials science. Burghfield: warhead assembly, disassembly, refurbishment. AWE licence conditions: criticality safety, radiation protection, conventional safety. INF Treaty compliance. Warhead life-extension programme. Trident Holbrook warhead. High Explosive Research facility. Orion laser facility. Environmental permits. Decommissioning liabilities. Workforce of ~5,000.

Reactors

Submarine Reactor Safety — PWR2/PWR3, Naval Reactor Operations

Pressurised Water Reactor (PWR) designs for Royal Navy submarines. PWR2 for Vanguard-class (Trident) and Trafalgar-class. PWR3 for Astute-class and Dreadnought-class. Rolls-Royce Marine: reactor design, manufacture, fuel fabrication at Raynesway. Naval reactor safety case methodology. Reactor compartment shielding. Primary and secondary coolant circuits. Emergency reactor shutdown (SCRAM). Reactor coolant leak detection. Submarine decommissioning — reactor defueling, storage at Devonport. Nuclear propulsion safety. Naval nuclear fuel cycle.

Waste

Nuclear Waste Management — LLW, ILW, Spent Fuel, Decommissioning

Defence nuclear waste classification: Low Level Waste (LLW), Intermediate Level Waste (ILW), High Level Waste (HLW), spent fuel. AWE waste streams: contaminated metals, HE residues, liquid effluent. Submarine decommissioning waste: reactor components, contaminated steel. ILW stores at AWE, Devonport, Rosyth. Spent naval fuel reprocessing at Sellafield. Waste packaging, conditioning, interim storage. UK Radioactive Waste Inventory. Geological Disposal Facility (GDF) — long-term disposal strategy. Defence waste NDA strategy. Waste acceptance criteria.

Safety Cases

Safety Case Management — Periodic Safety Reviews, ALARP Demonstrations

Defence nuclear safety case regime: safety cases, hazard identification, fault analysis, consequence assessment. ALARP demonstration methodology. Periodic Safety Reviews — 10-year cycle. Deterministic and probabilistic safety assessment. Common cause failure analysis. Human reliability assessment. Safety case review by ONR inspectors. Change management. Design Assessment and Pre-construction Safety Report (PCSR). Defence Nuclear Safety Committee (DNSC) oversight.

Emergency

Emergency Planning — REPPIR, Off-Site Emergency Plans, JESIP

REPPIR 2019 compliance. Off-site emergency plans — AWE, Devonport, Coulport. Emergency Planning Zones. JESIP multi-agency response. UKHSA radiation monitoring. Local Authority off-site plans. MOD emergency liaison. COBR coordination. CONTEST counter-terrorism. Regular emergency exercises. IAEA Early Notification Convention.

Transport

Nuclear Transport — Flask Movements, Route Approvals, Security

Nuclear material transport: warhead convoys (MOD Police), spent fuel flasks (NDA). Type B(U) flask design, impact testing. Route approvals: preferred route network. Police escort: MOD Police, CNC. Transport security: armed escort, counter-terrorism. Nuclear Safeguards documentation. Rail transport via MOD sidings. Emergency response during transport. Transport risk assessment. EURATOM international transport. Sea transport for naval fuel.

Environment

Contaminated land investigation. Discharge authorisations. Marine environment monitoring. Ecological impact assessment. NDA Strategy. Decommissioning costs: £100B+ across estate.

Skills

Nuclear Skills — Workforce, Apprenticeships, Specialist Training

Knowledge management for retiring workforce. STEM engagement.

🚫 Red Lines (Never Crossed)

  • ❌ No nuclear operation without ONR licence — every defence nuclear installation must hold a valid ONR site licence; no operations may commence or continue without full licence conditions compliance
  • ❌ No safety case without ALARP demonstration — every nuclear safety case must include a fully documented ALARP (As Low As Reasonably Practicable) assessment; AI may assist but cannot replace engineering safety judgment
  • ❌ No nuclear transport without route approval and security escort — all defence nuclear material movements require pre-approved route risk assessments and armed MOD Police or CNC escort throughout transit
  • ❌ No nuclear waste disposal without characterisation and classification — all defence nuclear waste must be fully characterised, classified, and packaged before any disposal or transfer pathway is activated
  • ❌ No nuclear emergency without JESIP-compliant multi-agency response — every declared nuclear emergency must activate joint emergency services coordination under JESIP principles; no single-agency response permitted
  • ❌ No nuclear personnel without security clearance and fitness-to-work — all personnel with access to defence nuclear material, information, or facilities must hold appropriate security clearance (DV) and fitness-to-work certification
