Benchmark-Quality Register

Signed, deterministic ratings of third-party benchmark quality — scored from public artifacts only, never an LLM judge. BetterBench's criteria × OpenSSF Scorecard's check design. Every record is measured-current-state, Ed25519-signed, hash-chained, and offline-verifiable against the published key.

Impartiality firewall (ISO/IEC 17020/17025): CSOAI's own boards are never scored on this register — a body must not assess its own work. Each record carries solicited:false · party_participated:false · access:public_artifacts_only · not_a_certification:true. A low score means "this benchmark currently lacks public evidence for X", never a condemnation of the authors.
