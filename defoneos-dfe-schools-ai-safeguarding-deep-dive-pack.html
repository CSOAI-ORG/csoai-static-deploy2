🚫 Red Lines (Never Crossed)

  • ❌ No child protection plan decision without social worker assessment and multi-agency strategy discussion
  • ❌ No exclusion from school without governing board hearing, parental right of appeal, and SEND assessment consideration
  • ❌ No Channel/Prevent referral without education welfare officer and police Prevent officer joint assessment
  • ❌ No EHCP placement change without annual review, parental consultation, and SEND tribunal right of appeal
  • ❌ No LADO decision to take no further action without multi-agency discussion and rationale documentation
  • ❌ No pupil data sharing beyond education setting without parental consent, lawful basis, or child protection necessity
