Passes — measured-current state records

A pass is a signed, time-boxed measurement of a system against frozen provisions — never a certificate. The pass states what was measured, on which instrument version, within which window, and when it expires. validUntil = min(window, provision-change-event): the pass dies when its time window lapses OR when the regulation it was measured against changes — whichever comes first. Re-measurement is free and unpurchasable.

Pass states

CURRENTmeasured within window; regulation unchanged
EXPIRED-WINDOW-LAPSEDtime window passed; re-measure to renew
EXPIRED-REGULATION-CHANGEDthe provision changed; the old measurement no longer speaks to it
NEVER-MEASUREDno measurement exists — reported as such, never invented

What a pass is not

  • Not a certificate — nothing reads as attestation of safety, compliance sign-off, or a pass/fail stamp buyers can rely on.
  • Not a legal determination — a pass maps measured signals to provisions with a confidence, "not a legal determination or legal advice."
  • Not permanent — every pass carries its expiry; a red estate publishes as red.
  • Not purchasable — no money either direction with anything ranked. Whoever pays (evidence-buyers) never buys a state.

How to read a pass

Each pass links to its signed card: id = sha256(canonical body), signature = Ed25519(id), prev chains the record. Verify offline with the published public key. The states above are rendered from the signed record — a stranger can recompute every one. Measurement, not certification. Verification is free forever.
