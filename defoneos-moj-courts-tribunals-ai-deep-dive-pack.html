2. Court Scheduling Optimisation

Machine learning on court scheduling — judge allocation, room utilisation, case duration prediction. Reduce effective trial rates, wasted sitting days, custody time limit breaches. Target: courtroom utilisation from 65% to 85%, effective trial rate from 55% to 75%, custody time limit breach rate under 1%.

3. Legal Aid Access Mapping

4. Tribunal Caseload Prediction

Target: tribunal case backlog prediction accuracy above 90%, hearing scheduling efficiency increase 30%, average tribunal waiting time reduction 25%.

5. Sentencing Consistency Engine

Target: sentencing guideline compliance monitoring 100%, sentencing disparity analysis quarterly, pre-sentence report quality scoring automated.

7. Family Court Safeguarding Intelligence

Target: domestic abuse identification accuracy above 95%, care order application processing time reduction 20%, child safeguarding risk assessment consistency above 90%.

🚫 Red Lines (Never Crossed)

  • ❌ No judicial decision overridden by algorithm — AI provides analysis and data; sentencing, case outcome, and judicial direction decisions remain exclusively with the judiciary
  • ❌ No legal aid eligibility auto-rejected without human review — all legal aid refusals must be reviewed by qualified caseworker; algorithmic assessment supports but does not replace human decision
  • ❌ No family court safeguarding risk scored without professional oversight — child protection risk assessments must be validated by qualified social worker or Cafcass officer
  • ❌ No criminal disclosure decision automated without prosecutor sign-off — disclosure obligations under CPIA remain with the prosecutor; AI assists review but does not determine materiality
  • ❌ No jury information exposed to digital tracking — juror personal data, deliberation content, and voting must never be digitised, tracked, or analysed
  • ❌ No court closure impact assessment published without statutory consultation — all HMCTS estate changes require formal consultation under HMCTS statutory duty
