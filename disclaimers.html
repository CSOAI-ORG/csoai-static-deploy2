Disclaimers

The disclaimers that govern every number published by Council of AI. They are the contract between a measurement and a reader.

Measurement, not certification

Every signed card on this site is a verified measurement credential — evidence of what was measured, by which method, and when. Nothing here is a certificate, approval, conformity mark, or attestation of safety or compliance. Never certification.

Not legal advice

Nothing on this site is legal advice or a legal determination. A measurement maps observed signals to provisions with a confidence — it does not declare a system lawful or unlawful. For a binding assessment, seek qualified legal advice or the relevant market-surveillance authority (EU AI Act Art. 85).

Reported baselines

Human baselines and external benchmark figures are reported from published external cohorts, not our own measurements — they are labelled as such. Vendor numbers are never echoed as our own; where we cite an external result it is attributed and re-measured before it appears on our surfaces.

UNMEASURED stays UNMEASURED

An axis with no quotable cell is reported as UNMEASURED with its limits. We never interpolate, average over a gap, or fill a cell to look complete. A gated slot is a finding, not a failure to report.

No money either direction with anything ranked

Council of AI takes no payment, equity, referral, or in-kind consideration from anything it ranks. Evidence-buyers (insurers, enterprises, regulators, researchers) pay for measurement products — never for placement, a rank, or a score change. Verification is free forever.

Not a notified body

Council of AI holds no EU/UK regulatory designation. No claim of notified-body status is made or implied.

Corrections supersede

If a number is wrong, the correction is published in the honesty register with its date. Nothing is deleted; corrections supersede. A number we cannot recompute is not a number we publish.
