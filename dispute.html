Dispute a measurement

Every signed measurement has an appeals path. It is free, loginless, and receipted — a dispute is itself a signed event, and every dispute gets a response. If a number is wrong, the correction is published in the honesty register with its date. Nothing is deleted; corrections supersede.

How to dispute

  1. Have the card. The disputed measurement is a signed card (id = sha256 of the canonical body; signature = Ed25519; prev links the chain). Verify it offline first with the published public key — the card itself is evidence of what was claimed and when.
  2. Open a dispute. Use the dispute-measurement template on our public issue tracker. Include: the card id, the cell you dispute, and your evidence (a recomputation, a method objection, or a source that contradicts the claim).
  3. Get a response. Disputes are triaged deterministically: a recomputation we can run, a method objection we can test, or a correction we publish. Every outcome is recorded.

What a dispute is not

  • Not a legal proceeding — a measurement dispute is answered with measurement.
  • Not a score change for payment — no money either direction with anything ranked, ever.
  • Not a silent fix — a correction is published in the honesty register, superseding not deleting.

Appeals path

If the first response does not resolve the dispute, the appeal goes to the published methodology owners with the full evidence trail. The standing is: a number we can't defend is corrected, not hidden. Verification is free forever.
