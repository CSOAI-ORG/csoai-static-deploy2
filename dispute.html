Dispute a measurement

2026-08-18 · the appeals protocol · corrections publish in the same record as results

A measurement body with no appeal path is a court with no defence counsel. Every figure we publish is signed; every signed figure can be disputed; every upheld dispute becomes a public correction in the same record as the original result. We do not retract — we correct, signed, in the open.

How to file

  1. Open a measurement dispute using the tracked template: github.com/CSOAI-ORG → dispute-measurement.
  2. State the artifact (card ID, DOI, or URL), the figure you dispute, and your evidence. Recomputed numbers beat opinions: if our digest does not recompute for you, say exactly that.
  3. You may stay pseudonymous. The evidence is what gets examined, not the filer.

What happens next — the SLA

  • Acknowledged: within 7 days, in the issue, signed.
  • Examined: the Prover lane re-walks the artifact backward — artifact to premises — independently of the original builder. The checker never reads the maker's reasoning.
  • Resolved: one of three signed outcomes — upheld (the figure stands, with the re-walk receipt attached), corrected (a signed correction enters the public corrections ledger at /honesty), or withdrawn-as-unmeasurable (the claim is pulled from quotation, the artifact stays in the archive, marked).

What you can dispute

Any signed artifact: the 15 release-proof cards (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21973002), the frozen bank seals (banks-manifest.json), board scores, the arena rounds, this site's identity documents. If it's signed, it's disputable. If it isn't signed, it isn't ours.
