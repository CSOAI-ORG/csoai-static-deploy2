🚫 Red Lines (Never Crossed)

  • ❌ No service family housed below Decent Homes Standard — all 49,000 SFA properties must meet minimum condition
  • ❌ No contaminated land left unremediated beyond risk threshold — MOD has legacy contamination obligations
  • ❌ No defence estate disposal without community benefit assessment and heritage review
  • ❌ No new defence building without net zero design standard — retrofit is acceptable but new-build must be green
  • ❌ No training area closure without environmental impact assessment and community consultation
  • ❌ No single point of failure in critical estate infrastructure — power, water, comms must have redundancy
