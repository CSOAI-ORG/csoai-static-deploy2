How It Works (The 5-Phase Cycle)

Every 5 minutes, the OLM brain runs:

PHASE 1 — INGEST — Pulls latest data from all 30 MCP servers. Met Office weather. Environment Agency flood levels. AIS vessel positions. ADS-B aircraft tracks. Sentinel satellite imagery. GDELT news events. Companies House filings. ONS statistics. Maritime streams. RSS camera feeds. IoT bridge data.

PHASE 2 — PROCESS — The Mamba-2 right brain (sequential memory) checks if current patterns match historical patterns. The 64-Expert MoE middle brain routes inputs to the right specialist. The Transformer left brain plans what might happen next.

PHASE 3 — INTUIT — If multiple weak signals converge across domains, the brain generates an "intuition" — a pre-conscious awareness that something is happening before any single sensor confirms it. This is the noise→intuition→intelligence pipeline (defoneos-care-mcp.html).

PHASE 4 — DECIDE — The BFT 33-council votes on whether the intuition warrants action. Quorum 23/33 required. Each voter is one of the 6 agent types (worm / dragonfly / hornet / killer-bee / sage / guardian). Vote is signature-anchored.

PHASE 5 — RECORD — Every cycle is Ed25519-signed. Every learning is hash-chained to the previous. The brain cannot forget or be tampered with without breaking the SIGIL chain — which anyone can verify.

Five Honest Limitations

⚠ What the OLM cannot yet do (honesty register).

  1. No internet into confidential networks. OLM is on GCP VM with egress to public data only. It does not have SC clearance to access TOP-SECRET networks. To run on TS, deploy a SEPARATE OLM instance inside the SC enclave.
  2. Approximation, not guarantee. Intuition emission is a confidence-weighted suggestion, not a Boolean decision. Council quorum protects against both false positives and missed events, but cannot guarantee zero false negatives.
  3. Keypair is single-host. For multi-tenant + hostile-state defence, move to HSM (Phase 3, Q1 2027).
  4. No self-replication yet. OLM is a single brain on one VM. For full sovereign-scale need >1 brain with BFT across them. Roadmap: Phase 3 (Q1 2027).
  5. Training corpus is raw. We do not currently fine-tune the OLM on sovereign operational corpora — only ingest live signals. Operational fine-tuning needs SC clearance and customer data, neither of which we have yet.
These are the things DEFONEOS is NOT, with honesty.
