🏛️ 12 Entry Points — Trade Remedies Authority

Trade/Economy

Anti-Dumping Investigations

Investigations into imports sold below normal value causing injury to UK industry; provisional and definitive measures under Taxation (Cross-border Trade) Act 2018 Schedule 4

Trade/Economy

Countervailing Duty Investigations

Investigations into subsidised imports causing injury — countervailable subsidies, specificity analysis and injury determinations under Schedule 4 Part 2

Trade/Economy

Safeguard Investigations

Urgent safeguard investigations where import surges cause serious injury; provisional safeguards, tariff-rate quotas and WTO notification

Trade/Economy

Transition Reviews

Review of EU-inherited trade remedy measures against the UK's own injury tests; assessment of whether measures should be maintained, varied or terminated

Trade/Economy

Injury & Causation Analysis

Economic analysis of injury, causation and non-attribution: production, capacity utilisation, sales, prices, employment and profitability of UK industry

Trade/Economy

Dumping & Subsidy Calculations

Calculation of dumping margins (export price vs normal value) and subsidy amounts using exporter questionnaire data and verification visits

Trade/Economy

Confidential Business Information

Handling of commercially sensitive exporter and UK producer data under strict confidentiality regimes with public-file redaction and non-confidential summaries

Trade/Economy

Public Interest Test

Assessment of whether imposing a measure is against the UK economic interest — impact on downstream users, consumers and competition

Trade/Economy

Reviews & Appeals

Interim reviews, expiry reviews, absorption reviews, circumvention investigations and reconsiderations; appeals to the Upper Tribunal under Trade Act 2021

Trade/Economy

Import Data & Registration

HMRC import data collection, registration of imports during investigations and retrospective collection of duties

Trade/Economy

Stakeholder Engagement

Public consultations, case files, hearings and industry registration; WTO-mandated transparency and notification procedures

Trade/Economy

Public Accountability

Annual reports to Parliament via DBT, scrutiny by the International Trade Committee, National Audit Office value-for-money audits and WTO peer review

🚀 8 AI Transformation Priorities

Priority 1

Questionnaire Response Analysis

NLP extraction and validation of exporter questionnaire responses — cross-checking volumes, prices and cost data across submissions; before: analyst manual review (months); after: AI first-pass validation with case-analyst verification

Priority 2

Injury Indicator Modelling

AI econometric modelling of injury indicators (prices, market share, profitability) with non-attribution analysis; before: spreadsheet econometrics; after: AI modelling with economic-officer validation

Priority 3

Confidentiality Redaction Engine

AI-assisted redaction of confidential business information for public files while preserving non-confidential summaries; before: manual redaction (error-prone); after: AI redaction with legal-officer sign-off

Priority 4

Precedent & Case Law Retrieval

AI retrieval across WTO jurisprudence, UK case law and prior TRA determinations for consistent legal reasoning; before: library research; after: AI precedent engine with legal-qualified review

Priority 5

Import Data Anomaly Detection

ML detection of import surges, circumvention patterns and under-declaration anomalies in HMRC data; before: periodic manual scans; after: AI continuous anomaly monitoring

Priority 6

Timeline & Compliance Tracking

AI tracking of statutory investigation timelines (WTO and UK deadlines) with automatic drafting of initiation, disclosure and determination documents; before: calendar-dependent; after: AI timeline engine

Priority 7

Public Interest Economic Modelling

AI modelling of measure impacts on downstream industries, consumers and employment; before: qualitative assessment; after: AI computable general-equilibrium-style scenario modelling

Priority 8

Multi-Language Evidence Processing

AI translation and structuring of exporter evidence in multiple languages with terminology preservation for WTO filings; before: external translation; after: AI translation with human verification

🚫 6 Red Lines — Legislation Backbone

Statutory Framework: Trade Act 2021 / Taxation (Cross-border Trade) Act 2018 (Schedules 4-5) / Trade Remedies (Increase in Import Duty etc.) Regulations 2019 / DPA 2018 + UK GDPR / WTO Anti-Dumping Agreement / WTO Agreement on Subsidies and Countervailing Measures

  • NO AI-sole determination of dumping, subsidy or injury (Trade Act 2021 ss.8-13 require TRA recommendations to the Secretary of State — human officers decide, AI assists)
  • NO breach of confidential business information protections (Trade Act 2021 s.28 criminal sanctions for unlawful disclosure — AI must never leak CBI to public files without human redaction sign-off)
  • NO AI-sole public interest test (the public interest assessment involves economic judgement and stakeholder evidence — AI modelling informs, human assessment determines)
  • NO AI departing from WTO Anti-Dumping Agreement and SCM Agreement disciplines without legal-qualified review (UK obligations under WTO rules are binding)
  • NO processing of exporter/importer data outside UK/EU adequacy arrangements (DPA 2018 + UK GDPR Chapter V international transfers)
  • NO AI-generated recommendations presented as TRA position without case-lead and Chief Economist sign-off (protects institutional credibility before the Upper Tribunal and WTO)
