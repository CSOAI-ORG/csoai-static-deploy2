IWM
Infinite World Memory · The Persistence Layer
Content-addressed signed corpus + temporal knowledge graphs. NOT "infinite" — large, well-indexed, signed memory with honest cost/latency limits.
What it is
The persistence substrate: every artifact becomes a content-addressed (SHA-256 CID), Ed25519-signed card via meok-sovereign-spine-mcp. 5 card kinds (measurement, arena-round, honey-data, provenance, charter).
What it's NOT
Not "infinite". Not "5D drawing mind". Not marketing. Real outcome: large, well-indexed, signed corpus with honest cost (storage scales), latency (retrieval p50 + LLM inference), retrieval quality ceilings, and hallucination risk.
Live state (15 Aug 2026):
  • 400 arena rounds signed + verified (/api/sov-arena/signed.jsonl, 373KB)
  • 5 card kinds registered (measurement, arena-round, honey-data, provenance, charter)
  • Ed25519 detached sigs via cryptography lib
  • Recompute-able: any party can take a card, recompute the CID, verify the sig
Honest ceiling: ~10–50M vectors is the practical scale ceiling for pgvector. Above that, migrate to Qdrant/Milvus. Hallucination risk is reduced (every claim is signed) but not eliminated (signing ≠ correctness).
