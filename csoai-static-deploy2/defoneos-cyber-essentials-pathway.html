Foundations

What is Cyber Essentials?

Cyber Essentials is a UK government-backed certification scheme that helps organisations protect against the most common cyber attacks. For defence AI companies, it is often a contractual prerequisite for MOD work.

🛡️

Cyber Essentials (Basic)

A self-assessment questionnaire verified by an accredited body. Covers five key technical controls and provides baseline protection against common threats. Required for most MOD contracts under £5M.

✓

Cyber Essentials Plus

Includes an independent hands-on technical audit of your systems. An assessor tests your defences against real-world attack simulations. Required for contracts involving sensitive or classified data.

⚡

Why It Matters for AI

AI systems handling defence data face unique threats: model poisoning, training data exfiltration, and inference API abuse. Cyber Essentials provides the foundational controls that protect these attack surfaces.

Technical Controls

The Five Key Requirements

Every Cyber Essentials certification evaluates these five control areas. DEFONEOS maps each one to automated checks and remediation workflows.

Firewalls & Internet Gateways

Configure boundary firewalls and internet gateways to prevent unauthorised access. This includes default-deny policies, logging, and regular rule review. For AI infrastructure, this means securing API endpoints, model serving ports, and training cluster boundaries.

DEFONEOS automates firewall rule auditing across cloud providers and on-prem clusters, flagging overly permissive rules and unused ports.

Secure Configuration

Remove or disable unnecessary software, services, and user accounts. Change default passwords. Disable unused ports and protocols. For AI systems, this extends to model serving configurations, container images, and orchestration defaults.

DEFONEOS runs CIS-benchmark scans against your infrastructure, container images, and AI serving stacks with auto-remediation playbooks.

User Access Control

Implement principle of least privilege. Use unique accounts (no shared credentials). Restrict admin privileges. Control access to data and services. For AI teams, this means role-based access to training data, model registries, and inference endpoints.

DEFONEOS integrates with your identity provider to enforce RBAC across model access, data pipelines, and infrastructure with automated access reviews.

Malware Protection

Deploy anti-malware solutions on all endpoints and servers. Use application whitelisting where possible. Keep signatures updated. For AI workloads, scan training data imports, model files, and container images for malicious payloads.

DEFONEOS scans model artefacts and training datasets for adversarial payloads, supply chain compromises, and data poisoning indicators.

Security Update Management

Apply security patches within 14 days of release. Keep all software up to date including OS, firmware, drivers, and applications. For AI stacks, this covers CUDA drivers, ML frameworks, serving libraries, and container base images.

DEFONEOS tracks CVEs across your full AI stack — from GPU drivers to PyTorch versions — with automated patching pipelines and compliance dashboards.

Certification Process

The IASME Certification Process

IASME (Information Assurance for Small and Medium Enterprises) is the National Cyber Security Centre's partner for delivering Cyber Essentials. Here is the step-by-step process.

Step 1 · Day 1–3

Choose a Certification Body

Select an IASME-approved Certification Body. Prices are fixed for the basic assessment. Consider DEFONEOS-integrated assessors who understand AI-specific infrastructure. All assessors are listed on the NCSC website.

Step 2 · Day 3–5

Complete the Self-Assessment Questionnaire

Answer questions covering your IT infrastructure, boundary firewalls, device configuration, user access controls, malware protection, and patch management. AI-specific scope (model serving, training clusters, data pipelines) must be included in your boundary definition.

Step 3 · Day 5–8

Submit & Assessor Review

Your Certification Body reviews the submission. They may request clarification or additional evidence. Typical review takes 3–5 working days. Failed submissions receive guidance on required remediation.

Step 4 · Day 8–12

Remediation (if needed)

Address any gaps identified. Common issues for AI companies: missing patching on GPU driver stacks, overly broad API access, default configurations on model serving infrastructure, and inadequate network segmentation.

Step 5 · Day 12–15

Certification Awarded

Receive your Cyber Essentials certificate, valid for 12 months. Your company is listed on the NCSC directory. For CE Plus, schedule the hands-on technical audit within 3 months of basic certification.

Step 6 · Ongoing

Annual Recertification

Cyber Essentials must be renewed annually. Maintain continuous compliance through automated monitoring. DEFONEOS provides real-time dashboards that show your certification readiness at any time.

Defence Contracts

Relationship to MOD Contracts

Cyber Essentials is not just a best practice — for UK defence AI companies, it is often a contractual obligation. Here is what you need to know.

📋 Contractual Requirements

Since April 2024, the MOD requires Cyber Essentials for all contracts involving the transfer of MOD identifiable information. Higher-risk contracts require CE Plus.

  • All contracts handling OFFICIAL data require basic CE
  • Contracts handling sensitive data require CE Plus
  • Defence Gateway and MOD network access requires CE Plus
  • Subcontractors handling MOD data must also hold CE

🏗️ DASA & Dstl Grants

Defence and Security Accelerator (DASA) and Dstl-funded AI projects increasingly require certification as a milestone deliverable, not just a prerequisite.

  • DASA Phase 2+ contracts typically require CE
  • Innovation fund holders should pre-certify
  • Dstl research contracts specify data handling tiers
  • CE status accelerates procurement evaluation

🔐 Beyond CE: DSPT & ISO 27001

Cyber Essentials is the foundation. MOD contracts for AI systems handling health, personnel, or classified data may require additional certifications.

  • Data Security & Protection Toolkit (DSPT) for health data
  • ISO 27001 for comprehensive ISMS
  • NATO AQAP 2105 for defence quality assurance
  • FedRAMP-equivalent for cloud-hosted AI systems

📊 Supply Chain Advantage

Holding CE certification puts you on the NCSC's published directory, making you discoverable by prime contractors seeking compliant AI suppliers.

  • Listed on NCSC Cyber Essentials directory
  • Eligible for Tier 1/2 supplier subcontracting
  • Required for MOD framework agreements
  • Differentiator in competitive tenders

AI-Specific Guidance

Unique Considerations for AI Companies

Defence AI companies face attack surfaces that traditional IT assessments do not fully cover. DEFONEOS addresses these gaps.

🎯

Model Serving Endpoints

Inference APIs (vLLM, TGI, Triton) are internet-facing services that need the same firewall discipline as web servers. DEFONEOS scans for exposed ports, missing authentication, and unencrypted model traffic.

📊

Training Data Security

Training data is a high-value target. Apply access controls to S3 buckets, NAS shares, and HuggingFace repos. DEFONEOS monitors data access patterns and alerts on anomalous downloads.

🔗

Supply Chain Integrity

Model weights from public repositories may contain backdoors. DEFONEOS validates model checksums, scans for known adversarial patterns, and maintains a trusted model registry.

🖥️

GPU Infrastructure

GPU drivers, CUDA libraries, and NCCL are often months behind on patches. DEFONEOS tracks GPU-specific CVEs and provides tested upgrade paths that do not break training runs.
