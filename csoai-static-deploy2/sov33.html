What SOV33 Is

SOV33 is a sovereign Open World Emergence Model (OWEM). It owns its own trainable weights (not a wrapper), runs a 12-around-1 topology with the 12 Sovereign Pillars as specialist workers, gates every response through care-floor 0.95 + Article 0 + 12 Pillars + BFT-33 quorum, and signs every response with Ed25519 SIGIL.

What SOV33 Is NOT

SOV33 is NOT a T-parameter foundation model. It is NOT AGI. It is NOT competing with frontier labs on raw capability. It is a different capability class — sovereign, governed, auditable.
