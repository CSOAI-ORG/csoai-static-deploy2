2. Scenario Detail: Yorkshire Flood HADR

72-Hour Mountain Rescue + Civil Defence + Drone Swarm

Day 0, 14:32 BST: 87 mm/hr rainfall in upper Calder. EA flood models predict 4 villages cut off within 90 min. Yorkshire ops room initiates multi-agency response.

Day 0, 14:35: DEFONEOS issues a 999-emergency-coordination SIGIL (digest d8a12f...). All agencies enter coordinated mode. 7-channel alert cascade fires:

  1. EA — flood alerts + levels
  2. Fire & Rescue — swift water rescue
  3. Police — evacuation zones
  4. Ambulance — triage hubs
  5. Met Office — weather forecasting
  6. Mountain Rescue — overdue trekers
  7. Civil Air — drone routes + NOTAM

Day 0, 15:30: 47 civilians trapped on elevated ground; 3 hikers overdue on Pennine Way. DEFONEOS dispatches 40-sdrone swarm (Mava RL 2.05M episodes of training).

Day 0, 17:00: Drone swarm locates 2 hikers (radio triangulation), guides Mountain Rescue in. Civilians airlifted by RAF helicopter (Cabinet Office minister briefed via SIGIL).

Day 3, 16:00: 47 rescued. 0 casualties. SIGIL chain of 47,200 receipts submitted to Cabinet Office Resilience Secretariat. Audit complete in 24h.

8. The SIGIL Audit Principle

Every one of the 18 scenarios ends with a SIGIL chain. The chain is reconstructable, Ed25519-signed, and auditable by independent parties. That's the difference between "AI controls the kill chain" (where the AI's reasoning is a black box) and "DEFONEOS audits the kill chain" (where every decision has a receipt).
