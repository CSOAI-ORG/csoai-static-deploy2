🚀 8 AI Transformation Priorities

Priority 1

Inactivity Risk Segmentation

AI segmentation of Active Lives data to identify inactivity risk clusters by geography and demographic; before: aggregate reporting; after: AI ward-level risk maps for targeted investment

Priority 2

Facility Investment Optimisation

AI modelling of facility demand, catchment analysis and condition data to prioritise capital grants; before: manual scoring; after: AI investment-portfolio optimisation with human decision

Priority 3

Grant Application Triage

NLP triage and completeness checking of grant applications, reducing officer time on ineligible applications; before: manual sifting (weeks); after: AI triage with funding-officer determination

Priority 4

Safeguarding Case Intelligence

AI-assisted pattern detection across safeguarding referrals (with human-led investigation and statutory escalation); before: siloed records; after: AI case-intelligence with designated-officer oversight

Priority 5

NGB Governance Monitoring

AI monitoring of governance indicators (Code for Sports Governance tier compliance) and financial-health signals across funded bodies; before: annual reviews; after: AI continuous assurance

Priority 6

Participation Forecasting

ML forecasting of participation trends to inform strategy cycles and funding allocations; before: linear extrapolation; after: AI scenario forecasting with confidence intervals

Priority 7

Coach & Volunteer Matching

AI matching of coaches and volunteers to clubs and events with safeguarding pre-checks; before: fragmented recruitment; after: AI matching with verified-credentials gate

Priority 8

Programme Evaluation

AI synthesis of programme evaluations, cost-per-active-participant analysis and counterfactual modelling; before: expensive external evaluations; after: AI evaluation engine with academic partnership

🚫 6 Red Lines — Legislation Backbone

Statutory Framework: Royal Charter 1996 (amended 2009) / National Lottery etc. Act 1993 / Equality Act 2010 / DPA 2018 + UK GDPR / Children Act 2004 / Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups Act 2006

  • NO AI-sole safeguarding determination (all safeguarding concerns require human investigation and statutory escalation — AI may surface patterns, never decide case outcomes)
  • NO processing of children's data outside UK GDPR Article 8 + age-appropriate design requirements (Children Act 2004 duty of care; DBS checks mandatory for regulated activity under Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups Act 2006)
  • NO AI-sole grant award (funding decisions remain with human funding panels and accountable officers — AI assists evidence, never determines outcomes)
  • NO automated discrimination in access to sport (Equality Act 2010 — algorithmic fairness audits for protected characteristics; access targets monitored by humans)
  • NO AI health advice presented as clinical guidance (physical-activity AI must signpost to registered health professionals, never diagnose or prescribe)
  • NO sharing of Active Lives individual-level data beyond its stated survey purposes (UK GDPR purpose limitation + Statistics and Registration Service Act 2007 confidentiality for official statistics)
