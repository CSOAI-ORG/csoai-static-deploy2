10. FAQs

Can I deploy Open Source in production? Yes — many do. You assume operation + patching.

What's the difference between Pro and Governance? Governance ships with EU AI Act Article 9/13/14/17 modules and JSP 936/440 helpers pre-wired. Pro is the platform + your compliance effort.

Is the Crown tier only UK government? Primarily UK/Crown entities (UK MOD / Cabinet Office / Home Office / NHS / NI / Crown Dependencies). Non-UK sovereign entities (e.g. Five Eyes) qualify for Crown via mutual recognition.

How does the open-source free tier stay sustainable? Paid tiers subsidise the open-source free tier's continuous maintenance + security patching. The 33-node BFT council is open too — anyone with the source can fork and run.

Do you charge for training? No. Certification is open-source (12 modules, 120h). We charge only for instructor-led delivery.
