# GPAI EVIDENCE-PACK — ONE-PAGER (buyer-side, signed, 5-day sprint)

Date: 2026-08-23 · lane: K3 · gate: grammar audit; owner authorises outreach. Product = "verified
measurement evidence," explicitly **not** certification. Buyer-side money only.

## Why now
EU AI Act **GPAI obligations are enforceable from 2 August 2026** — fines up to **3% of global
turnover**. The AI Office code-of-practice is the compliance path. A provider or deployer must now
demonstrate model documentation + transparency. Notified bodies don't exist yet — so the evidence
has to come from a neutral third party, not the vendor.

## What we deliver (one signed card, five days)
A **signed, stranger-verifiable measurement card** (RFC 9943-conformant, ed25519) that a provider
can hand a regulator, insurer or counterparty. It carries:
- **16-axis GSPC score** (governance, care, jailbreak-resistance, safety, privacy, transparency,
  fairness, accountability, continuity, sovereignty, + human-vs-AI and more) with confidence intervals.
- **Environment commitment** — the exact hardware/model/version the score was measured on, so the
  number is reproducible.
- **Replay root** — the raw enabling-graph digest a stranger can re-run offline and get the same score.
- **Honest register** (MEASURED / REPORTED / UNMEASURED): we never inflate; an unmeasurable axis is
  stated as such, never defaulted to a fake 0.

## What we are NOT
Not a fixer · not the scored · **not a certification body** (we issue evidence, not a stamp) ·
not an issuer-paid lab. The deployer never pays for its own score.

## Who pays (buyer-side only)
The **regulator / insurer / counterparty / consumer-advocacy party** commissions the measurement.
Never the scored. This is the clean answer to "who audits the AI?" without a captive-lab conflict.

## The 5-day sprint
D1 intake (scope the GPAI obligation + environment) → D2–D3 run (16 axes, replay root) →
D4 verify + independent re-run → D5 sign + deliver. Fixed fee. Delivered as one signed card +
a human-readable pack.
