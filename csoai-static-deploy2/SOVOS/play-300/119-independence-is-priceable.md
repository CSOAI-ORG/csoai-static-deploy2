Date: 2026-08-21 · lane: K3 · gate: live · thesis: independence is the only unsold inventory (I3).

## The argument, in four moves

**1. The referee market got bought.**
a16z holds referees (Vals $400M Aug 13, LMArena $1.7B, Braintrust) AND players (OpenAI, xAI,
Mistral) — and invokes Moody's, the issuer-pays captured rater, as the model. Six absorptions in
20 months (Arize→Dynatrace $915M, Galileo→Cisco, Langfuse→ClickHouse, Lakera→Check Point…).

**2. Captured referees can't sell what buyers need.**
Issuer-pays buys the rater; buyer-pays buys the truth. A referee paid by the scored cannot credibly
grade the scored. Insurers, deployers and regulators therefore cannot rely on existing scoreboards.

**3. Independence is scarce, and scarcity is priceable.**
The object buyers lack — a signed, third-party, re-measurable evidence record — is exactly what a
buyer-side, COI-screened, signed-artifact body produces. No lab relationships, no lab tokens,
grants never majority.

**4. The price sits on the integrity, not the benchmark.**
The paywall never sits on the benchmark (LMArena gave everything away and its repo is unmaintained).
The private layer sells because the public layer is provably neutral.
