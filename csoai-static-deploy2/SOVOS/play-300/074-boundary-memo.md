Date: 2026-08-21 · lane: K3 · gate: NICK signs; in sales playbook · REAL, D06 §6.

## The line we never cross
- **Never CE-relevant certificates.** Art 28: non-notified certificates are void. We are not, and
  will not claim to be, a notified body.
- **Stay on the evidence/scoring side of Art 43.** We score evidence quality; we do not issue
  conformity certificates or CE marks.
- **Never UKAS/ISO/accreditation as a product claim.** We track ISO 42001 / EN 18286 as *inputs*,
  not as stamps we sell.

## What we DO sell
"Verified measurement credential" — signed, buyer-side, offline-verifiable evidence of a score.
It is an input TO a conformity assessment, never the assessment itself.

## Consequences if breached
- Void certificates = liability + trust destruction. Independence is the asset; a certification
  claim forfeits it (Scale AI = cautionary tale).
