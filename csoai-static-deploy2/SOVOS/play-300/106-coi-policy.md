## 1. The 7 conflict classes (screen every inbound deal)
1. Scored-side capital (lab/vendor equity or sponsorship)
2. Issuer-pays (the rated pays the rater)
3. Board-in-scored (officer sits on a scored party's governance)
4. Grant-in-scored (funding from a party whose artifact we score)
5. Fund-stacking (one fund across evaluator + player + adjacent evaluator)
6. Sponsored-scoreboard (bench sponsors = the scored, TB3 pattern)
7. Access/token dependency (compute or model access from a scored party)

## 2. Management model
Disclosure-based (per leaderboard-governance literature): declare → classify → mitigate/refuse.
Never silence a conflict; publish it in the independence ledger (091) and screen in the CRM.

## 3. Hard refusals (no mitigation path)
- Lab sponsorships · vendor-collab indices · private best-of-N · paid placement (step 095).
- Lab tokens (buy compute at market, publish cost) · grants >50% revenue.

## 4. Training & audit
All lanes trained + quiz on the 7 classes (step 107). Quarterly independence audit (108).
Kill-switch: doctrine-breaching revenue → NICK veto same week.
