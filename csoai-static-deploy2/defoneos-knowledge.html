DEFONEOS Knowledge Graph

Defence-grade semantic infrastructure. Open. RDF/OWL-compatible. SPARQL-queryable.

Graph Structure

Objects: Entities in the defence domain — vessels, aircraft, vehicles, personnel, facilities, cyber assets, satellites.

Links: Relationships between objects — "tracks", "communicates-with", "owned-by", "located-at", "part-of", "targets".

Actions: Operations on objects — "deploy", "intercept", "monitor", "alert", "classify", "respond".

Rules: Business logic — "if confidence >85% AND BFT quorum → escalate", "if vessel has no AIS AND RF detected → classify threat".

SPARQL Query Example

SELECT ?vessel ?threat ?confidence
WHERE {
  ?vessel rdf:type def:Vessel .
  ?vessel def:hasAIS false .
  ?vessel def:detectedBy ?sensor .
  ?vessel def:threatLevel ?threat .
  ?vessel def:confidence ?confidence .
  FILTER(?confidence > 0.85)
}
