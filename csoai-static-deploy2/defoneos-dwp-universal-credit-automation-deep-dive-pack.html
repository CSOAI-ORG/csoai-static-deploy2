2. Fraud Detection AI Governance

Governed AI models for UC overpayment and fraud detection. Bias testing across protected characteristics. Explainable decision support. £8.3B annual fraud target.

3. Work Coach Decision Support

AI-assisted work coach journal analysis. Claimant engagement pattern recognition. Sanction risk assessment with HITL override. 27,000+ work coaches.

4. Health Assessment AI Ethics

Governed WCA decision support. Bias detection across disability categories. Explainable AI for benefit entitlement decisions. Appeals reduction through quality evidence.

🚫 Red Lines (Never Crossed)

  • ❌ No individual claimant surveillance or tracking beyond authorised UC processes
  • ❌ No automated sanction decisions without mandatory human work coach review
  • ❌ No use of AI to reduce legitimate UC entitlements
  • ❌ No sharing of individual UC claim data outside DWP without statutory authority
  • ❌ No predictive models that discriminate by protected characteristics (Equality Act 2010)
  • ❌ No automation that removes the claimant's right to mandatory reconsideration and appeal
