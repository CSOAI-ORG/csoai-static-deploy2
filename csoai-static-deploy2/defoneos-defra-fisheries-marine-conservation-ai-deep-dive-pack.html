🚫 Red Lines (Never Crossed)

  • ❌ No quota allocation without ICES stock assessment data and scientific advisory committee review
  • ❌ No MCZ activity consent without MCZ impact assessment and conservation objective compliance
  • ❌ No aquaculture expansion without full environmental impact assessment and public consultation
  • ❌ No fisheries enforcement action without vessel identification verification and evidence chain
  • ❌ No marine development without marine protected area compatibility assessment
  • ❌ No international quota agreement without parliamentary scrutiny and fisheries industry consultation

🤝 5-Step Engagement Model

1
Discovery: Map fisheries management maturity. Assess MCZ condition status. Benchmark IUU detection capability. Identify aquaculture sustainability gaps.
2
Governance Design: Deploy DEFONEOS MCP servers against fisheries use cases. Configure MCZ monitoring and IUU detection protocols. Establish marine spatial planning governance.
3
Pilot: Run 90-day pilot across 3 fisheries regions. Measure stock assessment improvement. Collect fisherman, conservation, and regulator feedback.
4
Scale: Roll out across all UK fisheries regions. Integrate with MMO and IFCA infrastructure. Deploy across the marine conservation ecosystem.
5
Assure: Continuous audit-grade fisheries governance reporting. Parliamentary evidence generation. International compliance transparency. Conservation outcome metrics.
