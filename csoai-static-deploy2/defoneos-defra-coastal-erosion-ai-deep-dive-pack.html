⚡ 8 Transformation Priorities

1. Prevention Over Cure

Invest in coastal defence before erosion impacts communities. Predictive modelling 10 years ahead. Maintenance-first strategy. Nature-based solutions preferred.

2. Community-First Managed Realignment

No community displaced without 5-year advance notice. Full compensation at market value. Relocation support. Community-led decision making.

3. Insurance for All Coastal Properties

Flood Re extended to all coastal erosion risk properties. Property-level resilience discount. Community flood group insurance pools. Premium transparency.

4. Nature-Based Coastal Defence

70% of new coastal defence investment in nature-based solutions. Salt marsh restoration. Dune rehabilitation. Oyster reef construction. Natural flood management.

5. Digital Coastal Twin

National digital twin of UK coastline. Real-time erosion monitoring. Climate projection overlay. Defence asset integration. Community risk visualization.

6. Climate-Adapted Planning

All coastal planning decisions use 2100 climate projections. Strategic coastal realignment zones. Green infrastructure mandatory. Managed retreat framework.

7. Cross-Agency Coordination

DEFRA/EA/LLFA/Natural England/MCA coastal governance integration. Single data platform. Coordinated investment. Shared community engagement.

8. Parliamentary Accountability

Annual coastal erosion impact report. Investment vs erosion rate metrics. Community displacement statistics. International comparison. Climate risk trajectory.

🚫 Red Lines (Never Crossed)

  • ❌ No coastal community left uninformed of erosion risk within 5 years of projected impact — proactive engagement mandatory
  • ❌ No managed realignment proceeding without community consent and full market-value compensation — people before policy
  • ❌ No coastal property denied insurance access due to erosion risk alone — Flood Re extension mandatory
  • ❌ No new hard coastal defence built where nature-based solution is viable — ecological preference default
  • ❌ No coastal planning decision using outdated climate projections — 2100 minimum horizon required
  • ❌ No cross-agency coastal data silo left unaddressed beyond 12 months — single platform commitment
