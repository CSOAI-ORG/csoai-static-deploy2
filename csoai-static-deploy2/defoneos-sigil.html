DEFONEOS SIGIL Chain

Ed25519-signed cryptographic proof for every AI decision. 49K+ receipts. Immutable. Bitcoin-anchored.

How SIGIL Works

Every time DEFONEOS makes a decision — detects a threat, deploys a swarm, votes in council — it emits a SIGIL receipt. The receipt contains: timestamp, action, parameters, result, agent ID, and Ed25519 digital signature. Receipts are hash-chained: each receipt references the previous receipt's hash. The chain is periodically anchored to the Bitcoin blockchain via OP_RETURN.

Legal Defensibility

SIGIL receipts are legally defensible evidence that an AI system made specific decisions at specific times with specific inputs. Unlike Palantir's centralized logs (which can be edited by the vendor), SIGIL chains are immutable and cryptographically signed. No one — not even CSOAI — can alter a SIGIL receipt after it's emitted.
