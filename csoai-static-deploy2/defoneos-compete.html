The 12-Point Moat Analysis

Palantir does data integration. Anduril does hardware. Helsing does targeting. Scale AI does labelling. None do everything. DEFONEOS covers 12 domains (defoneos-index.html) with actual working tools — not just dashboards.

5-Failure Honesty Register

⚠ Where DEFONEOS will lose, and what we will not pretend about.

  1. Hardware. Anduril builds actual drones, submarines, jammers. DEFONEOS is software + governance only. We integrate with their hardware (and BAE, Thales, Leonardo) but we will NEVER compete on hardware.
  2. Scale delivery. Palantir has 3,000 salespeople to deploy to 100+ defence customers. We have 1 founder + 33 AI agents. We will lose deals where procurement requires white-glove touch and we cannot scale fast enough.
  3. Top-Secret contracts. Without SC clearance we cannot bid on TS work. That's a category ceiling. We can bid on OFFICIAL and SECRET — which is >90% of UK defence procurement — but TS is excluded.
  4. Hypersonics, directed energy, biotech. Categories outside our 12-domain coverage. We will partner (QinetiQ, DSTL) or refer. We do not compete.
  5. Public-sector change management. UK MOD has 200-year procurement culture. Even if our tech is 10× better, we will lose to incumbent Palantir/Anduril/BAE on relationships, references, and trust — until we build those.
The moat above is structural, not absolute. We win the category; we don't win every deal.
