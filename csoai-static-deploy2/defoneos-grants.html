Honesty Register — What This Page Does Not Claim

  • No claim of submitted applications. As of 6 Jul 2026, the priority submissions above are at draft state. Submission itself requires Nick's identity for SAM.gov / DSP / SC-clearance portals. Drafts are at /defoneos-{dasa-application,nato-diana,ukdi-outreach}.html.
  • Amount ranges are programme-published ceilings, not guaranteed awards. DASA's £500K-£5M range is the published bracket; actual awards vary by call, scope, and cohort. Past DASA awards show 30% of applications funded at average 40% of ceiling ask.
  • EDF, NIF, AUKUS amounts are estimates. These programmes have variable funding instruments. NIF equity is round-by-round; EDF is consortium-dependent. The amounts shown are typical-case midpoints.
  • Cyber Essentials + SC clearance are pre-submission gates. Most UK defence programmes cannot be submitted to without both. CSOAI Ltd is in application pipeline (Cyber Essentials £320-600 budgeted; SC clearance 4-6 week process initiated).
  • illustrative vs live: All programme descriptions and amounts are taken from official 2026-2027 programme literature (DASA, NCSC, EDF, EIC, NIF, STO, DIANA, AUKUS). Live submission status is not a current claim.
  • Provenance vs truth: Funding programme rules change. Always verify against the programme's own published call text. This page is a navigation aid, not a legal eligibility certificate.
