🚀 Customer Onboarding

Your first 10 minutes with DEFONEOS. From zero to sovereign defence AI.

1

Sign Up

Complete the signup form at /signup. Choose Demo, Waitlist, or Investor contact.

2

Choose Your Plan

Review pricing at /defoneos-pricing. Starter (£50K/yr), Professional (£250K/yr), Enterprise (£1M+/yr), or Sovereign (custom).

3

Complete Payment

Stripe checkout (when Stripe live key is set). You'll receive a confirmation email with your subscription details.

4

Sandbox Provisioning

Your DEFONEOS sandbox is provisioned on UK GCP. You receive login credentials + API key within 24 hours.

5

Install MCP Servers

pip install defoneos-cyber-mcp defoneos-isr-mcp defoneos-swarm-mcp
6

Connect to SOV3

sov3 connect --mcp-servers cyber,isr,swarm --host YOUR_SANDBOX_HOST
7

Launch Cesium Globe

sov3 globe init --cesium-token YOUR_TOKEN\nsov3 globe set-camera --lat 55.0 --lon -1.0 --alt 1000000
8

Run First Simulation

sov3 sim run --scenario flood_response\n# Watch DEFONEOS detect, decide, act, and SIGIL the result
9

Review SIGIL Audit

Every decision DEFONEOS made during the simulation is Ed25519-signed. Review the audit trail.

10

You're Operational

Welcome to DEFONEOS. Your sovereign defence AI is live. Schedule your first check-in with our team.
