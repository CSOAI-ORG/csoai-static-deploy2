📋 Section 1: System Overview (Annex IV §1)

1.1 Intended Purpose

DEFONEOS is a sovereign open-source defence AI operating system designed for situational awareness, sensor fusion, compliance automation, and democratic oversight of AI-enabled public safety and defence operations. The system is intended for use by UK and allied government agencies operating under JSP 936, JSP 440, and equivalent allied frameworks.

5.2 Override & Kill Switch

Every DEFONEOS action can be killed by any one of: (1) Human command authority, (2) BFT-33 veto (1/33 sufficient to halt), (3) Automated red line detection. Kill is immediate (<100ms) and logged. The system cannot self-restart after a kill without explicit human re-authorization.

🔐 Section 6: Transparency & Explainability (Annex IV §6)

6.1 Audit Trail

Every DEFONEOS action generates a SIGIL receipt — an Ed25519-signed, SHA-256 hash-chained entry on an immutable ledger. Receipts include: timestamp, actor, action, parameters, result, confidence score, model version, human approver (if applicable), and BFT vote tally. Receipts are anchored to Bitcoin's blockchain via OP_RETURN every 1,000 entries for external verification.
