From Edge to Cloud

EDGE: Jetson Orin Nano (40 TOPS) runs quantized models for real-time drone detection, ISR processing, and acoustic classification. $500 device. 15W power.

FOG: Jetson AGX Orin (275 TOPS) at forward operating bases. Runs full SOV3 instance. Connects to M2 Mac for heavier inference.

CLOUD: GCP VM with L4 GPU (current). Planned: H100 cluster for large-scale training. Rubin B200 when available for 10x inference throughput.

OMNIVERSE: For enterprise customers, DEFONEOS exports Cesium scenes to NVIDIA Omniverse for photorealistic digital twins. Real-time bidirectional sync.
