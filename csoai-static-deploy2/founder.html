Nicholas Templeman is the founder and Executive Director of CSOAI Ltd, an independent measurement body for AI compliance. CSOAI does not sell certification — it measures and signs evidence: 30 governance frameworks crosswalked (EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, UK GDPR & DPA 2018, NIS2, DORA, the Cyber Resilience Act and more) anchored to 417 statutory provisions across the EU, UK and US, with every result Ed25519-signed and publicly verifiable offline.

Previously he founded and scaled businesses spanning construction logistics, waste management, agriculture, and technology — the operating base of the "AI-enabled physical economy" that CSOAI governs.

Positioning & independence
