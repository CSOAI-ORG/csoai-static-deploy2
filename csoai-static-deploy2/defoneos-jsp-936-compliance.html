What is JSP 936?

JSP 936 (Joint Service Publication 936) is the UK Ministry of Defence's governing standard for Defence Information Assurance (IA). It establishes the policy framework, risk management process, and security controls required to protect MOD information systems, networks, and data across all classification levels — from OFFICIAL to SECRET and above.

JSP 936 applies to all MOD organisations, defence contractors, and supply chain partners who handle, process, or transmit defence information. It defines a risk-based approach to information assurance, requiring organisations to identify threats, assess vulnerabilities, and implement proportionate controls aligned with the HMG Security Policy Framework (SPF).

For AI systems operating in defence contexts — autonomous decision support, intelligence analysis, logistics optimisation — JSP 936 provides the assurance baseline that must be met before any system can process defence information.

🛡️

Risk-Based Approach

JSP 936 mandates a structured risk management process. Every information system must undergo Business Impact Level (IL) assessment, threat profiling, and vulnerability analysis before accreditation.

IL0 – IL6 Scale

Accreditation Lifecycle

Systems require formal accreditation before processing defence information. JSP 936 defines the Accreditation Dependency Chain: Risk Owner → Accreditor → Service Provider, each with documented responsibilities.

Supply Chain Coverage

JSP 936 extends beyond MOD boundaries to defence contractors (DEFCON 659), subcontractors, and cloud service providers. Every link in the supply chain must demonstrate compliance.

The 14 JSP 936 Security Domains

JSP 936 organises information assurance controls into 14 domains. Each domain addresses a specific area of security management, with defined controls, evidence requirements, and risk assessment criteria.

# Domain Description Criticality Key Controls
1 Security Governance Organisational security policy, roles, responsibilities, and management oversight structure for IA. Critical Security policy documents, RACI matrix, board-level reporting
2 Risk Management Systematic identification, assessment, and treatment of information risks across the system lifecycle. Critical Risk registers, threat assessments, mitigation plans, residual risk acceptance
3 Security Architecture Design and implementation of security architecture including network segmentation, data flows, and trust boundaries. Critical Architecture diagrams, data flow maps, trust boundary documentation
4 Identity & Access Management User identity lifecycle, authentication mechanisms, authorisation models, and privilege management. Critical RBAC policies, MFA enforcement, access reviews, privilege escalation controls
5 Data Protection Classification, handling, storage, transmission, and destruction of defence information. Critical Classification marking, encryption at rest/transit, DLP, secure disposal
6 System Hardening Secure configuration baselines, patch management, and platform hardening standards. High CIS benchmarks, patch SLAs, build standards, configuration drift monitoring
7 Network Security Network architecture, segmentation, monitoring, and defence-in-depth controls. High Firewall rules, IDS/IPS, network monitoring, segmentation validation
8 Malware Protection Prevention, detection, and response to malicious code across endpoints and infrastructure. High EDR deployment, signature updates, behavioural analysis, sandboxing
9 Change Management Controlled change processes for systems, configurations, and security controls. Medium CAB approval, change records, rollback procedures, impact assessments
10 Incident Management Detection, reporting, response, and lessons-learned from security incidents. High Incident response plans, reporting timelines, forensic capability, post-incident review
11 Business Continuity Continuity planning, disaster recovery, and resilience of defence systems. High BCP/DRP documentation, RTO/RPO targets, failover testing, backup verification
12 Audit & Compliance Continuous monitoring, audit trails, and demonstrable compliance with IA requirements. High Audit logging, SIEM integration, compliance dashboards, gap analysis
13 Personnel Security Security vetting, training, awareness, and insider threat management. Medium SC/DV clearance records, security awareness training, insider threat programme
14 Physical Security Physical protection of facilities, equipment, and classified material. Medium Access controls, CCTV, secure storage, visitor management, zone accreditation

Key finding: Cyber Essentials+ covers only 5 of 14 JSP 936 domains. Defence organisations must supplement with additional controls to achieve full JSP 936 compliance. NIST CSF 2.0 and ISO 27001:2022 provide substantially broader coverage but lack defence-specific context (IL levels, DEFCON obligations, accreditation chains).

JSP 936 Compliance Checklist

Use this checklist to assess your organisation's readiness for JSP 936 accreditation. Each item corresponds to a specific control requirement. DEFONEOS automates evidence collection for starred (★) items.

Governance & Risk (Domains 1–2)

  • ★ Information Security Policy approved by Senior Information Risk Owner (SIRO)
  • ★ Security roles and responsibilities documented (RACI matrix)
  • ★ Business Impact Level (IL) assessment completed for all systems
  • ★ Risk register maintained with threat, vulnerability, and impact scores
  • ★ Residual risk formally accepted by Risk Owner with documented rationale
  • ★ Annual security governance review conducted and minuted

Architecture & Access (Domains 3–4)

  • ★ Security architecture diagram current and reviewed quarterly
  • ★ Data flow maps identify all cross-boundary information transfers
  • ★ Trust boundaries formally documented with associated controls
  • ★ RBAC model implemented with least-privilege enforcement
  • ★ Multi-factor authentication deployed for all privileged access
  • ★ Access reviews conducted quarterly with evidence of leavers process

Data & Systems (Domains 5–8)

  • ★ Data classification scheme applied to all information assets
  • ★ Encryption at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3) for IL3+ data
  • ★ System hardening baselines (CIS/NCSC) applied and drift-monitored
  • ★ Patch management SLAs met: Critical 72h, High 14d, Medium 30d
  • ★ EDR solution deployed on all endpoints with 24/7 monitoring
  • ★ Network segmentation validated with penetration testing evidence

Operations & Resilience (Domains 9–12)

  • ★ Change management process with CAB approval for security-impacting changes
  • ★ Incident response plan tested annually with tabletop exercises
  • ★ Security incident reporting within 1 hour for Category 1 incidents
  • ★ Business continuity plan with defined RTO/RPO for all critical systems
  • ★ Disaster recovery tested annually with documented results
  • ★ Audit logging enabled on all systems with 12-month retention
  • ★ SIEM operational with correlation rules for defence-specific threats

People & Physical (Domains 13–14)

  • ★ All personnel with system access hold appropriate vetting (SC/DV)
  • ★ Security awareness training completed annually (evidence retained)
  • ★ Insider threat programme operational with reporting mechanisms
  • ★ Physical access controls appropriate to classification level
  • ★ Secure storage for classified material meets JSP 440 requirements

Why Cross-Framework Alignment Matters

Defence organisations increasingly operate across multiple compliance regimes simultaneously. A supplier might need JSP 936 accreditation for MOD contracts, Cyber Essentials+ for government procurement, and ISO 27001 for commercial clients. DEFONEOS eliminates the "compliance tax" by collecting evidence once and mapping it to all applicable frameworks.

🎯

Cyber Essentials+ Gaps

Cyber Essentials+ addresses only 5 of 14 JSP 936 domains (access control, secure configuration, boundary firewalls, malware, patch management). JSP 936 adds governance, risk, architecture, incidents, continuity, audit, personnel, and physical security.

🇺🇸

NIST CSF 2.0 Alignment

NIST CSF 2.0's Govern (GV), Identify (ID), Protect (PR), Detect (DE), Respond (RS), and Recover (RC) functions map well to JSP 936 domains. However, NIST lacks defence-specific constructs: IL levels, DEFCON obligations, and the MOD accreditation chain.

🌍

ISO 27001:2022 Coverage

ISO 27001:2022 Annex A provides the broadest technical control coverage (93 controls across 4 themes). Nearly all JSP 936 domains map to ISO 27001 controls, but the standard lacks defence operational context and supply chain assurance requirements.
