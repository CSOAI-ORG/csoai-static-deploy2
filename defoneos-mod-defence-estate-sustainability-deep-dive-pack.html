🚫 Red Lines (Never Crossed)

  • ❌ No environmental monitoring that compromises operational security of defence bases
  • ❌ No AI-driven estate disposals without MOD Infrastructure Board human approval
  • ❌ No biodiversity AI that restricts military training area access without operational assessment
  • ❌ No sharing of defence estate location-specific data without security classification review
  • ❌ No automated building closure decisions without human facility management sign-off
  • ❌ No sustainability reporting that reveals operational tempo, troop movements, or capability gaps
