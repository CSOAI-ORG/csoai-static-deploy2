⛔ 6 Sovereign Red Lines

  • 🚫 No CCUS project approval initiated without DESNZ CCUS Directorate human sign-off — financial commitments over £100M require Ministerial approval.
  • 🚫 No geological storage data processed outside UK sovereign infrastructure — NSTA seismic data and reservoir models are nationally strategic assets.
  • 🚫 No carbon credit issued without independent MRV verification by accredited third party — automated credit generation requires human certification gate.
  • 🚫 No UK ETS carbon price manipulation or artificial market signal — carbon market intelligence is for analysis only, not automated trading or intervention.
  • 🚫 No CO₂ transport pipeline routing determined without HSE safety case approval and Local Planning Authority human consent for all onshore routes.
  • 🚫 No international CO₂ transport agreement data shared without DESNZ International Directorate and FCDO clearance — bilateral treaty obligations apply.
