🔌 What powers this

Reading from /api/sov-arena/rounds.jsonl (Cloudflare Pages Function, KV SOV_ARENA_STATE) and /api/sov-openttd/state.jsonl (KV SOV_OPENTTD_STATE). Both are 5-min tail-sync from the substrate running on oracle-micro-2. Every event is Ed25519-signed upstream. The dashboard renders honest numbers — if the substrate is down, the feed says so, no fabrication.
