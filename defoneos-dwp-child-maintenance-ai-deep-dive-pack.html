🚫 Red Lines (Never Crossed)

  • ❌ No automated maintenance calculation override without human caseworker review and notification to both parties
  • ❌ No enforcement action (DEO, bank deduction, passport curfew) without prior notice and appeal opportunity
  • ❌ No DA survivor contact details shared with paying parent under any circumstances
  • ❌ No child poverty assessment used to deny benefits without DWP decision maker and appeal pathway
  • ❌ No cross-border maintenance recovery without Central Authority legal review
  • ❌ No imprisonment recommendation without full tribunal review and legal representation opportunity
