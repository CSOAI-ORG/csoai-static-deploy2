## The Opportunity

The EU AI Act requires provenance markings for high-risk AI systems (Article 50). The UK is developing its own AI governance framework. Both need a measurement instrument that is:

- **Deterministic**: not LLM-as-judge
- **Measurable**: every verdict has an n, an interval, and a signed record
- **Reproducible**: anyone can recompute the chain locally
- **Honest**: we publish our own failures (7 refutations, 4 killed our own bets)

CSOAI has built this instrument. We are seeking a standards partner to validate and scale it.
