# Sovereign Town — Narrated Demo Script

## Hook (15s)

> “What if you could watch two versions of the same AI town — one governed, one
> ungoverned — and prove which regulation actually works?”

## Setup (30s)

1. Open `/fleet-status.html`.
2. Narrate: 28 hives, 24/7 signed episodes, zero governed crimes in production.
3. Open `/experiments.html`.
4. Show the DORA Finance experiment card.

## The experiment (60s)

1. Click into `/experiments/dora-finance.html`.
2. Explain treatment (automated DORA reporting) vs control (manual desk).
3. Highlight the metrics: detection time, missed incidents, cost index.
4. Show the aggregate comparison table.

## Live simulation (60s)

1. Open `/dashboard`.
2. Switch the WebSocket feed to **ungoverned** and watch crime/violation counts rise.
3. Switch back to **governed** and show the system stabilise.
4. Open `/town3d` to visualise the same town in 3D.

## Trust primitives (45s)

1. Open `/passport` or `/verify`.
2. Paste a sample passport JSON and click verify.
3. Explain Ed25519 signatures, hash-chained ledger, offline verification.
4. Open `/run.html?id=<run_id>` and show the signed manifest.

## Close (15s)

> “Sovereign Town turns AI governance from a slide deck into reproducible,
> signed evidence. Every policy experiment is a manifest you can verify.”
