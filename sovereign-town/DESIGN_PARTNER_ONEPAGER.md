# CSOAI Sovereign Town — design-partner one-pager

**What it is.** A governed agent-world that proves CSOAI governance works — and a live engine that turns
that proof into per-industry compliance intelligence + verifiable agent identity. In-simulation today (P0/P1),
running 24/7, every result cryptographically attested.

Robust across a 15-cell parameter sweep (governance holds in **every** setting) and across **28 industry
hives** running 24/7 — **20M+ self-labelled episodes** generated, every one Ed25519 hash-chained and
**verifiable by anyone, offline, at proofof.ai/passport.**

## Three things that make it real (not slideware)
1. **Tamper-evident record** — EU AI Act Art-12/14-grade: verify any run/agent with the public key alone, no server.
2. **The flywheel fixes models** — a model trained on the governed episodes hits 0.99 accuracy where our own prior production model was degenerate. The data compounds into a moat.
3. **The Looking Glass** — pre-compute outcomes under any regulatory regime (EU AI Act / DORA / NIST) *before* a rule is finalised or a firm invests. A wind-tunnel for regulation.

## What we're NOT (the discipline that makes it fundable)
Public data + simulation-labelled only · **never** assert a named firm is non-compliant · opt-in · regulators
get anonymized/aggregate intelligence, never name-and-shame · consent-based personal vault, never surveillance ·
defensive-only. The version that survives a regulator, a court, and due diligence.
