# Sovereign Town — Design Partner One-Pager

**The live argument for governed multi-agent AI.**

---

## The one-sentence pitch

Sovereign Town is a real-time simulation of 28 autonomous AI hives that proves, visually and auditabley, why governance architecture determines whether multi-agent systems thrive or collapse.

## Why it matters now

Emergence-era platforms are launching ungoverned agent swarms into high-stakes verticals — legal, logistics, finance, health, fisheries. Sovereign Town asks the counterfactual nobody else is testing: **same agents, same incentives, with and without an enforceable care gate.**

## The demo

Open `http://127.0.0.1:3940/town3d` and toggle **Governed / Ungoverned**.

- **Governed:** zero crimes, commons intact, trust stable.
- **Ungoverned:** scarcity triggers theft contagion, commons collapse, trust goes to zero.

The viewer streams live ticks from the Python engine. Every episode is Ed25519-signed and hash-chained.

## The moat

Data advantage comes not from hoarding episodes but from **grounding the simulation in live public data**: CISA KEV, OFAC SDN, UK Companies House PSC, FRED macro and energy series, FAOSTAT, NOAA climate anomalies, and MEOK compliance attestations. The sim becomes a wind tunnel for policy: change the real-world input, watch the town respond.

## Use cases

- **Regulators / policymakers:** test the mechanical effect of a rule before writing it.
- **Enterprise AI buyers:** evaluate vendor claims about “safe agents” against a reproducible baseline.
- **AI labs:** add a governed-world training distribution to alignment research.
- **Vertical SaaS:** demonstrate compliance posture to customers with a live, auditable demo.
