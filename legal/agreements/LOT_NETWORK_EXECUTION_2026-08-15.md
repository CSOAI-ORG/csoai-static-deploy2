## What LOT Network provides (from the executed text — verified)
- **License + release:** each LOT User grants every other Licensee a present, vested,
  irrevocable license to its Subject Patents (Sec 1.1).
- **Troll-immunity (Sec 1.2):** if a LOT User's patent is transferred to an **Assertion Entity**
  (PAE/troll), every other LOT User gets a license + waiver of patent assertion against them.
- **Return of financial benefit (Sec 1.4):** if a member receives payment from patent-assertion
  based on a patent transferred to a troll <4 months before, that payment is cancelled/returned.
- **No other rights (Sec 1.3):** the license is narrowly scoped to Subject Patents — does NOT
  give away the measurement IP, card format, or instruments.
