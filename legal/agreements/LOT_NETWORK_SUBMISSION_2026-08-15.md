## What LOT Network provides
LOT (License on Transfer) is a defensive patent agreement: if any member company's patents fall
into the hands of a patent assertion entity (PAE) / troll, all other members automatically receive
a license to those patents. Members agree that their patents, if transferred to a troll, become
licensed to all other LOT members — neutralizing trolls' core weapon (acquiring patents to sue).
