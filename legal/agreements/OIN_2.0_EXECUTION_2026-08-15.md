## What this commits CSOAI to (the honest scope)
OIN 2.0 is a **patent non-aggression pact** around the **Linux System**:

### CSOAI receives:
- Royalty-free, worldwide, non-exclusive license under **OIN Patents** to make/use/import/distribute
  any products or services (Sec 1.1)
- For the **Linux System** specifically: also protected from inducement/contributory-infringement claims (Sec 1.1)

### CSOAI grants back (Sec 1.2 — THE KEY SCOPE LIMIT):
- A royalty-free license to Licensees **ONLY for the "Linux System"** — NOT for CSOAI's core
  innovation, NOT for measurement instruments, NOT for the signed-card stack.
- Representations: CSOAI has full right to grant; no pending claim against others re: Linux System.

### Critical boundary — CSOAI's crown jewels are NOT in scope:
- "Your Patents" are licensed **only to the extent they cover making/using/distributing the Linux System**.
- The 3KB signed-card format, the GSPC measurement axes, the evasion/protection stacks,
  quantum/OWEM infrastructure — **none of these are Linux-System patents, so none are licensed away.**
- "Linux System" is a defined term per the OIN Website — a Linux-kernel-scoped definition, NOT
  "anything CSOAI makes."

### Key mechanics:
- **Capture Period:** patents captured run from Agreement Date until termination/limitation.
- **Limitation Election (Sec 2.2):** CSOAI can EXCLUDE patents via 30-day notice — a control lever.
- **Termination (Sec 3):** mutual; CSOAI's license ends if it sues a Licensee for Linux-System
  infringement; Licenses it won't be dragged into the GPL compliance (Sec 5.4).
- **Third-party beneficiaries (Sec 5.5):** every Licensee can enforce directly against CSOAI.
- **Governing law:** New York (Sec 5.6).
