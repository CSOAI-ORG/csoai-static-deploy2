The Police Investigations & Review Commissioner (PIRC) is Scotland's independent police oversight body — established under the Police and Fire Reform (Scotland) Act 2012 — responsible for investigating serious incidents involving Police Scotland, reviewing complaint handling, and conducting independent investigations into deaths in police custody, firearms discharges, and allegations of serious misconduct. This pack provides a sovereign AI governance framework for every PIRC scrutiny domain.

📋 12 Entry Points

COMPLAINT

Complaint Handling Review (CHR)

Independent statutory review of Police Scotland's handling of public complaints — AI governance for complaint triage, categorisation consistency, and pattern-of-conduct detection across Scotland's 13 policing divisions.

DEATH

Independent Death Investigations

Mandatory PIRC investigation of all deaths following police contact — deaths in custody, fatal road collisions, and deaths following police use of force — AI governance for case-file management and forensic evidence synthesis.

SERIOUS

Serious Incident & Conduct Reviews

Investigation of serious incidents and allegations of misconduct against senior police officers — AI governance for evidence cataloguing, timeline reconstruction, and witness-interview pattern analysis.

FIREARMS

Firearms Discharge Investigations

Mandatory PIRC investigation of every police firearm discharge in Scotland — conventional firearms, taser, AEP, and baton round — AI governance for forensic ballistics correlation, weapon-telemetry records, and use-of-force proportionality assessment.

CUSTODY

Custody Death & Serious Injury Review

Investigation of all deaths and serious injuries in police custody suites — AI governance for CCTV timeline analysis, custody-log reconciliation, and PACE (Scotland) compliance verification across all Police Scotland custody centres.

FORCE

Use of Force Oversight

Independent review of police use of force — restraint, spit-hoods, limb restraints — AI governance for use-of-force data analytics, de-escalation effectiveness measurement, and pattern-of-conduct trend reporting across divisions.

AUDIT

Police Scotland & SPA Audit Interface

PIRC recommendations to Police Scotland and the Scottish Police Authority (SPA) — AI governance for recommendation-tracking, compliance monitoring, and follow-up audit-cycle management with Parliamentary accountability.

WHISTLE

Whistleblowing & Protected Disclosures

Review of whistleblowing reports and protected disclosures within Police Scotland — AI governance for disclosure triage, reporter-protection verification, and investigation-outcome tracking under the Public Interest Disclosure Act 1998.

CONFIDENCE

Public Confidence & Community Scrutiny

Community engagement on police legitimacy and accountability — AI governance for public-confidence sentiment analysis, demographic disparity detection in complaint outcomes, and community-trust measurement frameworks.

LEGISLATIVE

Legislative Review & Policy Advisory

PIRC advice to Scottish Ministers and the Scottish Parliament on police oversight legislation — AI governance for legislative-impact modelling, international comparator analysis, and policy-option assessment.

INTERNATIONAL

International Oversight Standards (OPCAT)

UN Optional Protocol to the Convention against Torture (OPCAT) compliance — AI governance for National Preventive Mechanism coordination, detention-inspection analytics, and international human rights reporting obligations.

REPORTING

Scottish Parliament Accountability & Reporting

Annual reports, thematic reviews, and special reports to the Scottish Parliament — AI governance for investigation-statistics compilation, redaction assurance, and publication-quality report assembly under FOI (Scotland) Act 2002.

🚫 6 Non-Negotiable Red Lines

  • No AI-sole determination of complaints. All public complaint categorisation, investigation findings, and recommendations are human-determined. AI assists — it never decides complaint outcomes. Police and Fire Reform (Scotland) Act 2012 duty-of-independence.
  • No AI-sole death-investigation conclusions. All death-investigation findings, causation assessments, and Crown Office referrals are human-led with professional investigative judgment.
  • No AI surveillance of individual police officers. Workforce analytics assess divisional-level trends — no individual officer tracking, no automated performance scoring, no psychometric inference without explicit consent.
  • No automated disciplinary or conduct recommendations. AI governance tools may highlight patterns but never recommend specific disciplinary outcomes, fitness-to-practise findings, or conduct sanctions.
  • No legal-privilege breach. All investigation files, legal advice, and Crown Office communications remain privileged — AI tools operate only on non-privileged data with human privilege-review gates.
  • No investigation evidence leaves UK jurisdiction. All complaint files, death-investigation records, and forensic evidence remain UK-resident. DPA 2018 Part 3 (law enforcement processing) enforced by sovereign-keystore geographic binding.
