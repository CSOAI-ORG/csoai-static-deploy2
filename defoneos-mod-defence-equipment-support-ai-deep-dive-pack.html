🚫 Red Lines (Never Crossed)

  • ❌ No equipment programme cancellation without Secretary of State for Defence and Cabinet Office approval
  • ❌ No single source contract price modification without SSRO referral review and Defined Pricing Structure compliance
  • ❌ No AUKUS technology transfer without export control licence and security classification verification
  • ❌ No defence supplier blacklisting without MOD Supplier Relations Board determination and appeal pathway
  • ❌ No equipment plan affordability rebalancing without Treasury and Defence Board joint authorisation
  • ❌ No DIO estate disposal without Defence Infrastructure Planning Committee and DIO Chief Executive approval
