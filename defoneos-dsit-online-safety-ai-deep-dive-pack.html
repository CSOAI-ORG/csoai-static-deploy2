🚫 Red Lines (Never Crossed)

  • ❌ No mass surveillance of private communications without specific judicial warrant
  • ❌ No automated content removal without transparent appeals process and human review pathway
  • ❌ No age verification that requires biometric data without explicit consent and data minimisation
  • ❌ No AI classifier deployed for CSAM/terrorism detection without independent accuracy audit
  • ❌ No encryption backdoor mandated without Parliamentary vote and judicial oversight
  • ❌ No AI-generated content detection without proportionality assessment and false positive reporting
