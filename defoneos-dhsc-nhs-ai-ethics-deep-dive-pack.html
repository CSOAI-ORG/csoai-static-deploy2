🚫 Red Lines (Never Crossed)

  • ❌ No individual patient surveillance or tracking beyond authorised NHS care pathways
  • ❌ No automated clinical decisions without mandatory clinician review and override
  • ❌ No use of AI to deny or reduce legitimate NHS care entitlements
  • ❌ No sharing of individual patient data outside NHS without statutory authority and consent
  • ❌ No predictive models that discriminate by protected characteristics (Equality Act 2010)
  • ❌ No AI that bypasses the patient's right to refuse AI-assisted care
