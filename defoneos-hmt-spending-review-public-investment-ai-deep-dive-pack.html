⛔ 6 Sovereign Red Lines

  • 🚫 No fiscal forecast or OBR-sensitive data published without HM Treasury Press Office clearance and ONS pre-release access protocols.
  • 🚫 No spending review envelope data processed outside UK sovereign infrastructure — Cabinet-level classification applies to all SR modelling.
  • 🚫 No UKIB deal data shared with commercial competitors or market makers — market-sensitive investment intelligence requires HM Treasury approval.
  • 🚫 No automated debt management actions (gilt issuance, NS&I rate changes) initiated without DMO Chief Executive and Exchequer Secretary authorisation.
  • 🚫 No regional investment allocation adjusted by AI without Levelling Up Cabinet Committee human sign-off and Minister for the Cabinet Office approval.
  • 🚫 No whole-government accounts data consolidated outside GCSx-rated infrastructure — OAG audit trail and NAO access preserved at all times.
