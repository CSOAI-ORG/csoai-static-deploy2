Preamble

The UN Global Digital Compact (GDC) was adopted at the Summit of the Future on 22 September 2024. The GDC is a UN Secretary-General framework for digital governance. The GDC has 5 sections covering digital cooperation + AI governance + data governance + digital commons + global digital cooperation.

Section 1 — Digital Cooperation

The signatories commit to: (a) inclusive digital cooperation; (b) bridging the digital divide; (c) supporting digital public goods; (d) ensuring digital human rights.

Section 2 — AI Governance

The signatories commit to: (a) safe + secure + trustworthy AI; (b) human rights in AI; (c) AI ethics + accountability; (d) international AI cooperation.

Section 3 — Data Governance

The signatories commit to: (a) data rights + protections; (b) data sharing for the public good; (c) cross-border data flows with safeguards; (d) data sovereignty.

Section 4 — Digital Commons

The signatories commit to: (a) digital public infrastructure; (b) open-source + open-data; (c) digital commons as global public goods; (d) sustainable financing.

Section 5 — Global Digital Cooperation

The signatories commit to: (a) UN-led digital governance; (b) multistakeholder approach; (c) capacity building; (d) monitoring + accountability.
