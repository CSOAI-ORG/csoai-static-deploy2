6) Deployment topology (sovereign)

DEFONEOS uses the Frozen+Fluid pattern: frozen audited families (governed baseline) + fluid adapters (task-specific) served via UK-sovereign endpoints. No kinetic targeting, no personal surveillance, no third-party tracking.

L1 Identity  →  L2 Execution  →  L3 Council (BFT)  →  SIGIL receipts
