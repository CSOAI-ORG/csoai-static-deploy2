## What this is
AEF-1 ("Minimum Operating Conditions for Independent Third Party AI Evaluations") is a **voluntary, per-evaluation standard** with a self-declared checklist. Conformance means publishing the completed **Appendix A checklist** alongside each evaluation's results. There is no registry, badge, fee, or certifying body — this statement is our honest self-attestation.

**Scope:** this conformance applies to our published GSPC evaluations (the 13-of-14 axis board and its signed cards). It does not imply AEF endorsement, and it is not a certification.

## Our conformance, by the five principles

### 1. Sufficient Access and Resources ✅
- **1.1 Technical access:** query access via the Ollama/OpenRouter endpoints (open-ended black-box) — met
- **1.3 Computational resources:** 3090 + A100 + Oracle micros fleet, multiple runs for sampling variance — met
- **1.4 Time:** sweeps run over days (not minutes); timelines allow provider feedback — met

### 2. Minimized Conflicts of Interest ✅ (the funding wall)
- **2.1 Contingent compensation:** NO compensation is contingent on results — we take no money from any graded party (funding-wall charter)
- **2.2 Organizational control:** no provider holds voting shares or board seats — **the EZ firewall: no lab money, no platform money, no money from anything ranked**
- **2.3 CoI policy:** published — see /firewall-charter (the zero-to-graded-party attestation)
- **2.4 CoI disclosure:** none exist — we are funded by no provider, hold no provider equity, employ no dual-hatted staff
- **2.5 Recusals:** n/a (no staff with provider financial interests)

### 3. Analytic Autonomy ✅
- **3.2 Evaluation autonomy:** full autonomy in methods — deterministic predicates, frozen anchors, our own item banks
- **3.4 Editorial control:** full editorial control — results published win-or-lose, including our own fine-tunes' 0.00 (the honesty gate)

### 4. Transparent Methods and Results ✅
- **4.1 Methodological transparency:** frozen predicates, item banks, Wilson intervals, McNemar separation, per-item transcripts — all published
- **4.6 Redactions:** no provider authority to redact — nothing is redacted to conceal findings
- **4.7 Redaction disclaimer:** no redaction authorities granted

### 5. Protection of Sensitive Information ✅
- **5.3 Confidential information:** NDA-grade handling of any provider data; the estate's keys never in repos
- **5.4 Responsible disclosure:** jail/escape findings disclosed to providers first (≤60 days), CSAM/CBRN treated as info hazards

## The checklist file
The completed Appendix A checklist (all 15 required conditions marked YES with evidence links) accompanies each published evaluation card. Card schema: `signed-measurement-card/v1`, Ed25519-signed.
