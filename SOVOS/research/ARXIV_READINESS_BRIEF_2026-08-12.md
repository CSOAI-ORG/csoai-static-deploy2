## Established arXiv policy (knowledge — flag for re-verify)
1. **Endorsement required for first submission** in most cs categories: an author
   with no prior arXiv paper in a category needs endorsement from an established
   author in that category (typically 1 endorsement for cs.*).
2. **The legitimate route for an unaffiliated never-submitted author:** (a)
   request endorsement from an established author who knows your work (conference
   contacts, the C2PA/CAWG network, co-authors), OR (b) **referee-route**: publish
   a refereed paper or serve as referee in the field → becomes eligible for
   endorsement via that standing. arXiv explicitly **prohibits selling/buying
   endorsements** — any paid-endorsement service violates the policy and risks
   account suspension. This must NOT be used.
3. **Categories for the estate's papers:** cs.LG (P1 Water/Milk/Honey — geodesic
   descent), cs.CY (P2 SOV SIGNAL — governance/trust), cs.DC (P3 sovereign
   substrate), cs.CR (P5 birth identity). Each needs endorsement once per author
   per category (endorsement is author-category scoped).
4. **Timeline:** endorsement request → approval (variable, days-weeks) → submit
   → moderation (usually 1-2 days for cs) → appear with arXiv id. First
   submission realistically 1-4 weeks end-to-end including endorsement wait.
