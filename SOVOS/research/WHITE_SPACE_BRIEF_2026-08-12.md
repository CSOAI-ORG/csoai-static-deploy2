## The wedge (estate thesis, from financial baseline)
Every competitor is a **self-attested SaaS dashboard** (you run their scanner,
you see your score, you report it). The white-space: **third-party, independently
SIGNED benchmark attestation** — a financial-auditor-style opinion that a model
or system demonstrably complies, carrying a cryptographic (Ed25519) signature an
insurer/regulator can verify offline. Nobody in the crowded field does signed
third-party attestation; they do self-service reporting.

## Verdict (honest)
The wedge is **structurally real** IF two things hold: (1) buyer demand for
independent signed evidence over self-report (true for insurers/regulators, the
high-trust buyers — this is the ProvBench/Art 50 angle: "prove the whole
pipeline"), and (2) the estate can establish neutrality (no equity/board/success
fees from audited orgs — Charter Article 0, the Watchdog immunity rule). Both
hold in the canon. **The differentiator is not measurement — it's SIGNED,
THIRD-PARTY, VERIFIABLE measurement.**

## 3 positioning recommendations
1. **Lead with the signature, not the score.** "Any vendor can score your AI.
   One can prove it." The signed System Card + offline-verifiable Ed25519 is the

2. **Target the 2 Aug 2027 high-risk clock.** Sell the "be ready for high-risk
   obligations with independently attested evidence" story — the dated deadline

3. **Charter Article 0 as moat + trust.** Publicly commit (already done) to no
