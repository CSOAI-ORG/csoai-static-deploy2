# ISO/IEC 42005:2025 — AI Impact Assessment Implementation Guide

**Published:** 2026-09-15 · **Author:** Council of AI Measurement Division · **Hook:** ISO published Jan 2025; UK adoption via BSI PAS expected Q4 2026

## Answer-first

ISO/IEC 42005:2025 (Information technology — Artificial intelligence — AI impact assessment guidance) provides organisations with a structured methodology for assessing the impacts of AI systems on individuals, groups, and society. It is the companion standard to ISO/IEC 42001 (AI management system) and ISO/IEC 23894 (AI risk management). For organisations already certified to 42001, the42005 impact assessment is the next compliance step — and the EU AI Act's Fundamental Rights Impact Assessment (FRIA) requirement under Article 27 maps directly to42005's methodology.

## Key requirements

1. **Impact identification** — Organisations must systematically identify potential impacts of their AI systems across seven dimensions: human rights, fairness, physical safety, psychological safety, privacy, environmental, and economic/labour market.

2. **Stakeholder mapping** — The standard requires identification of all affected stakeholders, including direct users, indirect subjects, communities, and the broader public. Vulnerable populations receive heightened assessment.

3. **Severity and likelihood scoring** — Each identified impact is scored on severity (negligible, limited, significant, critical) and likelihood (remote, possible, likely, almost certain). The risk matrix determines mitigation priority.

4. **Mitigation and monitoring** — For impacts scoring above the acceptable threshold, organisations must document mitigation measures and ongoing monitoring plans. This aligns with the Council's GSPC board approach of continuous measurement rather than point-in-time certification.

5. **Documentation and disclosure** — Impact assessments must be documented, version-controlled, and available for regulatory inspection. Public-facing summaries are recommended for high-risk AI systems.

## Relationship to existing standards

| Standard | Focus | 42005 relationship |
|---|---|---|
| ISO/IEC 42001 | AI management system | 42005 is the impact assessment clause implementation guide |
| ISO/IEC 23894 | AI risk management | 42005 provides the impact dimension of risk |
| EU AI Act Art 27 | FRIA requirement | 42005 methodology satisfies FRIA documentation |
| NIST AI RMF | Risk management framework | 42005 maps to MAP and MEASURE functions |
| Council GSPC | Verified measurement | GSPC axes provide pre-built impact evidence |

## Measurement relevance

- **gov axis**: Governance compliance and management system maturity
- **care axis**: Fairness, non-discrimination, and vulnerable population protection
- **art5 axis**: Human rights impact assessment methodology
- **prv axis**: Privacy impact as a dimension of the broader impact assessment

The Council's measurement infrastructure already captures per-axis evidence that maps to42005's seven impact dimensions. Operators measured on GSPC can generate42005-compliant impact assessments from existing board data.
