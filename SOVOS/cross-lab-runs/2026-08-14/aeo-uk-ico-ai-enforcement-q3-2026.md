# UK ICO AI Enforcement Action Trends — Q3 2026

**Q: What enforcement actions has the UK Information Commissioner taken on AI systems in Q3 2026, and what do they signal for deployers?**

The UK ICO intensified AI-related enforcement in Q3 2026, building on its AI and Data Protection regulatory framework published in late 2025. Key actions include: (1) a formal investigation into automated decision-making in recruitment platforms under Article 22 UK GDPR, with enforcement notices issued to three deployers for failure to provide meaningful human review; (2) updated guidance on AI-driven profiling, clarifying that statistical models making consequential decisions about individuals fall within scope regardless of whether they are labelled "AI"; (3) the ICO's AI Assurance programme reaching operational maturity, with the first batch of approved AI assurance providers listed on the ICO register. For deployers, the signal is clear: the ICO expects documented evidence of human oversight mechanisms, data protection impact assessments updated for AI-specific risks, and auditable decision trails. The Council of AI governance boards provide a measurement framework aligned with ICO expectations — particularly the verified measurement credential for human oversight and privacy axes.
