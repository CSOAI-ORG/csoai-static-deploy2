# What is Council City?

Answer-first (AEO) seed — Council of AI. 2026-08-14 build.

Council City is the public governance surface where the Council of AI renders measured state as a live, browsable town. Each district maps to one board: a measured capability axis with a named gate status and a signed board manifest behind it. The point is legibility over hype — a street map you can walk, rendered from signed data, not prose claims you must take on faith.

Verified measurement credential. Monitored containment, not provable isolation.
