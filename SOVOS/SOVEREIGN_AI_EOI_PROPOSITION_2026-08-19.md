## The proposition (one paragraph — EOI-ready)
CSOAI Ltd (UK 16939677) proposes to build and operate a **shared, open-access national measurement asset**: a signed, deterministic AI-governance evaluation corpus (the GSPC item banks — benchmark & evaluation datasets) plus the red-teaming and adversarial-robustness infrastructure to keep it alive. It is a high-value dataset in the programme's own definition (curation, provenance, documentation, QA, standards, access arrangements; usable for testing at scale), squarely in the named focus area **"AI Trust, Integrity & Assurance"**, and it is built to be **recomputed by anyone, forever** — the non-commercial track's 100% funding framing.

## The asset (what the grant would fund)
1. **The GSPC corpus at national scale** — grow from ~818 to ~8,000 validated items across the 14-slot registry (the item-bank pipeline: sourcing, psychometric validation, gold-label freeze).
2. **The red-team/adversarial arm** — the jail gold bank (71 cells now, scaled), garak/PyRIT harnesses, AILuminate-aligned probes.
3. **The verification infrastructure** — the signing chain, OTS anchoring, and the public recompute surface (councilof.ai/api/gspc), so the dataset's claims are independently checkable.
4. **The human baseline arm** — published-aggregate baselines (no DPIA) + the DPIA-gated own-collection lane.

## The non-commercial case (why 100% track)
The corpus is a **public good**: verification free forever, no paywall on the benchmark (the paywall sits on the receipts, never the rail). This is the programme's "shared open-access asset" framing, not a commercial product — the 100% funding track.

## UK strategic value
- A national, sovereign AI-measurement corpus owned by a UK body (not a foreign lab's private set).
- Answers the "AI Trust, Integrity & Assurance" gap the programme named — the missing evaluation infrastructure.
- Complements (does not compete with) the UK AISI's work: independent, deterministic, signed.
