Dear AI Verify Foundation,

CSOAI LTD (UK 16939677) is an independent AI measurement body. We operate the
GSPC (Governance, Safety, Privacy, Care) measurement suite — a 14-axis signed (13 GSPC + jail)
benchmark that produces cryptographically attestable measurement cards for AI
systems.

We wish to express early interest in the AI Tester Accreditation Programme
announced at ISE 2026. Our signed measurement card format (Ed25519 over SCITT
RFC 9943 transparency) maps directly onto both the technical-testing and
process-check dimensions of the AI Verify Testing Framework:

- Technical tests: our 14 measured axes (13 GSPC + jail) cover fairness, robustness, transparency,
  safety, and governance — corresponding to the technical toolboxes in the
  AI Verify Toolkit.
- Process checks: we emit OSCAL Assessment Results (NIST SAR format) that
  satisfy documentary evidence requirements for AI Verify's 85 testable
  criteria.

Our key differentiator is that every measurement card is:
  1. Ed25519-signed for independent verification
  2. Registered with a SCITT transparency service (RFC 9943) for
     non-repudiability
  3. Paired (signed + unsigned) so the attestation overhead is measured

We would welcome the opportunity to participate in the Global AI Assurance
Sandbox as a technical testing provider, and to apply for accreditation under
AI TAP when applications open in Q3 2026.

Please let us know the next steps for expressing formal interest and any
documentation requirements.
