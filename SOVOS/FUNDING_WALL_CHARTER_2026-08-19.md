# FUNDING-WALL CHARTER — Council of AI (CSOAI Ltd, UK 16939677)
**Adopted 2026-08-19 · the zero-to-graded-party attestation · Vals Opening 2**

---

## The wall, stated
**Council of AI takes no money, in either direction, from any party whose AI it measures, grades, or ranks.** No lab pays for a score. No vendor pays to be on the board. No model-maker funds the instrument. The wall is published so a stranger can hold us to it.

## The funding sources (the only ones allowed)
| Source | Category | Why it's clean |
|---|---|---|
| Buyer-side subscriptions | demand-side | procurement decision support — the buyer pays, never the graded |
| Government / regulator audit contracts | demand-side | EU AI Act harmonised standards, UK/EEA public-sector adoption |
| Purchasers of the measurement instrument | demand-side | they buy the tool, not the verdict |
| The rails (free) / receipts (licensed) | the engine | "the rails are free, the receipts are the business" — receipts are demand-side |
| Grants (e.g. Sovereign AI Strategic Assets, non-commercial) | public | national measurement asset, no graded party involved |

## What is EXPLICITLY forbidden (the wall's teeth)
- ❌ Issuer-pays: no lab or model vendor pays for evaluation, ranking, or placement
- ❌ Private validation-set licensing to graded parties
- ❌ Pre-release eval runs sold to the labs being graded
- ❌ Investment from VCs who fund the graded labs (the Noah Intelligence critique)
- ❌ Any in-kind consideration from a graded party (access, compute, data) without full disclosure
- ❌ Gifts, equity, or advisory roles with a graded party

## The attestation (published quarterly)
> "In the quarter ending [date], Council of AI received £[amount] in revenue from [categories]. **£0 came from any party whose AI systems appear on the GSPC board or in our evaluations.** A signed version of this attestation accompanies every quarterly index release."

## Why this is the moat (not just a rule)
The incumbent (Vals AI) is issuer-pays — confirmed by BSKiller (16 Aug 2026, verdict TRUE) and Noah Intelligence (16 Aug 2026: "grading its investors' portfolio companies while taking their money"). **Our funding wall is the exact inverse of their attack surface.** It is the answer to the question every regulator and diligence buyer asks: *who pays the measurer?*
