## The one thing that must NEVER appear on a card (the false-rigor trap)
**Newton's third law / "equal and opposite reaction" / conservation law as PROOF of content effect.**

- There is **no conserved quantity** in model outputs. Effects amplify, cancel, or vanish;
  nothing balances a ledger. No physical theorem applies.
- Dressing that metaphor as "mathematical proof" is the exact σ-claim failure:
  a real-sounding formula over ground that can't support it. A physicist reading
  "downstream effect, proven via Newton's third law" costs the whole instrument credit.
- **Use Newton only as the intuition that motivated the chain + ledger. Never on a card as the proof.**

## Causal effect — the honesty wall
True downstream causality needs **counterfactuals** (what would have happened otherwise) —
an open research problem, not a solved theorem. We **measure** effect via proxies with
intervals; we do **not prove** it. The chain proves the measurements are intact;
the intervals measure the effect; the ledger enforces the reaction.
