## 2. Describe the processing

**What:** collection, storage, verification, and publication of AI
measurement records — signed cards, board runs, arena matches, index
cells, error statistics, and (when the human arena is live) participant
judgement responses.

**Purposes:**
1. Operate the neutral measurement body (registered purpose: measurement &
   attestation).
2. Publish verified measurement credentials (public, signed, time-anchored).
3. Recompute reproducibility (third-party verifiability — the Core of the
   fabric).
4. Human arena (future, gated): collect adult participant judgements under
   consent for calibration studies.

**Personal data types:**
- For measured orgs/users: account identity (email, name), permission records,
  card metadata (issuer identity).
- For human arena (future): participant pseudonym, judgement responses,
  consent record, optional demographic (age band only), no special-category
  data.
- No special-category data, no health, no children (unless the arena is
  specifically scoped with separate consent + parental gateway).

---

## 3. Necessity & proportionality

**Necessity:** a measurement body whose records are not attributable to a
stable issuer id cannot be audited by third parties; identity linkage (org
name to card) is minimal and necessary for the "who measured this" claim.
**Proportionality:** we collect the minimum fields needed to make a card
recompute-able (model, probeset, date), never full personal profiles.
**Alternative considered:** fully-anonymous measurement was assessed;
it fails the attestation purpose (can't be a credential without an issuer).

## 4. Risks to individuals & likelihood × impact

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Card metadata reveals an individual's proprietary prompt/model config | LOW | MEDIUM | Strip non-public model details; only card digests/axes public; full runs stay in processor storage |
| Human-arena participant data attribution | LOW (arena not live) | HIGH if it occurs | Pseudonym-by-default; consent management; no publishing of raw judgements |
| Re-identification via rare card combinations | LOW | MEDIUM | Aggregate stats with n≥30; no individual event rows for rare models |
| Processor breach | MEDIUM | MEDIUM | All processors ISO-reputed; data encrypted at rest (MinIO/Cloudflare); key custody on pod |
| Vendor key compromise | LOW | HIGH | Keys on signing node only; revocation ladder; immediate-revocation class |

## 5. Data retention & flows

- Cards + signed records: **retained permanently** (that's the point — a
  revoked card stays visible, marked REVOKED; deletion destroys institutional
  evidence).
- Raw runs / peritem JSONL: retained 24 months, then condensed to aggregate
  (axis accuracy + CIs), raw intermediate purged.
- Human arena records: consent record 36 months post study; raw responses
  pseudonymised + retained 5 years for regulatory auditability, then
  aggregate-only.
- Data flow: measurement pipeline (RunPod/Oracle) → signed card (Spine
  Ed25519) → public register (Cloudflare Pages/Workers) → verifier API.

## 6. Technical & organisational measures

1. **Spine**: Ed25519 signing keys on pod signing node only; private key
   never leaves the node (SOVOS/keys ceremony documented).
2. **Time-anchor**: every published card carries OpenTimestamps/sha256
   anchor; SCITT (RFC 9943) statement envelope.
3. **Verifier**: `csoai_verify.py` — public, stdlib, recompute-able by any
   third party.
4. **Access**: admin/storage nodes SSH-key only; RunPod/Oracle per-project
   IAM; no plaintext secrets in repos (env-injection guards).
5. **Audit**: card-event lifecycle log; error statistics; transparency report.
6. **Awareness**: staff and agents trained on processing boundaries;
   every processing assumption documented in this DPIA and reviewed monthly.

## 7. Consultation & review

- DPO: appointed when revenue or headcount crosses thresholds; until then the
  CISO seat performs the oversight function.
- ICO: consultation required only if any high-risk residual after mitigation —
  currently NOT triggered (all risks mitigated to LOW/MEDIUM).
- Next review: 6 months, or on any material change (new arena, new
  participant data class, EU expansion).
