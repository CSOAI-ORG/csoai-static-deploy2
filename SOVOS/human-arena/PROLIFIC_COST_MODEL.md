- 15-axis × human-vs-AI comparison cells (n=100 human judgements per cell)
- Paired signed/unsigned rows (same card schema as models)
- Cohen's κ ≥ 0.7 inter-rater gate → the human cell is QUOTABLE
- The most defensible ground truth a measurement body can own: humans judged
  the same items, same gold, same scorer, signed
