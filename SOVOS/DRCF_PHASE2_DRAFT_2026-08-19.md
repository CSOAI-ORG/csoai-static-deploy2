## Context
The Digital Regulation Cooperation Forum (DRCF) Phase 2 consultation covers **risk-management tools** for online safety / AI. **We ARE one** — an independent AI measurement body. This draft positions us as a supplier of deterministic, signed measurement evidence.

## THE DRAFT

**Subject: DRCF Phase 2 response — Council of AI (CSOAI Ltd, UK 16939677)**

**1. Who we are**
Council of AI is an independent AI-governance measurement body. We operate the GSPC (General Sovereign Performance Charter): a 14-slot measurement registry where **13 of 14 axes are measured** against frozen statutory provisions (417 provisions, sha256-anchored). Every result is a **verified measurement credential**: a 3KB card, Ed25519-signed, timestamp-anchored, recomputable by anyone. We are a measurement body, not a certification body, not a notified body.

**2. Why we're responding (risk-management tools)**
The GSPC instrument IS a risk-management tool: it lets platforms, regulators, and insurers measure AI system behaviour against frozen law deterministically. Key properties relevant to DRCF's goals:
- **Deterministic predicates, never LLM-judges** — verdicts are exact-label matches at temperature 0, not model opinions
- **Frozen anchors** — every score resolves to a specific statutory provision; the anchor never changes
- **Signed + anchored** — Ed25519 signatures + timestamp anchors make every result independently verifiable
- **Honest UNMEASURED** — unmeasured axes are reported as unmeasured, never hidden or fabricated

**3. Recommendations to DRCF**
1. **Standardize on signed, recomputable evaluation evidence** for AI risk assessment — an evidence format that regulators can verify without trusting the evaluator
2. **Require publication of evaluation methodology** (frozen predicates, item banks, statistical power) as the baseline for AI risk claims
3. **Consider interoperability with the EU AI Act's harmonised-standards ecosystem** (our anchors are the same 417 provisions the Act cites)
