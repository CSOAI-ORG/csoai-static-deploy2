# BSI ART/1 COMMITTEE SEAT — Application Pack (2026-08-15)

## Why this is the sleeper

**BSI ART/1** is the UK's artificial-intelligence standards committee — the
UK's mirror to **ISO/IEC SC 42** (the global AI standards committee) and the
technical body feeding **EU AI Act harmonised standards** (prEN 18286, etc.).
A seat costs **£0** and puts us **inside the room** where the standards that
everyone will have to comply with are drafted.

This is the highest-leverage £0 move in the arsenal: not just observing the
standards — helping write them, from a measurement-first (not vendor-first)
position.

## What BSI committee membership gives

1. **Drafting access** — comment on and shape AI standards before they publish
2. **Early signal** — know what's coming (Art 50 marking, GPAI obligations,
   conformity-assessment expectations) before it's public
3. **Credibility** — "Member of BSI ART/1" is a trust signal in every
   procurement, diligence, and regulatory conversation
4. **Warm-intro map** — every other committee member (large vendors, labs,
   regulators, universities) is a design-partner thread
5. **ISO/IEC SC 42 mirror** — the UK national position feeds the international
   standard; our measurement methodology can become the referenced approach
