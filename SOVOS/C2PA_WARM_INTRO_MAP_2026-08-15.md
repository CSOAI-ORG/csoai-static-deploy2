## The mechanism that makes the map work

C2PA membership gives us a **standing relationship**: co-members must respond
to each other's proposals in WG meetings. So the ask is never cold — it's "as
a fellow C2PA member, here's a signed measurement pattern that fits your
content-provenance work."

Then the **signed card does the rest**: we don't need to pitch — we show a
verifiable card of their own content's provenance handling (or a joint pilot
card) and the signature does the vouching.
