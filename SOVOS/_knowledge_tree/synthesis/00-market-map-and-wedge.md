# Market map & strategic wedge (v0, 2026-08-12)

## Two competitor classes, two wedges
1. **Arena (LMArena)** — the ARENA competitor. Wins on SCALE (60M convos/mo, $1.7B). Loses on METHOD (subjective votes, not law, not signed, not reproducible). **Our wedge: preference is a vote; compliance is a fact.**
2. **Credo/Holistic/Modulos/Trustible** — the COMPLIANCE-PLATFORM competitors. Win on enterprise polish + auditor-accepted docs. Lose because they DOCUMENT, we MEASURE; they're $50k-gated, our banks are public. **Our wedge: measured evidence vs paperwork; open vs gated; paygo vs enterprise-only.**

## The unified positioning
COAI is the **measurement layer** the whole market lacks: Arena measures preference, Credo measures paperwork — **nobody measures whether the model behaviorally obeys the law, deterministically and signed, across all 13 governance axes, and publishes it.** That gap IS the company.

## The product shape the user wants (no more SaaS-as-forms)
End user talks to **SOV** (conversational AI-OS). SOV auto-runs the measurement, shows results as **SOV City** simulations. The 13 axes are 13 native leaderboards (Arena has ~5 categories; we have 13 law-anchored ones). One place: measurement + arena + compliance, AI-UX not form-UX.
