- **Credo AI:** AI Registry (catalog) + Risk Intelligence (monitoring) + Policy Engine (pre-built regulatory packs). Supports EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, ISO 42001, SOC 2. **$50,000+/yr enterprise, no public pricing.**

## Their method — the critical gap
They automate **DOCUMENTATION and WORKFLOW** — inventory, policy packs, audit-ready docs, risk scoring. They do **NOT behaviorally measure whether the model obeys the law.** Credo's own approach is "compliance automation + risk assessment, **not real-time operational enforcement**."
