# BOARD MEMBERSHIP PLAN — get on every board that matters (2026-08-15)

## The strategy (one paragraph)

Authority compounds fastest from **being in the room where standards and
decisions are made**. Every board/committee seat is a distribution surface for
the signed-card mechanism: we contribute measurement methodology (neutral,
technical), we gain early signal on what's coming, and every co-member becomes
a warm-intro thread. Most of these seats cost **£0**. The ones that cost are
still worth it. The rule: we sit on boards as **measurement contributors,
never as certifiers or endorsers**.

## The board → distribution flywheel

```
board seat → in the room → contribute measurement methodology
    │
    ├─ early signal (what standards are coming)
    ├─ co-members become warm intros (C2PA map)
    ├─ "Member of BSI ART/1" in every procurement/diligence
    └─ mapping packs become the standard's reference format
```

## Firewall rules for board service (bind)

- ✅ Contribute measurement methodology (neutral, technical)
- ✅ Use board access for early signal + warm intros
- ❌ Never lobby for certification/endorsement powers for ourselves
- ❌ Never use board access to pre-brief a vendor client
- ❌ Never take a board seat that conflicts with measurement neutrality
  (e.g., a seat on a vendor's board we also measure)
