# Council of AI — Governance & Policy (v1, 2026-08-15)

This directory is the institution's governance-as-code: methodology, scoring
rules, appeals, conflicts policy, revocation ladder, and error statistics —
every change a signed commit. **The diff history IS the institutional
evidence** (pattern: github/site-policy, CC0).

## Sign-off

Every policy change is a signed commit (Ed25519 via the estate spine). A
policy change without a signature does not exist.

## Firewall note

These policies govern a *measurement/attestation body* — per ISO CASCO
vocabulary we issue "verified measurement credentials," never certifications,
and we are explicitly not a certification or accreditation body. The Lloyd's
and FAA adaptations are *oversight* patterns, never underwriting or
certification.
