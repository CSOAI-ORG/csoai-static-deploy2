# Conflicts Policy — the 17g-5 engineering, applied (v1, 2026-08-15)

Adapted from: SEC 17g-5 conflict engineering + Moody's/S&P analyst-compensation
firewalls + METR independence.

## Structural separations (binding)

1. **Whoever sells re-attestation subscriptions never sets thresholds.**
   Revenue and methodology are separate seats (CFO/CRO vs CISO) with no
   cross-reporting line.
2. **No analyst sits on the revenue side.** The people who run measurements
   never negotiate or benefit from a sale to a measured party.
3. **No ownership of measured entities.** Council holds no equity in any
   measured model, lab, or vendor.
4. **No referral fees tied to ratings** (locked in the Neutrality Charter and
   enforced in the board's CISO harness).

## What is regulated (not eliminated)

Conflicts are *engineered down*, not pretended away. The disclosure is
public:

- Funding sources are in the annual transparency report
- Any partnership with a measured party is disclosed on the relevant card
- A measured party may never fund the methodology that measures it
  (the METR rule; a cooperative peer-review fee is OK, sponsorship is not)

## The refusal log

Every refused conflict offer (paid placement, better-rating-for-listing,
endorse-our-model-for-a-fee) is logged with a signed timestamp. This log is
public — the refusals ARE the moat evidence.

## Enforcement

- Firewall lint (CISO seat) runs on every generated artifact
- Any detected reciprocity triggers immediate card-family revocation and a
  published incident (COAI-YYYY-NNNN)
