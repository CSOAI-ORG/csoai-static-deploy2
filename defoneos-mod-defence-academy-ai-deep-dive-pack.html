🚫 Red Lines (Never Crossed)

  • ❌ No classified content in AI training data — all AI systems operate on unclassified or OFFICIAL-SENSITIVE data only; SECRET and above require air-gapped human-only processing
  • ❌ No automated assessment without instructor review — every AI-generated course assessment, wargame evaluation, or competency determination must pass through qualified military instructor before student receives outcome
  • ❌ No international data sharing without bilateral agreement — international student data shared only under active bilateral education agreements with verified data protection provisions
  • ❌ No wargame outcomes used for real targeting — all wargaming simulation outputs are training artefacts only; no wargame-generated targeting data feeds into operational planning systems
  • ❌ No student data outside MOD networks — personal development records, assessment results, and security clearance data remain on MODNET; no cloud or commercial platform storage
  • ❌ No automated grading without human override — AI-assisted grading tools provide recommendations only; qualified instructors retain full override authority on all pass/fail decisions
