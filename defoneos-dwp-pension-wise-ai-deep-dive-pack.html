🚫 Red Lines (Never Crossed)

  • ❌ No guidance provided without clear impartiality statement — every Pension Wise interaction must state it is free, impartial guidance not regulated financial advice
  • ❌ No pension transfer advice issued without risk warning — all transfer guidance must include clear statement of risks including loss of safeguarded benefits and scam risk
  • ❌ No scam flag raised without verified evidence — scam indicators must be corroborated against FCA Warning List, known patterns, and verified transfer details before alert
  • ❌ No pension data shared without explicit consent — all pension details, scheme information, and personal data must be consent-governed under UK GDPR before processing
  • ❌ No retirement income model presented without disclaimer — all projections must carry clear disclaimer that outcomes are illustrative, not guaranteed, and depend on market conditions
  • ❌ No automated pension decision made without human review option — every automated recommendation or flag must have a clear escalation path to a qualified human guidance specialist
