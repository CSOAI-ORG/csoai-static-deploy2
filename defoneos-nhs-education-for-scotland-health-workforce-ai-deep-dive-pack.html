NHS Education for Scotland (NES) is the national education and training body for NHSScotland — a Special Health Board responsible for developing and delivering education, training, and workforce development for over 50,000 NHS staff across all professions. NES operates the Turas digital platform, Scotland's single digital gateway for health and care learning. This pack provides a sovereign AI governance framework for every NES training domain.

🚫 6 Non-Negotiable Red Lines

  • No AI-sole training assessment decisions. All ARCP outcomes, competency pass/fail determinations and fitness-to-practise recommendations remain human-made — AI provides decision support only.
  • No automated processing of trainee special-category health data. Under DPA 2018 Part 2, trainee health, disability, and occupational-health data is processed only with explicit consent and human oversight — never by AI alone.
  • No AI discrimination in recruitment or training access. All AI-informed recruitment, selection, and widening-access analytics are audited for bias under Equality Act 2010 (protected characteristics: age, disability, sex, race, religion, sexual orientation, gender reassignment, marriage, pregnancy).
  • No AI credential issuance without human ratification. Professional qualifications, CPD certificates, and training completion records are human-signed — AI tools assist verification but never issue credentials independently.
  • No trainee-identifiable data leaves UK jurisdiction. Turas data, trainee records and workforce analytics data remain UK-resident — data residency enforced by sovereign-keystore geographic binding.
  • No AI surveillance of staff wellbeing or performance. Workforce analytics assess population trends only — no individual tracking, no automated performance scoring, no psychometric inference without explicit opt-in.
