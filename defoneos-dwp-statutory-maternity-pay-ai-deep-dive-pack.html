⚡ 8 Transformation Priorities

1. Employer Digital Integration

End-to-end digital SMP pathway from pregnancy notification to HMRC recovery. Automated EPS filing and NIC compensation. Zero-paper employer submission. Payroll system API integration (Sage, Xero, ADP, Workday). Target 95% digital-first SMP claims by 2028.

2. Eligibility Cascade Automation

Real-time HMRC RTI employment verification. 26-week continuous employment auto-check from payroll data. Earnings threshold calculation from P60/RTI data. Zero manual eligibility assessment for straightforward cases. Target 80% automated approval without caseworker intervention.

3. SPL Digital Pathway

Seamless SMP-to-SPL opt-in digital process. Employer notification automation via shared portal. Shared parental pay calculator with mixed-leave support. SPL block planner and booking system. Target 35% SPL uptake by 2029 (currently ~12% of eligible couples).

4. Maternal Workplace Protection

Digital redundancy protection register linked to birth notification. KIT days tracking system. Return-to-work guarantee digital confirmation. Antenatal appointment time-off verification. Employment tribunal readiness score for employers.

5. HMRC Recovery Streamlining

Automated NIC compensation claims via RTI integration. Small employer relief instant processing. Cross-tax-year SMP reconciliation. Payroll agent portal with delegated authority. Target 48-hour HMRC recovery cycle for compliant employers.

6. Take-Up Maximisation

Close SMP awareness gaps among self-employed and agency mothers transitioning to employment. Multi-employer SMP eligibility checker. Gig economy worker pathway. Refugee and asylum seeker access programme. Target 98% SMP take-up among all eligible mothers.

7. Counter-Fraud Without Hostility

HMRC RTI employment verification as primary control. Proportionate risk-scoring focused on employer-side irregularities. Dignity-first compliance: no blanket investigations of new mothers. Target <0.1% fraud rate while maintaining 98% take-up and maternal trust.

8. Cross-Scheme Family Intelligence

Single family record across SMP/SPL/UC/CB/TFC. Auto-eligibility cascade triggered by SMP claim. No mother navigating five separate systems. Joined-up family support from pregnancy notification through early years. Single digital family identity.
