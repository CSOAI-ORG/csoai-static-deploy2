12 Entry Points

Devolution

1. Stormont & Devolved Government

Northern Ireland Assembly (90 seats), Executive formation, d'Hondt mechanism, cross-community consent. Petition of concern, ministerial code, Programme for Government, and legislative consent motions under the NI Act 1998.

Policing

2. Policing & Justice

PSNI oversight (8,500 officers), NI Policing Board, Police Ombudsman, Policing & Community Safety Partnership. Justice devolution, organised crime, paramilitary threat, and community policing across 11 council areas.

Legacy

3. Legacy & Reconciliation

Independent Commission for Reconciliation & Information Recovery (ICRIR), Troubles pension scheme, inquest reform, oral history archive. Legacy Investigation Branch, Police Legacy Unit, and victims' pension administration.

Security

4. Security & Counter-Terrorism

MI5 lead for NI, paramilitary monitoring, threat level management (SEVERE), border security. Dissident republican and loyalist group tracking, Explosive Ordnance Disposal, and protected persons programme.

Border

5. Windsor Framework & Border

Irish Sea border, customs declarations, SPS checks, green/red lane for trade. Trader Support Service, movement certification, Sanitary & Phytosanitary Inspection, and trusted trader schemes.

Identity

6. Identity & Citizenship

Irish passport holders in NI, British/Irish dual identity, Good Friday Agreement birthright provisions. Electoral identity, driving licence jurisdiction, national insurance, and social security cross-border coordination.

Economy

7. Economic Development

Belfast Region City Deal (£1bn), Derry-Londonderry growth deal, Invest NI, InterTradeIreland. Shared Island Fund, Peace Plus programme, enterprise zones, and skills pipeline.

Relations

8. British-Irish Relations

Intergovernmental Conference, North-South Ministerial Council, British-Irish Council, East-West strand. Strand One (NI internal), Strand Two (North-South), Strand Three (East-West) governance architecture.

Community

9. Community Relations

Together: Building a United Community (T:BUC) strategy, shared education, shared housing, interface barriers. Community transformation, good relations indicators, and peace walls programme.

Justice

10. Civil & Criminal Justice

NI Courts & Tribunals Service, Public Prosecution Service, Legal Aid Agency NI. Juryless Diplock legacy, coroner's inquests, and judicial appointments.

Veterans

11. Armed Forces & Veterans

NI veterans' welfare, Operation BANNER legacy, in-service NI postings, veterans' gateway. Armed Forces Covenant NI adaptation, regimental connections, and memorial care.

Accountability

12. Parliamentary Accountability

NI Affairs Committee, NI Questions, Westminster reserved matters, Statutory Instruments. Secretary of State powers, direct rule provisions, and emergency legislation.
