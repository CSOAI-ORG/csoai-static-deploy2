Insurance Evidence — signed per-agent measurement reports

The evidence-buyer product. A signed, per-agent evidence report + a queryable feed — underwriting evidence, never underwriting. Council of AI measures and signs what an agent did (deterministic, recomputable, Ed25519); it never underwrite, never advises on premium, and never names insurers on the public surface (FCA Art 33B-safe). The same body that publishes the number publishes when the number was wrong — that is what makes the evidence dependable. Measurement, not certification. Verification free forever.

Who pays (and who never does)

  • Evidence-buyers pay: insurers, enterprises, regulators, researchers — for measurement products (signed reports, the queryable feed, re-attestation freshness).
  • Never: no money either direction with anything ranked. A subject never pays for placement, a rank, or a score change. Verification is free forever.

The product

  • Signed per-agent evidence report — cells with value, n, 95% CI, source; quotable only (usable n with an interval); UNMEASURED reported never hidden.
  • Queryable feed — /api/evidence (JSON, filter by agent and axis); recomputable from the signed board chain (evidence chain).
  • Re-attestation freshness — validUntil = min(window, provision-change-event); the pass dies when the regulation changes (see passes).

Underwriting Data Feed Licence (the NOW product)

Underwriting data, never underwriting. The regulatory calendar composed as an underwriting input table: every deadline carries its statutory penalty tier (€35M/7% prohibited · €15M/3% transparency/high-risk · $1M/$3M Illinois · CRA €15M/2.5%) — the quantified exposure an MGA/carrier uses for warranty-KPI and covenant design. Re-verified quarterly; a date or tier change becomes a published correction, never a hidden edit. Buyer: MGA/carrier product & underwriting leads, actuaries. Pricing: annual data subscription + API (~$10k floor scaling to low-six-figures).
