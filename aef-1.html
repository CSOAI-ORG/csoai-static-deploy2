AEF-1 Conformance — independent third-party evaluation conditions

Council of AI adopts AEF-1: Minimum Operating Conditions for Independent Third Party AI Evaluations (AI Evaluator Forum). This page states, line by line, how we meet each condition of independence, access, and transparency. We conform to the conditions — we are not certified by the Forum or anyone else. Measurement, not certification; verification free forever.

Independence

No money either direction with anything ranked.
Council of AI takes no payment, equity, referral, or in-kind consideration from any subject it measures or ranks. Evidence-buyers (insurers, enterprises, regulators, researchers) pay for measurement products — never for placement, a rank, or a score change. This is enforced by written policy and published in the honesty register.
No certification revenue.
We sell signed measurement credentials and evidence products, never certificates or conformity marks. Nothing reads as attestation of safety, compliance sign-off, or a pass/fail stamp buyers can rely on. The word "certified" appears only as a disclaimer of what we are not.
Unsolicited measurement permitted.
We may measure an in-scope system by the published method whether the operator cooperates or not. Measurement is not conditioned on consent to buy anything. (Wording held by counsel.)

Access

Public method and frozen banks.
The instrument, probe banks, and grading rules are public and frozen (hash-anchored). Anyone can recompute a result — the deterministic core decides; an LLM only narrates, never judges. 417 frozen provisions, CELEX-keyed, open under MIT (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21991104).
Offline verification, free forever.
Every card verifies client-side with the published public key: id = sha256(canonical body), signature = Ed25519(id), prev links the chain. No login, no fee, no verification service to trust. A stranger can replay everything with one command (reproduce.sh).
Right of reply and appeals.
Every named subject gets a right of reply before publication of an independence-index row, and every signed measurement has a dispute path (dispute-measurement template). Corrections are supersession chains, never deletions — published in the honesty register.

Transparency

UNMEASURED reported, never hidden.
13 of 14 slots are measured; the gated slot is reported with its limits. We never interpolate, average over a gap, or fill a cell to look complete. An axis not measured is UNMEASURED — the gap is the finding.
Reported baselines labelled.
Human baselines and external benchmark figures are labelled as reported from published external cohorts, never our own measurement. Vendor numbers are never echoed as ours — attributed and re-measured first.
Corrections ledger public.
Every correction is published with its date and the superseding record. A number we cannot recompute is not a number we publish. The watchdog (11 checks) and live status page report our own estate's state — a red estate publishes as red.
