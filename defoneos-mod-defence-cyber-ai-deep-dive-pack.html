1. Zero-Trust Mandate

Deploy zero-trust across all MOD networks. Micro-segmentation. Continuous verification. Least privilege. Never trust, always verify.

2. AI-Powered SOC

Automate Security Operations Centre. AI threat detection. Automated response. Reduced dwell time. 24/7 autonomous monitoring.

3. Offensive Cyber Governance

Govern offensive cyber with proportionality. Legal compliance. Rules of engagement. Battle damage assessment. Civilian oversight.

4. Supply Chain Hardening

Mandatory SBOM for all defence vendors. Nth-party risk assessment. Hardware provenance verification. Continuous monitoring.

5. Cyber Workforce Pipeline
