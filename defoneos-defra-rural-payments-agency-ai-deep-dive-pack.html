🚫 Red Lines (Never Crossed)

  • ❌ No farmer payment delay beyond statutory deadline — BPS and SFI payments must be processed within the payment window with real-time status transparency for all claimants
  • ❌ No land parcel dispute without right of appeal — every parcel boundary challenge, over-declaration finding, and RLR modification must include first-tier tribunal access for the claimant
  • ❌ No cross-compliance penalty without evidence threshold — GAEC and SMR penalties require geospatial or inspection evidence meeting proportionality test before deduction from payments
  • ❌ No fraud investigation without risk-scored evidence — satellite, geospatial, and financial data must meet confidence threshold before any claimant is subjected to investigation, with full right of reply
  • ❌ No farmer excluded from ELM transition without assisted digital pathway — digital-first design must include face-to-face, phone, and paper options for digitally excluded rural claimants
  • ❌ No environmental outcome claim without verified measurement — carbon, biodiversity, and water quality outcomes linked to ELM payments require remote sensing or on-site verification against published metrics
