6 Red Lines for FCA AI Integration

  • Automated decisions leading to financial penalties or withdrawal of authorisation without full human review and due process (Financial Services and Markets Act 2000).
  • AI systems influencing financial product design or pricing without full transparency, auditability, and regulatory approval (Consumer Duty, Product Governance).
  • Processing of sensitive personal financial data by AI without explicit consent, robust anonymization, and DPA 2018 / GDPR compliance.
  • Deployment of AI in critical financial infrastructure without independent security audit, stress testing, and NCSC/Bank of England assurance.
  • Use of AI for enforcement actions against firms or individuals without human oversight, appeal mechanisms, and adherence to administrative law.
  • Any AI system that compromises the UK's financial stability, market integrity, or international financial obligations (FSMA 2000, Solvency II, Basel III).
