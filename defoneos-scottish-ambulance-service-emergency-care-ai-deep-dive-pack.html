The Scottish Ambulance Service (SAS) is the national NHS ambulance service for Scotland — a Special Health Board handling over 1.8 million calls per year through three Emergency Operations Centres (EOCs) in Inverness, Cardonald and South Queensferry. SAS operates Scotland's Air Ambulance (fixed-wing and rotary), a fleet of over 1,400 vehicles, and Scotland's only dedicated Trauma Desks. This pack provides a sovereign AI governance framework for every SAS operational domain.

📋 12 Entry Points

EOC

Emergency Operations Centres (3 EOCs)

Inverness, Cardonald, South Queensferry — triaging all NHS 999 calls and coordinating emergency response — AI governance for call prioritisation, dispatch decision support and resource allocation analytics.

AIR

Scottish Air Ambulance Service

Fixed-wing aircraft (King Air B200C) and rotary-wing (EC135/EC145 helicopters) — AI governance for flight tasking, weather-risk assessment, and inter-hospital transfer coordination across Scotland's islands and remote mainland.

PTS

Patient Transport Service (PTS)

Non-emergency transport for over 1 million patient journeys annually — AI governance for scheduling optimisation, eligibility assessment, and fleet deployment across all 14 territorial health boards.

CFR

Community First Responders & Resilience

Volunteer-led first response in remote and rural communities — AI governance for deployment coordination, training compliance, and defibrillator network management linked to Save a Life for Scotland.

TRAUMA

Trauma & Major Incident Response

Scotland's Trauma Network integration — SAS Trauma Desks coordinate pre-hospital trauma care across four major trauma centres — AI governance for trauma bypass decision support and mass-casualty triage.

RESUS

Clinical Response & Resuscitation Strategy

Save a Life for Scotland — out-of-hospital cardiac arrest (OHCA) strategy — AI governance for bystander CPR deployment analytics, defibrillator location optimisation, and resuscitation outcome measurement.

OOH

Out-of-Hours & Urgent Care Integration

NHS 24 integration, urgent care pathways, and Clinical Assessment Service partnerships — AI governance for call-stream coordination, clinical-decision support and referral routing.

TELE

Telehealth & Clinical Advice (NHS 24 Interface)

Clinical advisors, advanced practitioners and the Scottish Centre for Telehealth — AI governance for remote clinical assessment tools, advice algorithms and call-back prioritisation.

FLEET

Fleet, Estates & Vehicle Operations

1,400+ vehicle fleet across 150+ stations — AI governance for vehicle maintenance scheduling, fuel/emissions analytics, EV transition planning, and station deployment optimisation.

WELLBEING

Workforce Wellbeing & Mental Health

Staff resilience, psychological support and TRiM (Trauma Risk Management) — AI governance for wellbeing analytics, crisis-support deployment and shift-welfare optimisation without intrusive surveillance.

DATA

Data, Analytics & Clinical Audit

Clinical outcome measurement, call-cycle analytics, and AMPDS compliance auditing — AI governance for performance dashboards, trend detection and statutory reporting to Scottish Government.

RESILIENCE

Counter-Terrorism, CBRN & Major Events Resilience

Special Operations Response Teams (SORT), multi-agency CBRN response, and major event medical planning (Commonwealth Games 2026, COP) — AI governance for resource modelling, chemical-agent detection integration and inter-agency coordination.

🚫 6 Non-Negotiable Red Lines

  • No AI-sole clinical triage or dispatch decisions. All 999 call prioritisation, resource dispatch, and clinical decision support is human-overridden. AI assists — it never decides patient priority. Emergency Workers (Scotland) Act 2005 duty-of-care.
  • No AI overrides of safety-critical clinical protocols. AMPDS, JRCALC, and major trauma triage protocols remain human-executed — AI decision support is advisory and always auditable.
  • No patient-identifiable data leaves UK jurisdiction. All patient data — 999 call recordings, clinical records, dispatch logs — remains UK-resident. DPA 2018 Part 2 + UK GDPR enforced by sovereign-keystore geographic binding.
  • No AI-based mental health call routing without clinical review. Mental health crises, suicide risk, and psychological-distress calls are routed to human clinical advisors — AI tools may suggest but never decide.
  • No AI surveillance of staff performance or wellbeing. Workforce analytics assess population-level trends — no individual staff tracking, no automated performance scoring, no psychometric inference without explicit consent.
  • No AI-sole emergency vehicle routing in live operations. Dynamic deployment suggestions are human-validated — no automated vehicle redirection without EOC dispatcher confirmation.
