🚫 Red Lines (Never Crossed)

  • ❌ No EHCP decision without parental/carer consent and right of appeal under Children and Families Act 2014
  • ❌ No SEND data processing without explicit DPIA and lawful basis under UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018
  • ❌ No AI-driven placement recommendation without human professional override and tribunal access
  • ❌ No alternative provision placement without safeguarding review and local authority approval
  • ❌ No assistive technology deployment without accessibility audit and user consent
  • ❌ No SEND funding algorithm without equality impact assessment and bias testing against protected characteristics
