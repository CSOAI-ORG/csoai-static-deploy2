The Scottish Courts and Tribunals Service (SCTS) is Scotland's independent court and tribunal administration body — established under the Judiciary and Courts (Scotland) Act 2008 — responsible for providing the people, buildings and technology to support Scotland's judiciary across 39 court locations. SCTS administers the Supreme Courts (Court of Session and High Court of Justiciary), sheriff courts, justice of the peace courts, the Sheriff Appeal Court, and the Scottish Tribunals. This pack provides a sovereign AI governance framework for every SCTS administration domain.

🚫 6 Non-Negotiable Red Lines

  • No AI judicial decision-making. All judicial decisions — verdicts, sentences, bail determinations, and case-management directions — remain exclusively judicial. AI assists court administration; it never decides case outcomes. Judiciary and Courts (Scotland) Act 2008 judicial independence guarantee.
  • No AI-sole sentencing or penalty determination. Fines, community payback orders, and custodial sentences are imposed by judges and magistrates — AI may provide analytical support but never recommends or calculates specific sentences.
  • No breach of legal privilege. All court filings, solicitor-client communications, and judicial deliberations remain privileged — AI governance tools operate only on administrative and non-privileged data with human privilege-review gates.
  • No court data leaves UK jurisdiction. All case files, court records, and judicial documents remain UK-resident. DPA 2018 + UK GDPR enforced by sovereign-keystore geographic binding.
  • No AI jury-interference or juror profiling. Jury selection, vetting, and management remain human-administered — no automated juror profiling, no AI-based excusal decisions, no algorithmic jury-composition analysis.
  • No AI-sole bail or custody decisions. All decisions affecting personal liberty — bail, remand, custody-time-limit extensions — are made by the judiciary with statutory prosecution and defence representations.
