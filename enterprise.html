Enterprise — measured-current-state records & audit-support evidence

For enterprises, boards and auditors: signed, deterministic, recomputable evidence of what an AI system was measured to do — measured-current-state records, never certificates. A record states what was measured, on which instrument version, within which window, and when it expires. It never attests safety or compliance sign-off. Measurement, not certification. Verification is free forever.

Products

Measured-current-state record
A signed per-agent record for procurement and board packs: cells with value, n, 95% CI, source; quotable only; UNMEASURED reported never hidden. validUntil = min(window, provision-change-event) — the record dies when the regulation changes (see passes).
Audit-support evidence pack
Signed records + the corrections ledger, packaged for the Illinois SB 315 annual third-party audit regime (mandate 1 Jan 2028) and the CRA Art 14 reporting duty (11 Sep 2026). The same body that publishes the number publishes when the number was wrong — that is what makes the evidence dependable.
Reg-deadline compliance calendar
The date-anchored regulatory calendar as a subscription, with statutory penalty tiers — the exposure table (JSON). Re-verified quarterly; a wrong date becomes a published correction, never a hidden edit.
