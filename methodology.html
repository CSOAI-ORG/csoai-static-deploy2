Methodology

version 1.0 · dated 2026-08-18 · this is a versioned document with a changelog, not an editable wiki

1. The canon

Public measurement runs against the GSPC 14-slot canon (registry: SOVOS/canon/gspc-tree.json, ruling of 2026-08-18): 13 axes measured, 1 gated (swarm — 3-item bank, not yet statistically quotable; shown as UNMEASURED, never guessed). The frozen item banks total 818 items, hash-sealed in banks-manifest.json (Ed25519, estate-chain key) and externally witnessed (RFC 3161 + OpenTimestamps).

2. How a score is produced

  • Deterministic predicates. Each item grades by published criteria (keyword/predicate contracts in the bank), not by an LLM judge's vibes. The harness and grader configs are frozen per sitting.
  • Confidence, honestly. Per-cell scores report Wilson 95% intervals. Cells whose intervals cannot separate models are reported as unresolved — 0 of 15 GovBench dimensions resolved a winner in our own 2026-08 audit, and we say so on the dataset card.
  • Minimum cell size. An axis becomes quotable at n≥30 surviving discriminating items per cell (Wilson ±0.17). Below that: gated.

3. The signature chain

Canon: json.dumps(payload, sort_keys=True, separators=(",",":"), ensure_ascii=False) → content_id = sha256(canonical utf-8) hex → Ed25519 signature over content_id.encode(). Public keys live in did:web:csoai.org (two identities: site-release and estate-chain, per KEY-CONTINUITY.md). Verification is free forever: in-browser, via MCP, or the stdlib-only recipe in verification.schema.json.

4. Corrections and disputes

Corrections are append-only and publish in the same record as results (/honesty). Any figure can be disputed through the appeals protocol; upheld disputes become signed corrections. We do not retract — we correct, signed, in the open.
