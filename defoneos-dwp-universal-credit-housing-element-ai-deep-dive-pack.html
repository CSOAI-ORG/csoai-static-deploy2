⚡ 8 Transformation Priorities

1. Compassionate LHA Communication

Advance LHA rate change notification (minimum 3 months). No surprise housing cost reductions. Clear personalised impact statements. Area-specific rent market information. Support pathways for shortfalls.

2. Full Housing Cost Transparency

Real-time housing element calculation visibility. LHA rate comparison tools. Rent reasonableness evidence. Bedroom entitlement explanation. No hidden deductions without notice.

3. Digital-First Housing Claims

Pre-populated housing element from tenancy data. Landlord verification via HMRC integration. Rent amount self-declaration with spot-check. 48-hour housing element payment setup. Paper-free evidence submission.

4. Arrears Prevention Priority

Proactive rent shortfall alerting before arrears accrue. Automatic DHP referral at risk threshold. Landlord engagement before rent default. Emergency housing cost support pathway. Eviction prevention as measurable outcome.

5. Supported Housing Protection

Zero tolerance for losing supported housing placement due to UC delays. Priority payment processing for refuge/supported accommodation. Landlord accreditation verification before payment. Care cost separation transparency.

6. Shared Accommodation Fairness

Age-appropriate housing rate communication. Clear exemption pathway for care leavers and disabled claimants. Room rate vs one-bedroom rate transparency. No homelessness caused by SAR application.

7. Migration Safety Net

No housing cost gap during HB-to-UC transition. Transitional protection for housing element. 2-week HB run-on to UC bridge. Migration support claimant commitment relaxation.

8. Social Housing Dignity

No bedroom tax without full disability assessment. Spare room subsidy explanation with alternatives. Foster carer and armed forces exemption awareness. Housing cost modelling before move.

🚫 Red Lines (Never Crossed)

  • ❌ No LHA rate reduction notified less than 3 months in advance — tenants need planning time for housing costs
  • ❌ No housing element suspended without alternative payment arrangement — no homelessness by administrative error
  • ❌ No supported housing placement lost due to UC processing delays — priority processing mandatory
  • ❌ No rent arrears accrued without proactive DHP referral — prevention is the standard, not recovery
  • ❌ No bedroom tax applied without full disability-related needs assessment — dignity over arithmetic
  • ❌ No HB-to-UC migration without transitional housing cost protection — zero gap is the standard
