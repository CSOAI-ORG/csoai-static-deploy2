12 Entry Points

Asylum

1. Asylum Processing & Decision-Making

Asylum intake and screening interview scheduling, substantive interview coordination, and first-instance decision quality. Legacy backlog clearance programme, asylum accommodation provision (including hotels and dispersed housing), and safe third-country processing under the Illegal Migration Act 2023.

Visas

2. Visa Casework & Immigration Routes

Points-Based System casework for Skilled Worker, Student, and Family visa routes. EU Settlement Scheme (EUSS) processing, Global Talent visa, Innovator Founder route, and Youth Mobility Scheme. Processing throughput, refusal rates, and sponsor compliance integration.

Border

3. Border Force Operations

Border control operations at 138 UK ports of arrival, juxtaposed controls in France and Belgium, and eGates deployment. Freight screening, clandestine entrant detection, sanctions enforcement at the border, and carrier liability for inadequate documentation checks.

Enforcement

4. Immigration Enforcement

Illegal working operations targeting employers, removals and deportations programme, and immigration detention management. Immigration Enforcement crime investigation, intelligence-led operations, and compliance with Returns Protocol and readmission agreements.

Modern Slavery

5. Modern Slavery & NRM

National Referral Mechanism (NRM) referral processing, Reasonable and Conclusive Grounds decisions, and slavery victim identification. Adult safeguarding, child criminal exploitation (county lines), supply chain transparency reporting, and the Modern Slavery Act 2015 statutory framework.

Nationality

6. Nationality & Citizenship

Registration and naturalisation applications, citizenship ceremonies, and Good Character requirement assessment. British Nationality Act 1981 compliance, right of abode verification, and dual citizenship implications across 180+ origin nationalities.

Digital

7. Digital Immigration System

eVisa rollout replacing BRPs, digital status verification for employers and landlords, and immigration account management. View and Prove service, right-to-work checking, and the transition from physical immigration documents to fully digital status.

Data

8. Immigration Data & Analytics

Migrant journey mapping across the immigration lifecycle, casework performance metrics, and operational intelligence. Data-sharing frameworks with DWP, HMRC, NHS, and police under Immigration Act 2014 and 2016 powers. Exit checks and overstayer identification.

Detention

9. Immigration Detention & Removal

Immigration Removal Centre (IRC) oversight, detention review scheduling, and removal charter flight operations. Detention Gatekeeper scheme, bail accommodation, voluntary returns programme, and Detention Centre Rules 2001 compliance.

Compliance

10. Sponsor Licence & Compliance

Employer sponsor licensing for Skilled Worker route, education sponsor compliance for Student visa route, and compliance visit scheduling. Civil penalty regime for illegal working (up to £60,000 per worker), right-to-work checking guidance, and sponsor rating system.

Appeals

11. Immigration Appeals & Tribunals

First-tier Tribunal (Immigration and Asylum Chamber) caseload, Upper Tribunal judicial review, and appeal outcome analytics. Legal aid provision for immigration cases, appeal success rate tracking, and accelerated detained appeals process.

Accountability

12. Parliamentary Oversight

Home Affairs Select Committee evidence and reports, Immigration Act scrutiny, and ministerial accountability. Written ministerial statements on migration statistics, Statement of Intent data, HM Chief Inspector of Borders & Immigration inspection reports, and FOI disclosure.

⛔ 6 Sovereign Red Lines

  • 🚫 No individual immigration case data (asylum claims, visa applications, NRM referrals, detention records) processed outside Home Office-approved UK sovereign infrastructure — Immigration Act 1971 and Data Protection Act 2018 apply.
  • 🚫 No automated asylum decisions — every first-instance decision requires a human caseworker with Home Office Grade 7+ authorisation. AI provides decision support only, with full audit trail.
  • 🚫 No immigration detention decisions initiated by AI — all detention reviews require human Detention Gatekeeper authorisation under Immigration Act 1971 and Detention Centre Rules 2001.
  • 🚫 No personal data shared with country-of-origin governments or safe third countries without explicit Home Secretary approval and non-refoulement verification under Refugee Convention Article 33.
  • 🚫 No modern slavery NRM data processed outside secure Multi-Agency Safeguarding infrastructure — Modern Slavery Act 2015 s.45 defence and NRM confidentiality preserved at all times.
  • 🚫 No border risk profiling decisions based on protected characteristics (race, religion, nationality, sexual orientation) — Equality Act 2010 s.149 Public Sector Equality Duty compliance mandatory.
