🚫 Red Lines (Never Crossed)

  • ❌ No automated child removal or placement decisions without judicial officer review
  • ❌ No predictive policing of families without multi-agency safeguarding panel approval
  • ❌ No cross-system data sharing between family court and immigration enforcement without statutory authority
  • ❌ No algorithmic legal aid eligibility denial without human caseworker review and appeal pathway
  • ❌ No DA risk score used to deny contact without contested hearing and judicial determination
  • ❌ No adoption matching AI without adoption panel human approval and child's wishes consideration
