⛔ 6 Sovereign Red Lines

  • 🚫 No individual pupil data (UPN, SEN status, free school meal eligibility, attendance records) processed outside DfE-approved UK sovereign infrastructure — Data Protection Act 2018 and UK GDPR apply.
  • 🚫 No Ofsted inspection data or pre-publication grades used for predictive modelling or leaked before official release — Education Act 1996 s.176 and Ofsted confidentiality requirements apply.
  • 🚫 No SEND tribunal decisions or EHCP content used to train models without explicit parent/carer consent and DfE Data Sharing Framework approval — Children & Families Act 2014 s.19 applies.
  • 🚫 No teacher performance management data linked to individual identities outside school-level HR systems — Teachers' Pay & Conditions Document and Workforce Census confidentiality preserved.
  • 🚫 No safeguarding referral data processed outside secure Multi-Agency Safeguarding Hub (MASH) infrastructure — Working Together to Safeguard Children 2023 statutory guidance applies.
  • 🚫 No automated decisions on academy transfer, RSC intervention, or trust termination without Regional Schools Commissioner human authorisation — Academies Act 2010 provisions preserved.
