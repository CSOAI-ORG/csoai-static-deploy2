   - Comp: £80–120k + 0.5–1.0% equity
   - Role: customer delivery + partnership + audit ops

3. **Senior #3**: ex-OneTrust or ex-Holo, AI safety background
   - Comp: £80–120k + 0.5–1.0% equity
   - Role: DefBench + Safety SKU delivery

### Hiring decision framework (Berkus-style)
- Sound idea: does the role unblock a pricing bottleneck?
- Prototype: does the role ship the next SKU?
- Quality: ex-[credible org] + AI governance credibility?
- Strategic: brings relationships (NPL, DSIT, BSI, Big 4)?
- Product rollout: can they close 3+ deals in Year 1?
- Each attribute scores 0–5; sum × £100k = comp ceiling

### Hiring process
1. Inbound from N-site or cold outreach
2. 30-min culture call (founder)
3. 60-min technical call (bench walkthrough)
4. 90-min system design (audit deliverable walkthrough)
5. Reference checks (3)
6. Offer
7. Probation 6 months

## §11 Decision framework (the daily call)

For every decision, ask 3 questions:
1. **Does it move a priced-in asset?** (See IP_VALUATION_4METHODS)
2. **Does it survive the self-test?** (See self_test_5bench.py)
3. **Does it advance Series A/B milestones?** (See WORLD_DOMINATION_ROADMAP)

If any answer is "no", deprioritise.

If all 3 are "yes", execute immediately.

If 1 or 2 are "yes", defer to next quarterly review.
