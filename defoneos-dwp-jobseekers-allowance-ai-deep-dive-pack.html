📋 12 Entry Points

Eligibility

Eligibility — NI Contributions, Residence, Age

New Style JSA requires Class 1 NI contributions in last 2 complete tax years (26 weeks at or above LEL). Aged 18-SPA. Not in full-time education. Available for and actively seeking work. Income-based JSA being replaced by UC. Habitual residence test. Right to work in UK.

Claimant Commitment

Claimant Commitment — Work-Related Requirements

Claimant commitment sets conditions: work search, availability, work preparation. Three tiers: all work-related requirements, work-focused interview only, no requirements. Tailored to claimant circumstances — health, caring, age. Signed agreement. Breach = sanction risk. Digital claimant commitment.

Work Search

Work Search Conditions — 35 Hours/Week, Activities

35 hours/week work search. CV, applications, networking, interviews, skills. Jobcentre Plus advisor support. Universal Jobmatch. Work search review every 2 weeks. Flexible support for carers, disabled, over-50s. Work search journal. Employer contact evidence. Skills gap analysis.

Sanctions

Sanctions Regime — Low, Medium, High Level

Low-level: 1-13 weeks (failure to attend, incomplete job search). Medium-level: 4-26 weeks (failure to apply, refusing work). High-level: up to 156 weeks (voluntary resignation, misconduct). Hardship payments available. MR and appeal rights. Sanctions rate ~3% of JSA caseload. Mandatory reconsideration before appeal.

Payments

Payment Rates — Weekly JSA, UC Standard Allowance

New Style JSA: under-25 £71.70/wk, 25+ £90.50/wk (2026 rates). Paid fortnightly. 6-month duration for New Style (contribution-based). Income-based replaced by UC. UC standard allowance: under-25 £311.68/mo, 25+ £393.45/mo. UC work allowance taper. JSA and UC interaction — cannot claim both simultaneously.

UC Transition

Universal Credit Migration — Legacy to UC

Income-based JSA migrating to UC via managed migration. Transitional protection payments. Natural migration via change of circumstances. Gateway conditions. SDP transitional element. Severe disability premium. Carer element. Migration notice issued by DWP. 3-month deadline to claim UC. TP erodes over time.

Restart Scheme

Restart Scheme — Intensive Support, 12 Months

Restart Scheme for 12+ months unemployed. Intensive employment support. Provider-led — Maximus, SERCO, Reed. Placement history. Skills assessment. Work experience. Employer engagement. Referral from JCP advisor. Claimant can request alternative provider. Provider performance monitoring.

Youth

Youth Employment — Kickstart, Youth Offer, Traineeships

18-24 youth employment support. Youth Obligation — enhanced claimant commitment for 18-21. Traineeships. Apprenticeships. Sector-based work academies. Youth Hub in JCP. Youth employment advisor. DWP/DfE integration. NEET data sharing. Careers advice. Digital skills bootcamps.

Over-50s

Over-50s Employment — Midlife MOT, Experience Corps

Over-50s tailored support. Midlife MOT — health, skills, finances. Experience Corps volunteer mentoring. Flexible working expectations. Pension awareness. Age-positive employer engagement. Skills assessment for career changers. Redundancy support. Part-time work consideration. Carer-friendly claimant commitment.

Fraud

Fraud and Error — Overpayments, Detection, Recovery

JSA fraud rate ~3.2%. Overpayment detection via PAYE data cross-check. CIS/RTI real-time earnings matching. Investigation officers. Civil penalty regime. Prosecution. Voluntary disclosure. Debtor management. Recovery from UC. Capital limits. Partner income. Living together as married. Failure to disclose change of circumstances.

Data

Data Governance — Caseload, Duration, Outcomes

~1.2M JSA claimants (New Style). Claim duration distribution. Off-flow rates — employment, UC transfer, sanction. Restart Scheme outcomes. Vacancy ratio by region. Long-term unemployed (>6 months, >12 months). Conditionality group distribution. Statistical bulletins. FOI readiness. LA data sharing.

Parliamentary

Parliamentary Evidence — Scrutiny, Reform, Evidence

W&P Select Committee evidence — sanctions effectiveness, UC migration, Restart Scheme outcomes. PQ evidence: claimant numbers, duration, off-flows. Youth unemployment statistics. Over-50s employment rates. Sanctions appeal overturn rates. Cost per job outcome. Regional employment disparities. White Paper contributions.

🚫 Red Lines (Never Crossed)

  • ❌ No JSA sanction without prior warning and opportunity to comply — every sanction requires documented evidence of breach, notification, and claimant response before DM decision
  • ❌ No claimant data shared with employers without explicit consent — work search records, CV data, and personal information governed by DWP data protection, not employer access
  • ❌ No automated sanction decision without human DM sign-off — AI may flag compliance issues but cannot impose sanctions; trained DM must review and decide
  • ❌ No UC migration without transitional protection calculation — every managed migration must include TP assessment before legacy benefit termination
  • ❌ No Restart Scheme provider allocation without claimant consideration — placement history, travel, caring responsibilities, and health conditions must inform provider selection
  • ❌ No JSA payment delayed beyond statutory timeframe without claimant notification — every payment delay must be explained with reason and expected resolution date
