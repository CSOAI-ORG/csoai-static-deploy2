## OPERATIVE CLAUSES

### 1. DEFINITIONS

**"Employment IP"** means all intellectual property rights created, developed, conceived, or reduced to practice by the Employee, alone or jointly with others, during the Employment, that:
- (a) Relate to the Business or its actual or anticipated research, development, or products;
- (b) Result from work performed by the Employee for the Employer; or
- (c) Use the Employer's equipment, supplies, facilities, or trade secrets.

**"Employment Period"** means the period of the Employment plus any period during which the Employee is engaged as a consultant or contractor to the Employer.

### 2. ASSIGNMENT OF EMPLOYMENT IP

The Employee HEREBY ASSIGNS with full title guarantee to the Employer ALL the Employment IP, including:
- (a) All patents, copyright, database rights, design rights, trademarks, and trade secrets;
- (b) All rights of action and remedies accrued or to accrue;
- (c) All priority rights and continuations;
- (d) All moral rights (waived to the extent assignable).

The assignment is for the entire term of such rights throughout the world.

### 3. CONSIDERATION

The Employee acknowledges that the salary and benefits provided under the Employment constitute full and adequate consideration for the assignment of Employment IP.

### 4. FURTHER ASSURANCE

The Employee shall:
- (a) Execute patent assignments, declarations, and other documents required for patent prosecution;
- (b) Provide inventor declarations and testimony in patent proceedings;
- (c) Disclose promptly in writing to the Employer all Employment IP created during the Employment;
- (d) Cooperate with the Employer in obtaining patent, copyright, and trademark protection;
- (e) Execute OpenTimestamps proof-of-existence stamps for trade secret evidence.

### 5. PRIOR INVENTIONS (SCHEDULE A)

The Employee has disclosed in Schedule A all inventions made before the Employment that should be excluded from this Agreement. If no Schedule A is attached, the Employee represents that there are no prior inventions to be excluded.

### 6. NON-COMPETITION AND NON-SOLICITATION

For a period of **12 months** following termination of the Employment:
- (a) The Employee shall not engage in any business that competes with the Business within the United Kingdom or the European Union;
- (b) The Employee shall not solicit any customer, supplier, or employee of the Employer;
- (c) The Employee shall not use or disclose any trade secrets of the Employer.

The Employee acknowledges that these restrictions are reasonable and necessary for the protection of the legitimate business interests of the Employer.

### 7. CONFIDENTIALITY AND TRADE SECRETS

The Employee shall:
- (a) Hold all trade secrets in strict confidence during and after the Employment;
- (b) Not disclose trade secrets to any third party without prior written consent;
- (c) Use trade secrets only for the purpose of performing duties for the Employer;
- (d) Return all confidential materials upon termination;
- (e) Comply with the Employer's trade secret programme (see TRADE-SECRETS/ for tier classification).

### 8. INVENTOR REMUNERATION (UK SECTION 40)

If the Employment IP becomes the subject of a patent, the Employee acknowledges that the Employer's salary constitutes adequate consideration for the invention under section 40 of the Patents Act 1977, and the Employee shall not be entitled to any further award under section 40 unless the patent is of **outstanding benefit** to the Employer having regard to the size and nature of the Employer's undertaking (the **"Section 40 Threshold"**).

### 9. THIRD-PARTY OBLIGATIONS

The Employee represents that the Employee has no agreement with, or obligation to, any third party that would conflict with this Agreement, including any non-compete, non-disclosure, or IP assignment obligation.

### 10. DATA PROTECTION

The Employee consents to the Employer processing personal data (including sensitive personal data) for the purposes of:
- (a) Managing the Employment;
- (b) Maintaining records of intellectual property assignments;
- (c) Defending claims relating to Employment IP.

Processing shall be in accordance with the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

### 11. TERMINATION

Upon termination of the Employment:
- (a) The Employee shall immediately return all materials containing trade secrets;
- (b) The Employee shall delete all copies of confidential information from personal devices;
- (c) The Employee shall provide written confirmation of compliance;
- (d) The obligations under Clauses 6 and 7 shall continue in full force.

### 12. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
