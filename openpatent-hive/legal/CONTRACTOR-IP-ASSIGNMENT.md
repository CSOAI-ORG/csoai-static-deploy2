## OPERATIVE CLAUSES

### 1. DEFINITIONS

**"Services"** means the services described in **Schedule A** (Statement of Work), as amended from time to time by written agreement.

**"Contractor IP"** means all intellectual property rights created, developed, conceived, or reduced to practice by the Contractor, alone or jointly with others, in the course of providing the Services, including:
- (a) Source code, object code, scripts, configurations;
- (b) Algorithms, architectures, protocols;
- (c) Designs, documentation, specifications;
- (d) Training data, datasets, evaluation results;
- (e) Models, weights, embeddings, adapters;
- (f) Discoveries, improvements, enhancements;
- (g) Trade secrets, know-how, confidential information.

### 2. ASSIGNMENT OF CONTRACTOR IP

The Contractor HEREBY ASSIGNS with full title guarantee to the Principal ALL the Contractor IP, including:
- (a) All patents, copyright, database rights, design rights, trademarks, and trade secrets;
- (b) All rights of action and remedies;
- (c) All priority rights;
- (d) All moral rights (waived to the extent assignable).

The assignment is for the entire term of such rights throughout the world.

### 3. CONSIDERATION

In consideration of the assignment, the Principal shall pay the Contractor the fees set out in Schedule A. The Contractor acknowledges that the fees constitute full and adequate consideration for the assignment of all Contractor IP.

### 4. MORAL RIGHTS

The Contractor IRREVOCABLY AND UNCONDITIONALLY waives, in favour of the Principal, all moral rights conferred by Chapter IV of Part I of the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 and any analogous laws worldwide.

### 5. FURTHER ASSURANCE

The Contractor shall:
- (a) Execute all documents and do all acts necessary to perfect the Principal's title;
- (b) Provide inventor declarations and testimony for patent prosecution;
- (c) Disclose promptly in writing all Contractor IP created during the engagement;
- (d) Cooperate with the Principal in obtaining patent, copyright, and trademark protection;
- (e) Execute OpenTimestamps proof-of-existence stamps on request.

### 6. WARRANTIES

The Contractor warrants that:
- (a) The Contractor has the right and authority to enter into this Agreement;
- (b) The Contractor IP is the Contractor's original work or properly licensed to the Contractor;
- (c) The Contractor IP does not infringe any third-party rights;
- (d) The Contractor has not granted any conflicting rights to any third party;
- (e) The Contractor is not subject to any third-party IP assignment or non-compete obligations.

### 7. PRE-EXISTING IP (SCHEDULE B)

The Contractor has disclosed in Schedule B all pre-existing intellectual property that is to be licensed (not assigned) to the Principal for use in connection with the Services. The Contractor grants the Principal a **perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free, sublicensable licence** to use such pre-existing IP for the purposes of the Business.

If no Schedule B is attached, the Contractor represents there is no pre-existing IP.

### 8. CONFIDENTIALITY AND TRADE SECRETS

The Contractor shall:
- (a) Hold all trade secrets and confidential information of the Principal in strict confidence;
- (b) Use such information only for the purpose of providing the Services;
- (c) Not disclose to any third party without prior written consent;
- (d) Comply with the Principal's trade secret programme (see TRADE-SECRETS/);
- (e) Return all materials upon termination.

### 9. NON-SOLICITATION

For a period of **12 months** following termination, the Contractor shall not solicit any employee, contractor, customer, or supplier of the Principal with whom the Contractor had material contact during the engagement.

### 10. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR STATUS

The parties acknowledge that:
- (a) The Contractor is an independent contractor and not an employee;
- (b) The Contractor is responsible for all taxes, National Insurance, and benefits;
- (c) The Contractor has no authority to bind the Principal.

### 11. SUBCONTRACTING

The Contractor shall not subcontract any of the Services without the prior written consent of the Principal. The Contractor shall ensure that any approved subcontractor signs an IP assignment on terms no less protective than this Agreement.

### 12. INSURANCE

The Contractor shall maintain professional indemnity insurance of not less than £1,000,000 per claim and £2,000,000 in aggregate, with the Principal named as an additional insured.

### 13. TERM AND TERMINATION

This Agreement commences on the date first written above and continues until the Services are completed or until terminated by either party on **14 days' written notice**.

### 14. CONSEQUENCES OF TERMINATION

Upon termination:
- (a) The Contractor shall deliver all work product, materials, and confidential information;
- (b) The Principal shall pay for all Services properly performed up to the date of termination;
- (c) The assignment of Contractor IP and the confidentiality obligations shall survive.

### 15. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
