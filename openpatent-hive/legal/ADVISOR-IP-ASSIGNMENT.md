## OPERATIVE CLAUSES

### 1. DEFINITIONS

**"Advisory Services"** means the strategic, technical, commercial, or other advisory services described in **Schedule A**, including without limitation:
- (a) Attending board, strategy, or technical meetings;
- (b) Providing written reports, analyses, or recommendations;
- (c) Reviewing products, code, designs, or strategies;
- (d) Making introductions to investors, partners, customers, or talent;
- (e) Speaking on behalf of the Company at events.

**"Advisor IP"** means all intellectual property created, developed, or contributed by the Advisor, alone or jointly with others, in the course of providing the Advisory Services, including:
- (a) Ideas, strategies, recommendations, analyses;
- (b) Designs, specifications, documentation;
- (c) Code, algorithms, models, methods;
- (d) Discoveries, improvements, methodologies;
- (e) Trade secrets, know-how, confidential information.

### 2. ASSIGNMENT OF ADVISOR IP

The Advisor HEREBY ASSIGNS with full title guarantee to the Company ALL the Advisor IP, including:
- (a) All patents, copyright, database rights, design rights, trademarks, and trade secrets;
- (b) All rights of action and remedies;
- (c) All priority rights;
- (d) All moral rights (waived to the extent assignable).

The assignment is for the entire term of such rights throughout the world.

### 3. CONSIDERATION

In consideration of the assignment and the Advisory Services, the Company shall:
- (a) Pay the Advisor a fee of £_____ per [meeting / month / quarter];
- (b) Issue to the Advisor ___ options over ordinary shares of £___ each at £___ per share, vesting over ___ months;
- (c) Grant the Advisor the title of **[Senior Advisor / Technical Advisor / Strategic Advisor]** for use on the Company's website and marketing materials.

The Advisor acknowledges that the consideration constitutes full and adequate consideration for the assignment.

### 4. MORAL RIGHTS

The Advisor IRREVOCABLY AND UNCONDITIONALLY waives all moral rights to the extent assignable or waivable under any applicable law.

### 5. FURTHER ASSURANCE

The Advisor shall:
- (a) Execute all documents necessary to perfect the Company's title to Advisor IP;
- (b) Provide inventor declarations and testimony for patent prosecution;
- (c) Disclose all Advisor IP created during the engagement;
- (d) Cooperate with the Company in obtaining IP protection.

### 6. PRE-EXISTING IP (SCHEDULE B)

The Advisor has disclosed in Schedule B all pre-existing IP to be licensed to the Company. The Advisor grants the Company a **perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free, sublicensable licence** to use such pre-existing IP for the purposes of the Business.

If no Schedule B is attached, the Advisor represents there is no pre-existing IP.

### 7. CONFIDENTIALITY AND TRADE SECRETS

The Advisor shall:
- (a) Hold all trade secrets of the Company in strict confidence during and after the engagement;
- (b) Not disclose to any third party without prior written consent;
- (c) Use such information only for the purpose of providing the Advisory Services;
- (d) Comply with the Company's trade secret programme (see TRADE-SECRETS/);
- (e) Return all materials upon termination.

The confidentiality obligations survive for **5 years** following termination, and indefinitely with respect to trade secrets.

### 8. NON-SOLICITATION

For a period of **12 months** following termination, the Advisor shall not solicit any employee, contractor, customer, or supplier of the Company.

### 9. NON-COMPETE (ADVISORY CAPACITY ONLY)

For a period of **6 months** following termination, the Advisor shall not provide advisory services to any direct competitor of the Company in the sovereign AI governance space.

The Advisor acknowledges that this restriction is reasonable given the strategic sensitivity of the advisory role.

### 10. NO AUTHORITY TO BIND

The Advisor has no authority to bind the Company. The Advisor shall not represent the Company in any contractual or legal matter without prior written authorisation.

### 11. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR STATUS

The Advisor is an independent contractor and not an employee. The Advisor is responsible for all taxes and is not entitled to employee benefits.

### 12. TERM AND TERMINATION

This Agreement commences on the date first written above and continues for an initial term of **12 months**, automatically renewing for successive 12-month periods unless either party gives **30 days' written notice** of non-renewal.

The Company may terminate immediately for:
- (a) Breach of confidentiality;
- (b) Material breach of this Agreement;
- (c) Conduct bringing the Company into disrepute.

### 13. CONSEQUENCES OF TERMINATION

Upon termination:
- (a) The Advisor shall cease using the Advisor title;
- (b) The Advisor shall return all materials and confidential information;
- (c) The assignment of Advisor IP and the confidentiality obligations shall survive.

### 14. INDEMNITY

The Advisor shall indemnify the Company against all losses arising from:
- (a) Breach of this Agreement;
- (b) Negligence or wilful misconduct;
- (c) Infringement of any third-party IP by the Advisor's pre-existing IP or advice.

### 15. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
