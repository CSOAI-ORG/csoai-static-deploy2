⚡ 8 Transformation Priorities

1. Compassionate First Contact

Trauma-informed DWP contact protocols. No cold calling bereaved within 28 days. Single point of contact. No repetition of circumstances. Gentle, patient communications.

2. Funeral Poverty Elimination

Upfront funeral cost grants. No means-testing for basic dignity funeral. Public funeral provision for those with no estate. Average funeral £4,000+ — benefit must cover realistic costs.

3. Child Protection Priority

Immediate financial continuity for bereaved children. Guardian's Allowance fast-track. Education continuity fund. Emotional support auto-referral within 48 hours.

4. Single Notification, Full Access

Tell Us Once extended to all bereavement benefits. One notification triggers all entitlements. No separate applications for BSP/WPA/FEP. Cross-department data sharing.

5. Digital Inclusion for Grieving Families

Telephone-first for all bereavement claims. Paper forms maintained. Trusted person proxy. No digital-only barriers. Bereavement-trained call handlers.

6. Mental Health Integration

Auto-referral to NHS bereavement services. Cruse partnership. Workplace bereavement support. Perinatal loss specialist pathways. Child bereavement counselling routing.

7. Bereaved Parent Fast-Track

Priority processing for bereaved parents with dependent children. 5-day target for BSP claims. Emergency payment within 48 hours. No documentation delays during acute grief.

8. Cross-Department Bereavement Strategy

DWP/DHSC/MHCLG/HMRC bereavement coordination. National bereavement framework. Funeral poverty dashboard. Parliamentary bereavement champion. Annual bereavement impact report.

🚫 Red Lines (Never Crossed)

  • ❌ No bereaved person contacted within 28 days of death for routine DWP business — compassion over compliance
  • ❌ No funeral cost benefit below the median UK funeral cost — prevent funeral poverty trap
  • ❌ No bereaved child left without financial continuity — immediate benefit bridge to Guardian's Allowance
  • ❌ No bereavement claim rejected without telephone or in-person support option
  • ❌ No grief support delayed beyond 48 hours for bereaved parents with dependent children
  • ❌ No debt recovery pursued against a recently bereaved person without 90-day grace period
