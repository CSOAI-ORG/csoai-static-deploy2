## PART 1 — THE OOWM SYNTHESIS RULING

**"We're building an organic open world model — they have all the other pieces, we mergekit them as monorepos, use their training data, become agentically capable (IWM/OWM/VWM), aware of surroundings AND able to work inside a robot?"**

**YES — with one correction: you don't train the OOWM, you COMPOSE it.** The world model is not a model; it's an assembly of open organs with a sovereign nervous system (ours). Verified inventory:

| Organ | Open source | License | SOVOS slot |
|---|---|---|---|
| **IWM — inner world model** | sovos-world (absorbed: OWEMBrain, Stigmergy, GNN, RAG, sub-spaces) | ours | self-state, memory, clan dynamics |
| **OWM — outer world model** | **NVIDIA Cosmos 3** (released May 31 2026, full framework + Curator + Evaluator [^2300^]) + **Meta V-JEPA 2** (open-sourced, robot planning [^2305^]) | OpenMDW-1.1 — **commercial use OK with attribution**, gated HF download [^2228^][^2300^] | physics prediction, dreaming (drum.rs connector) |
| **VWM — visual world model** | Depth Anything 3, Cosmos-Reason (video understanding) | open / OpenMDW | perception, Glass OS depth |
| **Agentic body** | GR00T N1.7, π0, OpenVLA, Octo | Apache / commercially licensable [^2276^] | policy heads, embodiment |
| **Training data** | DROID + BridgeData V2 (**CC-BY 4.0** [^2272^]); OXE **per-component only** [^2273^] | gated | fleet learning corpus |
| **Synthetic data unblock** | Cosmos generates physics-accurate training video (trained on 20M hours [^2302^]) — **you don't need their raw data; you generate your own** | OpenMDW | the corpus factory |

**The correction to "use all their training data":** licenses gate raw corpora (OXE = 60+ upstream licenses [^2273^]; Cosmos's own 20M-hour training set is not public). **But Cosmos inverts the problem** — it *generates* physics-accurate synthetic data under a commercial license [^2302^]. The OOWM feeds itself. License gate stays on the intake (Part X harvest pipeline).

**"Aware of surroundings AND works inside a robot":** Cosmos + V-JEPA 2 give world-awareness; GR00T's per-embodiment heads give the body; Unitree SDKs give the hardware; and SOVOS adds what none of them ship — **the governed, signed, measurable wrapper** (Article 0 gates, σ, SIGIL, ChainResult evidence). One line for the deck: *"The world models are open. The trust layer isn't. That's us."*

**Bonus signal:** Cosmos adopters at launch included **1X, Agility, Figure, Fourier, XPENG, Uber, Waabi** [^2301^] — the whole humanoid field drinks from the open stack. None of them wrap it in governance.
