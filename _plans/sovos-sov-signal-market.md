## 4. THE MATHEMATICS — LAWS THAT GOVERN AN INDEX (mined for P2)

### 4.1 Merton/KMV distance-to-default → THE central isomorphism ✅
Moody's KMV, built on Merton (1974), prices corporate credit risk as **distance-to-default**: how many standard deviations the firm's asset value sits above the default boundary, mapped *empirically* to an Expected Default Frequency from a database of 250,000+ company-years [Bank of England WP; ScienceDirect; MetricGate].
**The isomorphism:** *Fisher-Rao distance from the permitted manifold* ≡ *distance-to-default*. SOV SIGNAL is the distance; the EDF-equivalent is an empirically calibrated failure-frequency curve built from our append-only ChainResult logs. **Insurers already trust this math — Moody's built a business on it. P2 should say so explicitly: we are not asking the market to learn new mathematics, we are giving it its own mathematics pointed at a new asset class.** The bank-modification lesson (NBER w25807) applies too: distance shrinks nonlinearly when volatility rises — i.e., **trust must be re-measured under drift**, which is exactly our σ.

### 4.2 The VIX lessons — how an index becomes a standard ✅
Cboe's VIX history [Cboe White Paper; Investopedia]:
1. **Publish a methodology white paper.** The VIX white paper is the genre P2 must imitate — formula, worked example, step-by-step calculation.
2. **Replicability turns a number into a standard.** The 2003 redesign "transformed the VIX from an abstract concept into a practical standard for trading and hedging" because it shipped with a replication script. **Our replication script already exists — the four-call chain. P2 should ship with code.**
3. **Model-free beats model-dependent.** VIX stopped depending on Black-Scholes assumptions and aggregated observable prices. Analog: SOV SIGNAL should be computed from *observable chain verdicts*, not from our own model of models.
4. **Name the gauge.** "Fear gauge" made VIX famous. SOV SIGNAL = the **trust gauge**.

### 4.3 Index number theory — how the 12+1 axes compose ✅

### 4.5 Basel/Vasicek single-risk-factor — the systemic-correlation sub-index ✅

### 4.6 Glicko-2 — every rating carries its own σ ✅
