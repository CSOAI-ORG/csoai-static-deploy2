4. **Graphons** — ANALOGY, explicitly do-not-build at current scale. (D6)

Zero empirical risk inherited. (D1)
8. **Coherence as monad component** — ✗ (Zwart–Marsden no-go without exhibited distributive law). Provenance+Lamport is a monad; coherence isn't. (D7)
9. **R₀ is value-neutral** — good and bad signals with equal deposit rates have equal R_bus. Quality enters only via governance coverage. The epidemic math measures spread, not truth. (D6)
10. **Lyapunov certificates certify the model, never the LLM semantics.** No global stability guarantee is possible for an LLM-society plant — the certificate is per-regime, and that limitation ships ON the artifact. (D2)
