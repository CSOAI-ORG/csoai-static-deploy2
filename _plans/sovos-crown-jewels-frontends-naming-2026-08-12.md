**Steal:**
- **The ticker** — but ours carries *evidence*, not awards: "615/619 tests PASS · d = 4.2053σ · n=237 governance axis, Wilson 95% [0.451, 0.578]".
- **The pipeline diagram** — Unmanaged → Governed is exactly our intake→arena→gates→chain→OSCAL harness. One diagram, already true.
- **LIVE stat strip** — only after Part AW truth-state repair; our strip must source from a real endpoint or say UNMEASURED.
**Don't steal:** "created the category" chest-beating without a citation. Their claim survives because Gartner backs it; ours must be backed by the ledger or not said.

### 3.2 Holistic AI (holisticai.com) — the logo-wall machine
**What the page does:** verb triad IDENTIFY · PROTECT · ENFORCE; interactive circular platform diagram; a 16-logo customer wall (Unilever, eBay, Siemens, GSK, AON, GE HealthCare); "#1 for AI Risk and Compliance Use Case" Gartner banner with report download.
**Steal:**
- **The verb triad.** Ours is already written into the doctrine: **MEASURE · PROVE · GOVERN** (or DECLARE · FIX · RE-MEASURE). Three verbs, everywhere, identical across Council and MEOK-adjacent surfaces.
- **The wall** — we can't fake customer logos. Our equivalent: an **evidence wall** — model cards, OSCAL docs, HF datasets [^2422^], the benchmarks table [^2410^], each tile linking to a real artifact. Borrowed trust replaced by verifiable trust. That IS the brand.
**Don't steal:** analyst-ranking banners until one exists.

### 3.3 Saidot (saidot.ai) — the quiet professional
