🚫 Red Lines (Never Crossed)

  • ❌ No abstraction licence issued without environmental flow assessment — every abstraction must demonstrate minimum river flow and ecological requirements are protected
  • ❌ No water quality incident unreported to DWI — every breach of drinking water standards must trigger immediate DWI notification and investigation
  • ❌ No flood defence maintenance deferred without risk assessment — every maintenance backlog must be risk-assessed and reported to Environment Agency
  • ❌ No CSO discharge without real-time monitoring — all combined sewer overflows must be monitored and reported to EA under Environment Act 2021 obligations
  • ❌ No water company performance data concealed from Ofwat — all regulatory data must be transparently reported under price review obligations
  • ❌ No groundwater abstraction in protection zones without EA approval — Source Protection Zone abstractions require Environment Agency consent and impact assessment
