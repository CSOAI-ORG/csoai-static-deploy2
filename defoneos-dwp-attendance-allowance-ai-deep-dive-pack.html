⚡ 8 Transformation Priorities

1. Take-Up Maximisation

Identify eligible non-claimants. Simplify application process. Remove barriers for elderly. Community outreach targeting. Target 100% take-up for those with care needs.

2. Fast-Track Terminal Illness

Same-day processing for SRTI claims. Automatic GP notification. No delays for terminal prognosis. Compassionate-first approach. Carer benefit cascade.

3. Digital Inclusion

Paper forms maintained for elderly claimants. Telephone claims prioritised. Assisted digital support. Trusted person proxy access. Large-print and accessible formats.

4. Fraud Prevention Without Harassment

Evidence-based risk scoring. No blanket investigations of elderly or disabled. Proportionate compliance checks. Dignity-first surveillance protocols.

5. Seamless PIP Transition

Clear eligibility guidance. No gap in support during conversion. Best-rate auto-recommendation. Overlapping benefit prevention. Unified assessment where possible.

6. Care System Integration

NHS care pathway linking. Social services coordination. Hospital admission auto-suspension. Care home transition support. Carer's Allowance cascade notification.

7. Parliamentary Transparency

Real-time take-up dashboard. Caseload reporting. Regional disparity metrics. Poverty impact modelling. Social care integration analytics.

8. Review Compassion

No unnecessary reassessments. Deterioration-only reviews. Light-touch change notification. 12-month minimum award periods. Dignified review processes.

🚫 Red Lines (Never Crossed)

  • ❌ No elderly claimant left without care support — Attendance Allowance must be auto-assessed for all eligible pensioners
  • ❌ No terminal illness claim delayed beyond 24 hours — SRTI fast-track must be automatic and immediate
  • ❌ No reassessment of stable conditions — 12-month minimum award periods with deterioration-only reviews
  • ❌ No fraud investigation of disabled claimants without risk-scored evidence threshold and dignity protocol
  • ❌ No benefit gap during PIP-to-AA or AA-to-PIP conversion — continuous support guaranteed
  • ❌ No care needs assessment without face-to-face or telephone interview with the claimant or their representative
