# The Canon Store — Council of AI 3KB unit corpus

The atomic asset of the data-annuity engine. Every research deliverable decomposes into dense, canonical, self-contained **units** (≤~3KB) that are classed, content-hashed, Merkle-linked, and signed into the chain.

## What a unit is
See `unit.schema.json`. A unit is the smallest machine-usable finding: trainable (fine-tune/RAG/eval), composable (hash-linked into chains → datasets → benchmark suites), verifiable (signed), reviewable (a lane checks a unit in minutes).

## The two-form rule (T1-4)
Every deliverable from 2026-08-17 ships in TWO forms: the human document AND its unit decomposition. The master parts are the seed corpus.

## The compounding loop
MEASURE → SIGN → ARCHIVE → DISTILL → AGGREGATE → MONETISE. Each measurement grows the archive; the archive makes each new measurement more valuable. This is the Bloomberg/MSCI/Verisk mechanic applied to AI behaviour.

## Guards (canon, unrelaxed)
- No money in either direction with anything ranked; flat, published, identical-for-all pricing.
- Judge/scorer models never train on honey/containment data (Firewall 2).
- Units are "verified measurement data," never certifications.
- No client-confidential measurement enters the public corpus.
